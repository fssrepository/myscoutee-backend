PURPOSE
Create a planning storyboard board, not a final video, for the Founder's
self-organized six-day Egypt journey after he had already returned home from
London. He coordinated four driver-led days in southern Nile Egypt through
WhatsApp, local hotels and local people. The story is about improvised human
coordination, wonder, humor and boundaries under uncertainty — not luxury,
danger tourism or a claim that Egypt lacks systems.

STYLE AND IDENTITY
Use the approved Founder identity in the premium painterly cinematic 2.5D style.
Keep him curious, observant, budget-conscious and capable. Egyptian residents,
drivers, vendors, hotel workers, police and balloon crew are distinct adults
with dignity and agency, never anonymous exotic scenery. 9:16 mobile-first,
warm Nile light, deep desert shadow, believable scale, no generated text.

CHRONOLOGY LOCK
This trip happened after the London homecoming. Any use inside the film's
three-second world montage must visibly fold time through the cognac notebook;
never imply that Egypt preceded the return.

PANEL 1 — CAIRO AND GIZA, FIRST DAY
At the Giza Plateau, use the confirmed pyramids and camel experience without a
tourism-ad pose. A camel handler overcharges the Founder despite posted pricing
guidance. The Founder returns serious and shows one phone photograph of the
camel. A helpful site manager notices, then two stern armed tourism-security
officers seat him nearby while the handler is identified. He later rides in an
ordinary police vehicle to request part of the money back. Treat this as one
specific autobiographical dispute-resolution memory, not proof that vendors are
dishonest or Egypt is unsafe. No readable price sign, humiliation, weapon
close-up, chase, raid, triumph or comic nationality.

PANEL 2 — FOUR DAYS ORGANIZED THROUGH PEOPLE
Show a clean abstract WhatsApp-like planning plate without brand or readable
text: the Founder coordinates a driver, local hotel contacts and four southern
Nile days. A route line connects Cairo → Luxor → Edfu/Kom Ombo candidates →
Aswan → Abu Simbel. Human handoffs, keys, calls and agreed times make the journey
possible. Do not claim there was no transport anywhere; show only that this
specific itinerary depended on driver and hotel coordination.

PANEL 3 — LUXOR BETWEEN TWO WORLDS
From a modest West Bank hotel outside central Luxor, frame two directions in one
visual sentence: rugged western necropolis landscape toward the Valley of the
Kings on one side, and Luxor's eastern-bank lights reflected across the Nile on
the other. A few serious tourism-security figures remain background observers,
not villains or spectacle. The Founder remembers a tourism sector recovering
from a fearful period; present that as personal atmosphere only. Confirm before
final use: Karnak Temple, Luxor Temple, Valley of the Queens, Hatshepsut's temple
at Deir el-Bahari and the Colossi of Memnon.

PANEL 4 — BALLOON IN THE VEGETABLE GARDEN
At Luxor dawn, a hot-air balloon descends unexpectedly into a vegetable plot.
Half the passengers exit through a private family home while residents, crew and
travelers cooperate to lift or guide the basket and balloon equipment around or
over a fence for removal. Use hands, eye contact and practical teamwork; the
landing is funny and communal, not a crash, disaster, invasion or mockery of the
home. This is the preferred single Egypt frame for the main film.

PANEL 5 — SOUTHERN NILE SITES, CONFIRM EACH
Create separate small candidate plates, never one impossible panorama:
- EDFU CONFIRM: the Temple of Horus and its monumental pylon;
- KOM OMBO CONFIRM: the dual-axis temple above the Nile;
- ASWAN CONFIRM: Philae on Agilkia Island by boat, the Unfinished Obelisk, the
  High Dam, Nubian Museum or a felucca only if actually visited;
- ELEPHANTINE PROBABLE: strongest candidate for the Nubian island visible from
  the riverside hotel, with villages and Nile channels; visual confirmation is
  mandatory;
- ABU SIMBEL CONFIRMED CITY/STOP: the Great Temple of Ramesses II and Small
  Temple of Hathor/Nefertari; confirm which interiors or viewpoints were seen.

PANEL 6 — THE LAST-DAY SHARED BUDGET
On the final day the Founder does not want to exchange more Egyptian pounds. He
still wants one modest souvenir for his mother and tells the taxi driver exactly
how much money remains. The driver proposes a practical shared constraint: the
amount the Founder can respectfully bargain off the souvenir is the amount left
for the airport ride. Show one finite envelope split between a small gift and a
car, mutual calculation and restrained humor. Do not show cheating a vendor,
fake currency values, desperate poverty or a guaranteed fare.

PANEL 7 — CURRENCY UNCERTAINTY WITHOUT AN ECONOMIC CLAIM
Merchants hesitate to bargain because the local currency feels uncertain in the
Founder's memory. Express this through paused hands, exchanged looks and a price
tag deliberately kept unreadable. Do not state a devaluation date, rate or cause
until the trip date and contemporary source are confirmed.

PANEL 8 — CAIRO AIRPORT TAXI, A STORY CREATED IN SECONDS
After arrival in Cairo, a taxi driver makes a personal claim that unofficial
exchange would be better than the formal counter. The Founder declines the
expensive private ride and instead shares a vehicle with a Sudanese married
couple. He and the woman begin a playful conversation while both partners react
with visible amusement. As the taxi pulls away, the couple's suitcases, stacked
high on the roof, fall down and instantly turn flirtation into a practical
shared interruption. Hold one warm reaction tableau rather than a slapstick
crash. The woman is an autonomous participant, the husband is not humiliated,
the couple is not an ethnic joke and the driver's exchange claim is not film
fact. This is the first alternate to Panel 4 for the main-film Egypt frame; use
one or the other, never both inside the eight-image burst.

EMOTIONAL PURPOSE
The Founder is not alone because nobody exists around him. He moves through a
dense chain of brief human cooperation — driver, hotel, vendor, taxi, police,
family, balloon crew, travelers, a Sudanese couple — yet those connections are temporary and must
be rebuilt from zero at every threshold. This supports the later MyScoutee thesis:
preserve consent, context, capacity and the memory of real meetings so a human
path can continue. It does not claim MyScoutee would prevent scams, guarantee
safety or replace local judgment.

OUTPUT
One clean planning board with seven separated panels and no generated text.
The editor adds city names, chronology and CONFIRM labels in post. Generate no
motion and no invented landmark.
