# Product truth and story research

Status: research note and creative rationale. External observations are not
MyScoutee performance claims. Research was checked on 2026-08-07.

## 1. What MyScoutee is

The repository describes MyScoutee as **the platform behind meetings, not
swipes**. Its target success event is not a profile view, binary match or message;
it is a completed, mutually welcomed real-world meeting.

The built product path evidenced in the repository is broader than matching:

1. A user gives an unknown individual or pair an editable private 1–10 priority.
2. Reciprocal ratings, profile/experience signals, distance, impressions and
   context contribute to weighted affinity.
3. A graph-based planner forms balanced group candidates of people who may not
   have met; people still accept or decline.
4. Accepted groups continue through chat, invitations, event creation and joining,
   sub-events/stages, schedules, policies, resources, tickets/check-in and
   structured feedback.
5. After the first meeting, former strangers are known contacts who can be
   directly invited again; Auto Inviter can fill available places according to
   the priority plan. Priorities remain editable, so lowering one later makes a
   future shared group/event path less likely.
6. Moderation, privacy/account controls, notifications, administration,
   branding and an authenticated operator surface support the operating loop.

The film should compress this to plain human actions:

> editable priority for unknown people/pairs → reciprocal/context group of
> strangers → accept/decline → contextual chat → first managed event → known
> contacts and later invitations → evolving priority and possible community

The lifecycle is dynamic rather than a one-time match. After a real encounter,
each participant may privately raise or lower rates according to personal
intuition and experience. Those changing inputs contribute to recalculated
affinity and future group/event opportunities together with other participants'
independent inputs, reciprocity, availability, distance and context. Safe
wording is **“private experience reshapes who is likely to be proposed next,”**
not “one user scores another publicly” or “the algorithm decides whom everyone
must meet.” Every later proposal still requires independent acceptance.

Do not animate the implementation as a magical black box or giant affinity graph
that declares a perfect match. Show strangers making private choices, deciding
whether to accept a group, meeting in reality, then becoming known contacts whose
future priority and invitations can change.

### Why the path is group-first

The group format reduces the all-or-nothing pressure of a two-person date. A
shared activity, several conversations, future friendship and a later invitation
can make the event meaningful even when one possible pair has no chemistry. A
larger accepted group can also support more substantial place, transport,
supplies, cost and responsibility arrangements.

The planner and Auto Inviter target a viable, complete group. When somebody
declines or becomes unavailable, an open place can be offered through the
priority path to another person, who still accepts or declines independently.
Safe wording is **“designed to restore intended capacity”**, not “the group is
always full.” The repository does not prove fill rate, attendance or that
participants experience every event as worthwhile.

### Why the 1–10 priority matters in this story

A binary swipe collapses several different states — curiosity, weak interest,
strong interest, current capacity and context — into yes/no. The 1–10 input is
best narrated as **editable priority**, not attractiveness and not human value.

That framing connects directly to the observed attention problem:

- some users receive too little response and expand their likes;
- others receive more matches/messages than they can meaningfully process and
  become more selective or disengage;
- multiple simultaneous chats compete for finite time;
- a match can be real but still lose to work, travel, anxiety or the next
  notification;
- a photograph and short profile offer a thin basis for judging how someone
  behaves around other people.

A richer input does not prove better outcomes. It gives the system and the user
more nuance with which to allocate scarce attention and form a group context.

The speed claim must be structural rather than promotional. People do not have
infinite time, so an online dating system needs a fast filter. MyScoutee's built
path can rapidly narrow private priority, reciprocity, distance and context into
a candidate group, then ask each person to accept or decline. The filter is
fast about whether a real shared context is worth offering; it is not a fast
public verdict on human worth. The intended humanization is a shorter route from
catalogue/chat overload to freely chosen real-world context. Better outcome or
time-to-meeting claims remain unproven until measured in pilots.

The emotional claim is not that MyScoutee removes personal problems or finds
problem-free people. Housing, money, distance, health, age, fear and history
remain part of real relationships. The bounded thesis is **context before a
whole-person verdict**: repeated shared reality may let people decide whether
they can accept one another and move through difficulties together. Consent,
truthful context, boundaries and the right to leave remain essential; acceptance
must never be framed as tolerating harm.

The Founder may state a personal conviction rather than a population statistic:
**most people want to be accepted as whole human beings, not declared
flawless**. The product cannot manufacture that acceptance. It can only create a
faster, reciprocal and better-informed real-world context in which acceptance
can be tested.

## 2. What the operator model means in the film

The intended film model is a verified install-package handoff to technically
capable, accountable operators. Each can run the shared core on an independently
hosted server under its own brand and test it with local communities and cultural
differences. Those servers may cooperate through a voluntary network/virtual
enterprise with opt-out. Each operator controls local community relationships,
partners, pricing, payment account, proceeds, refunds, tax and the customer
relationship. Attributable commission from a venue/activity/travel partner
belongs to the operator who acquired that partner, not to the founder.

The two-and-a-half-minute emotional film should not teach registry protocols,
contribution-unit formulas or exit allocation. The audience only needs to
understand:

- the underlying relationship-to-meeting problem is shared and remains the
  product's validation target;
- every country and city has its own cultural, language, venue and trust boundaries;
- each operator can test the shared core under its own brand and infrastructure;
- the founder has built the core and believes in it;
- network cooperation can be more efficient than rebuilding the same solution
  alone, but this remains a hypothesis to prove;
- participation is voluntary and an opt-out deployment remains independent.

Safe film wording:

> “Local operators can test one shared core under their own brands and cultures,
> cooperate through a voluntary server network, and still keep an opt-out.”

Avoid implying current proven operator income or live affiliate conversion.
Production payment charging is disabled even though checkout, pricing,
promotion, payment-session and provider-adapter code exists.

### Localization means place and context, not translation alone

The repository supports a bounded localization claim in two connected senses:

- distance and location can contribute to discovery/affinity and event views;
- events carry a real location, time and resource path, including transport and
  place/accommodation;
- independently branded operators can adapt language, culture, venues,
  boundaries, partnerships and community practice to a city or region.

The pre-London Budapest memory of arranging a short date across the
Debrecen–Budapest journey makes the problem concrete: mutual interest still has
to fit departure, arrival, meeting place, return and a limited time window. The
film or book may use this as a localization beat. It must not claim that
MyScoutee erases geography, guarantees attendance or replaces an end-to-end
travel planner.

Country change is a strong use case for continuity of method. A newcomer can
again use the rapid group-first path, distance/location context, local events
and a locally operated cultural layer instead of rebuilding every introduction
route alone. The Founder may say that London could have offered more recurring
belonging if such a path had existed; this remains a personal counterfactual,
not evidence of a MyScoutee outcome.

Do not imply cross-operator profile roaming in the current release. Raw
profiles, chats, contacts and precise location remain deployment-local, and
Firebase/global identity migration is a later roadmap milestone. Safe wording
is **“the same locally adapted meeting method can be available after a move,”**
not “your full social history automatically follows you to another country.”

## 3. Ways people can meet — and the missing bridge

The user explicitly does not want a spoken catalogue of everything tried. This
map is for the writer/director. The film uses only a few sensory fragments.

| Route | What it genuinely offers | Why it often loses continuity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| swipe/photo-first apps | very large reachable pool, explicit availability, filtering | thin cues, abundant alternatives, unequal attention, parallel chats, mismatched intent, burnout, safety/scam filtering, weak transition to a real plan |
| longer-profile dating sites | more intent and biography | profile labour, stale accounts, choice overload, message-to-meeting friction |
| speed dating | clear romantic intent, many introductions in one evening | interview rhythm, very short evaluation window, performance pressure, participant mix and follow-up remain fragile |
| singles mixers/pub events | immediate face-to-face contact, social energy | noise, alcohol dependence, uneven ratios, unclear approach rules, one-off attendance, little structured continuation |
| dance classes | recurring shared activity, embodied communication, easy conversation topic | attendance is not consent to date; many are partnered; skill levels split the cohort; beginner novelty can disappear before repeated bonds form |
| language exchange/Meetup | accessible to newcomers, cultures mix, recurring events can work | traveler/expat turnover, large-room small talk, language confidence, cliques, inconsistent repeat attendance, no supported next step |
| sport/run/fitness clubs | repetition, shared effort and visible behavior | not dating-first, ability/access barriers, schedules and geography, approaching members can violate the community purpose |
| volunteering | common purpose, prosocial behavior, repeated cooperation | relationship formation is secondary; commitments and causes must remain authentic, not used as a dating hunting ground |
| classes, board games, arts, faith/community groups | shared focus and natural repeated contact | closed circles, capacity, cost, timing, cultural fit; a connection can stay trapped inside the activity |
| friends of friends | trust context, social accountability | limited pool, relocation resets the network, rejection can affect existing ties |
| work/university | repeated exposure and context | professional boundaries, power differences, remote/hybrid work, risk to the shared environment |
| travel/tours/hostels | shared novelty and quick openness | people leave; geography and life plans diverge immediately |
| direct approach in cafés, parks or streets | organic and no platform | ambiguous availability/consent, safety, interruption, unequal comfort approaching strangers |
| professional matchmaking | human curation and explicit intent | cost, limited pool, access and dependence on the matchmaker's judgment |

The unifying story insight is not “nothing works.” It is:

> There are many doors into a first encounter. Few systems carry reciprocal
> intent into a small, repeatable, well-coordinated real-world context.

MyScoutee is presented as one designed bridge, not as the replacement for every
door. An operator can use existing dance schools, volunteer groups, venues,
festivals, sports, travel and community events as the real-world destination.

### Recover shared context without building a reputation system

The founder's sharper observation is that photo-first screens invite an opinion
before two people share a room, task, acquaintance or story. Older introductions
often arrived with some human context: who brought someone, where they met, what
they were trying to do together. That past was not universally kinder or safer,
and a mutual acquaintance is not proof of character. The useful function to
recover is **context before verdict**, not gatekeeping or nostalgia.

The product can truthfully express this in a bounded way:

- private editable priority records current intent without publishing human
  value;
- inside/outside-network and people-in-common cues may make an invitation less
  cold;
- after a real event, the event and meeting time become grounded context;
- former strangers become known contacts who may be directly invited again;
- when someone lacks time or declines, an available place can be offered through
  the priority plan, but the next person still decides independently;
- no acquaintance publicly endorses, guarantees or certifies another person;
- no public reputation, gossip or punitive score follows a changed relationship.

The film's visual answer is therefore a continuous human lifecycle, not four
static cards: premature opinion falls away → private intent and shared context →
six autonomous strangers → introductions and first event → known contacts and
later invitations → capacity changes and relationships evolve.

## 4. London and Budapest

### Rural Hungary to Budapest — the Founder's point of departure

The Founder comes from a countryside family whose earlier generations were
small agricultural producers. This is not decorative folk ancestry. It means
practical work, seasons, finite resources, self-reliance and the expectation
that useful effort should have a visible result. Show soil on hands, a produce
crate, weathered tools and an ordinary rural station; no national costume,
horse/puszta shorthand, noble-estate nostalgia or claim that the family remained
unchanged across generations.

His route toward Budapest followed a familiar personal logic: education and the
hope of work pull a young person from the countryside toward the capital. Do not
say “everyone in Hungary moves to Budapest” or present the countryside as empty
and backward. The film shows one Founder and a wider stream of commuters,
students and jobseekers converging without quantifying a national rule.

Budapest must be visibly beautiful and culturally alive. The Danube, bridges,
Buda Castle/Parliament silhouettes, Andrássy geometry, yellow tram, old
underground rhythm, museum and concert light, cafés, markets and thermal steam
form a recognition palette — never one impossible landmark panorama. UNESCO
describes the Danube banks, Buda Castle Quarter and Andrássy Avenue as an
outstanding urban landscape and a centre that received and disseminated cultural
influences. The film therefore does not blame an ugly or lifeless city.

Inside that beauty, the Founder finished university in 2004 after studies
combining economics, programming and mathematics. A delayed dissertation meant
that 2005 was the earliest year in which he could have collected the diploma.
He still could not collect it then because one required intermediate-level
foreign-language examination was missing; the diploma was actually received
considerably later, on a date still to be confirmed. Employment did not
immediately produce stable housing, a professional
future or social belonging. His first post-study attempt to establish himself
in Budapest lasted about one month with relatives in their large house. Feeling
that he was imposing is his own account, not a claim that the relatives treated
him badly. He reports that the job did not match what the employer had led him
to expect, so the employer remains anonymous and the film makes no independent
finding of fraud. He returned to his parents, then made several one-day Budapest
interview trips whose travel cost was reimbursed by the employment centre.

In October 2005, about a year and a half after finishing university, the Founder
was hoeing in a cornfield when an Indian company finally hired him. It sent him almost
immediately to Thiruvananthapuram, also known as Trivandrum, for roughly a month
and a half with an Irish and Hungarian team. He reports that this was where he
began to learn English in practice. English was both the working bridge inside that
international assignment and the language needed to clear the outstanding
formal condition for collecting his diploma. Do not state the later examination
date unless the Founder confirms it.

Official district and Kerala tourism material locates the Kerala capital near
the southwestern edge of mainland India, across low coastal hills between the
Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats. The district includes established technology
activity as well as internationally known beaches, but neither fact identifies
the Founder's employer, campus or shoreline. His memory that he practically saw
no tourists applies only to the particular beach he visited. It must not be
expanded into a claim that Trivandrum or southern Kerala lacked tourism.

When he later came to Budapest for settled work, he lived in a workers' hostel
because access to a private room felt more dependent on
personal acceptance and less predictable than the London room-rental route he
would encounter later. Do not claim that Budapest lacked rental stock or that
Hungarians as a group refused to rent to him.

One recurring personal memory makes that instability concrete: after a late
shift he could find the shared room locked and sleep in the corridor. A later
private room proved to be an undisclosed summer substitution; when the previous
social circle wanted the place back, pressure made the arrangement untenable.
The release film must anonymize the street, owners and occupants, show no
identifiable person and make no legal accusation. Returning to the impersonal
hostel meant accepting strict shared rules, but those rules were at least more
legible than an opaque personal arrangement.

His parents eventually helped him acquire a flat. In his account this also
meant he did not need to enter the foreign-currency-mortgage path that later
trapped many Hungarian households. The avoided loan is a personal
counterfactual, not proof of what would otherwise have happened. The flat gave
him material security before London; it did not solve his feeling of being
outside surrounding social life. Over many years, including after later
returning home, he lived alone.

The fuller public story also keeps the Founder accountable for relationship
paths that did not continue. Before London, one woman met through online dating
wanted to keep getting to know him, but he became frightened by commitment and
withdrew. During later visits home from London, he used online dating in
Budapest while concealing that London was where he lived. That omission kept
distance from becoming an early label, but it also denied the other person the
real context needed to judge any continuation.

On one such period he met a former woman colleague whose sudden language of love
and wish for a child felt to him more connected to motherhood than to knowing
him. That is the Founder's interpretation of speed and incomplete context, not a
claim about her private motive. These memories prevent the product origin from
blaming women, housing or platforms for every lost possibility: fear,
withholding and premature interpretation could come from the Founder too. Their
full privacy-safe visual route is
`prompts/budapest-before-london-relationship-memory-board.txt`; they are not
additional hero-film scenes.

The opening visual contradiction is therefore exact: completed studies and
employment are real while the diploma initially remains behind a language
requirement; hostel key, locked room, temporary-room suitcase and finally a flat key
show changing material access; beautiful Budapest outside remains full of
culture and people. No crowd turns its back on him and no employer slams a
literal door. One apartment light finally holds the Founder alone. This becomes
the first form of the MyScoutee thesis: resources, places and people can all
exist while the connecting social infrastructure is absent.

### Founder work context — personal testimony, not a market statistic

The film starts in rural Hungary, moves through Budapest and only then reaches
London. The Founder reports that professional work did not become the stable
housing and social future he had hoped education would open. Through his final
pre-London employer, he worked in the United States. In his account, promised
overtime followed an exhausting workload but the overtime payment did not
arrive, so he left. The employer remains anonymous and the project makes no
independent legal finding.

The Founder reports that blood in his stool first appeared under the extreme
pressure of this assignment. He personally associated the symptom with work
pressure; the project does not state a medical diagnosis or independently prove
causation. He was still experiencing the symptom when he left for London in
2011. His Hungarian public-health-insurance coverage was no longer active, and
he received medical care in London. Keep the symptom non-graphic, the clinical
details private and the health-system statement strictly first-person.

The assignment lasted roughly four or five months, with an employer-arranged
hotel base on the outskirts of Baltimore and a company-rented car. The Founder
remembers Ocean City, Washington and Adams Morgan, Greyhound travel between
Baltimore and New York, Niagara Falls, Amish country, an amusement park and
mountain/park landscapes west of the eastern urban corridor. On the New York-to-
Baltimore return he first felt that the temporary hotel base was “home.” This is
the balancing truth: the assignment was an expansive dream and a repeated local
route, even while workload left almost no private life and the overtime dispute
became the final break. Do not turn the travel into a triumph montage or the
work dispute into the only meaning of the stay.

The Founder's Adams Morgan memory is that women smiled and sometimes initiated
contact. Treat this as his subjective experience of unusual social openness,
not as a sexualized generalization about women, nationality or the
neighbourhood. Ocean City is likely the Maryland destination and Six Flags
America is a plausible amusement-park candidate, but both exact identifications
remain `CONFIRM` until the Founder recognizes the visual reference. Do not state
or visualize visa/work-authorization details in public material without
documentary and legal review.

Six and a half months without work followed. His final Hungarian application
was a four-round process at a small company; the fourth round included
psychological testing, and no offer followed. Do not make the test or the firm a
villain. The narrative truth is the Founder's experience of repeated gates,
effort and non-selection becoming the last addition to a longer pattern.

After his parents helped him secure a flat, he moved to London in 2011 and found
programming work within two weeks despite weak English. He reports that he could
barely converse with colleagues for almost three years. This does not contradict
the Trivandrum chapter: that assignment made English practically usable but did
not make him fluent or socially effortless. He worked in Soho and commuted home
on the Victoria line to Tottenham, where he lived in a converted Victorian HMO
with twelve other adults.

His initial London living conditions felt materially better than the Budapest
workers' hostel, and ordinary room rental felt more established as a route into
the city. Keep that early improvement distinct from the later crowding and the
expensive, unmanageable London rental conditions that became his stop-loss. The
December 2015 savings threshold meant independence only in lower-cost Budapest,
where the parent-assisted flat had stood empty, never in London.
The story is not a simple “Hungary bad, London good” reversal; each city offers
real possibility and a different form of constraint over time.

All twelve flatmates were Hungarian. The house itself was therefore a Hungarian
microcosm of different economic, professional and personal departures, while
the surrounding office, streets, friendships, dates and cultural life were
visibly international. Do not “diversify” the HMO by changing nationalities; give
its residents individuality through age, body, region, work rhythm, ambition,
temperament and personal history.

The Founder remembers that a substantial share of the people around him no
longer wanted to return to Hungary — in his rough household/social-circle memory,
perhaps about one in three. This is not a measured migrant statistic and must
never appear as an on-screen percentage. Some felt that their old roots had
loosened; even inside crowded, materially constrained London life, they felt
happier or more fully among people than they had felt at home. Others still
planned their return. Show both directions: one resident keeps a Hungarian
family photograph visible, another quietly stores a return plan, and another
invests in a new local relationship. Do not call anyone rootless, failed or
disloyal. The dramatic point is that shelter, prosperity, home and belonging are
different things.

Film-safe wording:

> “I went to London for work. By day I wrote software in Soho; at night the
> Victoria line took me back to Tottenham.”

This is first-person testimony. Do not turn it into a generic claim about every
EU migrant in London or identify an employer. The visual should be specific and
ordinary: software work, commute, shared routines and recognizable house
geography rather than factory, raid or anonymous work-site imagery.

### London — work, movement and solitude

London visibly offers everything: Meetups, Eventbrite/singles events, dance and
language classes, sport and running clubs, pub quizzes, cultural groups,
volunteering through Team London, and repeat activity/community formats such as
GoodGym. But in this autobiographical chronology, the founder first goes there
to earn money. Long workdays consume the hours in which a social life would have
to form. Travel broadens his world without automatically giving him someone with
whom to share it. The image is not “London rejected him”; it is “he built a life
whose schedule left almost no room for belonging.”

London-specific frictions to show without explaining:

- long travel between boroughs turns a short date into a three-hour commitment;
- different accents, humor, conversational distance and dating expectations can
  make “the same language” feel culturally new;
- newcomers and long-time residents live on different time horizons;
- cost, work hours and the last Tube shape spontaneity;
- a huge population produces abundant first contacts but not automatic repeat
contact;
- exclusion and unequal access can hide inside a crowded city.

The London decision is materially specific. The Founder feared that London's
costs would force him to return to Hungary before he had really begun. For five
years he therefore shared a Victorian Tottenham HMO with twelve other adults:
one full bathroom, one separate WC (the founder's “one and a half bathrooms”
shorthand), and a shared bedroom. The rent saved by sharing the bedroom paid for
much of the travel that made those years expansive and allowed savings to
accumulate. By December 2015, his private diagram showed that those savings
meant financial independence at Budapest's much lower cost base. The Budapest
flat his parents had helped provide before London had stood empty throughout
those five years and changed the home calculation completely. He was never
financially independent in London: normal private London housing would erode
the Budapest position, while permanent room-sharing was no longer a viable
life. Housing became his stop-loss and a material reason to return home. Do not
imply that the savings bought another flat or made him independent in London.

Cost also removed an ordinary source of belonging. Soho lunches with colleagues
were too expensive for his budget, so he performed a different version of
himself: he pretended not to be hungry. After they left, a two-litre milk bottle
and muesli supplied the recurring private meal. Show the split between capable
programmer and concealed constraint without blaming the colleagues; the Founder
does not report that they excluded him or knew the real reason. Do not present
this as a recommended diet, literal proof that he ate nothing else, or moral
superiority through deprivation.

Show a modest, maintained but genuinely crowded house, respectful and distinct
flatmates, the one-bath/one-WC geography, shared bedroom, milk bottle and muesli.
Do not portray homelessness, starvation, filth or London residents as the enemy.
Do not show invented rent or wage figures. If a later factual cut uses cost
figures, date and source them separately.

The emotional truth is not continuous grey deprivation. The Founder arrived in
2011; the public energy of the 2012 Diamond Jubilee and Olympic summer entered
his already established London life the following year. Soho colleagues
from different countries, museums, exhibitions, nightlife and dates across
cultures made London feel unlike anywhere else. A true comic memory has an Indian
woman colleague and the Founder buying a costume animal tail in Soho for a
departing Polish man colleague. The joke is friendship, not nationality.

Low-cost travel extended that magic while exposing incompatible capacity:

- a one-pound overnight coach to Edinburgh traded comfort — including the smell
  of other passengers' socks — for adventure;
- Loch Ness and a Glasgow River Clyde powerboat opened a much larger landscape;
- at one ticket counter the Founder nearly paid for a German tourist's child
  after the parent found the price too high; the point is spontaneous generosity,
  not nationality or judgment;
- a precisely planned London–Manchester–Liverpool–North Wales–Cardiff–London
  circuit succeeded but became needlessly compressed because nobody in the
  group could take one additional day;
- a wide low-tide North Wales beach with steep hills and a village behind is
  probably Llanfairfechan, but remains unconfirmed until the Founder recognizes
  it visually.

These details should appear as two- or three-second visual/sound memories under
existing narration. The product response is not “replace unavailable friends.”
It is a consent-preserving capacity path: a person may decline, an open place may
be offered to another suitable invitee, and that person independently decides.

One shared-room memory captures the unstable private world in a single sound.
A resident who had lost his money gambling exchanged his place with an
acquaintance; the Founder realized someone new was in the room only when a
different man snored. Present the unannounced turnover as dry housing absurdity,
never as mockery of loss, debt or the resident.

The former house was in the South Tottenham area adjoining Stamford Hill, where
official local sources locate a major Charedi Jewish community. The Founder
personally felt the immediate area was safer while nearby directions felt more
volatile. That is subjective memory, not evidence that religion caused safety
or a general claim about local crime. Use ordinary, respectful street life and
avoid exoticizing visible religious residents.

The first three years retained the city's wonder. By years four and five, the
same work, rent and shared space made a temporary bargain feel permanent, while
the rental alternatives felt too expensive and unmanageable. Brexit is valid as
the period's emotional and political background around work and belonging.
Official UK analysis did not find clear overall employment harm to UK-born
workers and did not support free-movement reform as a quick fix for low wages,
so the film must not assert that migrants displaced British workers or caused
low pay.

London City Hall treats loneliness as partly structural and emphasizes the
ability to build equal, meaningful relationships across difference. The film's
line “The city wasn't empty. The path between people was” is an artistic
compression of that insight, not an official diagnosis.

### Budapest — origin, return and deliberate search

Budapest opens as home, disappears while the founder works and travels from
London, then becomes the place to which he deliberately returns to seek a
partner. Current examples include international/language-exchange Meetups and
BAIS community sport/volunteering, alongside dance, board-game, quiz, culture
and social events. Budapest is not portrayed as the problem or as the whole
market. It is where the founder finally decides to give the connection problem
his full time and attention.

The tram wire changes into a London rail line on departure, then the same line
returns as a pencil stroke when he comes home. Migration, work, travel and the
founding decision are therefore one continuous visual thought.

### Nearly forty countries — beauty and global context

The founder reports visiting nearly forty countries across Europe, Asia and the
Americas. Confirmed memories named for development include Italy, Thailand,
Egypt, India's Golden Triangle, Portugal, Norway, Spain, Russia, Angkor Wat in
Cambodia, Neuschwanstein Castle in Bavaria, the Netherlands, Belgium, Baltimore,
Washington, Niagara, Virginia/Chesapeake, Ocean City, Slovakia's High Tatras and
Schönbrunn in Austria. This list is for
reference; the voice-over names only the three regions and “almost forty
countries.”

No original photographs remain, so label the visual sequence **illustrated
memory**, not archive footage. The Egypt trip happened after the London
homecoming, so this sequence folds time through the notebook instead of claiming
strict chronology. The visible travel burst is only **three seconds**: a
deliberately surprising, fast animated chain of eight reconstructed memories,
each held roughly 8–12 frames at 24 fps and joined by matching shapes. The voice
starts before the burst and ends after it, so the edit never has to recite
locations.

Create eight respectful reconstructed plates:

1. Norwegian coast or mountain weather;
2. Angkor Wat in humid morning light, with the Founder and one negotiated
   tuk-tuk handoff kept at ordinary human scale;
3. one Agra/Taj Mahal bus memory: a brief reciprocal conversation with a young
   woman travelling with her grandfather, followed by the Founder's recognition
   that distance left no credible next step; never frame her as a prize or imply
   that Delhi, Agra and Jaipur are one place;
4. Neuschwanstein as the Bavarian castle actually visited, not a “Disney castle”
   or Disney-branded object;
5. the High Tatras in Slovakia;
6. Schönbrunn in Austria;
7. one human Egypt memory: the Luxor balloon resting in a vegetable plot while
   crew, passengers and residents cooperate around a wall;
8. an eastern-US water/city memory such as Chesapeake, Ocean City or Niagara,
   with the exact location chosen and labelled in production notes.

The montage demonstrates lived cross-cultural range and supports a globally
adaptable operator idea; it must not tokenize people or reduce cultures to flags,
costumes and monuments. Show ordinary gestures and distinct spatial rhythms.
Russia appears only if useful as a personal historic memory, without a current
political claim. The emotional point is not a travel flex: extraordinary beauty
does not automatically become shared life.

### Global route, local encounter — discovering the self

The travel principle is **global route, local encounter**. A destination becomes
personally meaningful when a local driver, passenger, host, colleague, vendor,
resident or fellow traveller opens a practical or human threshold. Moving
through unfamiliar environments also reveals a different part of the Founder:
curiosity, fear, thrift, generosity, negotiation, attraction, embarrassment or
trust. The film therefore says that he discovered each place — and himself —
through local people. It never says that monuments or collecting countries
transformed him automatically.

This is also the cleanest bridge into MyScoutee. The product can have a shared,
globally reusable core without flattening local life into one universal culture.
Local operators, communities, hosts and participants decide which places,
activities, customs, boundaries and forms of introduction make sense. In visual
language, one continuous route line crosses borders, but it changes texture and
rhythm whenever a local person touches it. The same line later becomes the
shared product core surrounded by visibly independent local paths.

### Budapest as a regional starting point — one-day local encounters

The Founder also remembers repeated one-day organized bus journeys from Budapest
to the Slovak Tatras, Austria, Croatia and Slovenia. Current route examples make
Starý Smokovec/Hrebienok and Štrbské Pleso, Vienna or Semmering, Zagreb or
Plitvice, and Bled/Vintgar or Ljubljana plausible visual candidates. They are a
recognition board, not a locked itinerary: each specific sight stays `CONFIRM`

These compact journeys fit the product philosophy particularly well. A person
does not need unlimited time or a permanent travel companion; a bounded local
event can assemble available people, transport, place and supplies, then let a
temporary group acquire real shared context. MyScoutee is not credited with the
historic trips and does not replace travel organizers. The relevant planning

### Egypt after London — a temporary human network

The six-day self-organized Egypt trip is valuable because almost every threshold
depended on a different short-lived human bridge. The Founder arranged four
driver-led southern Nile days through WhatsApp, local hotels and local people;
handled a camel overcharge at Giza with a manager and tourism-security officers;
stayed on Luxor's West Bank between the western necropolis landscape and eastern
city lights across the Nile; joined a balloon landing that required passengers,
crew and residents to cooperate through a vegetable plot and private home; and
negotiated a final finite budget between a souvenir for his mother and the taxi
to the airport.

Use this as specific memory, not a country judgment. Armed security is period
background in the Founder's recollection, never visual proof that Egypt is
inherently dangerous. The camel dispute does not characterize vendors. The
driver/taxi/hotel coordination does not prove an absence of public transport.
Currency uncertainty remains an unreadable hesitation until the trip date and
contemporary exchange-rate context are confirmed.

Likely route anchors, each requiring Founder confirmation before being labelled
as visited:

- Cairo/Giza: Giza Plateau, pyramids and Sphinx;
- Luxor east: Karnak and Luxor temples;
- Luxor west: Valley of the Kings, Valley of the Queens, Hatshepsut's temple at
  Deir el-Bahari and the Colossi of Memnon;
- between Luxor and Aswan: Edfu and Kom Ombo;
- Aswan: Philae, the Unfinished Obelisk, High Dam and Elephantine Island, the

Only the communal balloon landing is currently selected for the three-second

One additional Cairo arrival memory is a strong **first alternate** to the

as the taxi pulled away, the couple's suitcases, stacked on the roof, fell down

must remain warm and self-aware: the woman is an autonomous participant, the
couple is not a comic ethnic type, and the falling luggage is not evidence about
Egyptian transport. It demonstrates how quickly strangers can acquire a common
story, while still having no durable path to continue it.

### India in November 2016 — beauty inside a cash discontinuity

The Founder dates his five-day Golden Triangle trip to November 2016: roughly
three days on the ground and two travel days through Dubai. On 8 November the
Indian government announced that existing ₹500 and ₹1000 notes would cease to
be legal tender from 9 November; banks and ATMs then required a temporary
closure and ATM reconfiguration. This supplies exact period context, not a
timeless depiction of India.

The Founder remembers being unable to exchange money, depending on a WhatsApp-
coordinated driver for some entrance payments, long ATM queues and people being
paid to wait in those queues. The first two are autobiographical details; the
last two remain personal observation unless a scene needs separate historical
sourcing. Do not turn scarcity into spectacle or portray the driver as a wallet.
The visible route is Delhi–Agra–Jaipur, the established Golden Triangle, with
each city kept geographically distinct.

The strongest human beat is not the monument. On a bus at the Taj Mahal, the
Founder heard a pleasant voice behind him, turned and met a young woman visiting
with her grandfather. They talked briefly and reciprocally; he then decided the
distance made a continuation unrealistic. It is a clean MyScoutee problem beat:
an encounter can be real and respectful yet disappear because it has no shared
context, consented continuation or realistic capacity. The product must not be
presented as able to erase geography or guarantee romance.

### Thailand and Cambodia — paid handoffs and unsolicited kindness

This was not a brief Thailand stop. The Founder spent roughly **two and a half
to three weeks** moving through several parts of the country, including
northern, western, eastern/Gulf and southern Thailand. “I saw everything” means
wide regional range in memory, not a literal completeness claim for the film.

The memory board distinguishes atmospheres instead of presenting one generic
Thailand:

- **Bangkok/central:** dense river-and-rail movement, temple compounds, markets
  and modern city layers. Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Wat Arun and Chatuchak are
  recognition candidates until the Founder confirms which were visited.
- **North:** cooler mountain/Lanna atmosphere. Chiang Mai, Doi Suthep, Doi
  Inthanon and Tha Phae are candidate anchors; Chiang Rai, White Temple and the
  northern Golden Triangle stay separate candidates, never merged with India’s
  Golden Triangle.
- **West:** river, forest, waterfalls and historical infrastructure.
  Kanchanaburi, the River Khwae bridge, Erawan and Sai Yok are candidate anchors.
- **Pattaya/eastern Gulf:** a concentrated beach-resort and entertainment city.
  The Founder remembers it as emotionally neutral and less enjoyable than the
  rest of the trip. That is a personal response, not a verdict on the city or
  its residents. Sanctuary of Truth, Jomtien, Pattaya Beach and Ko Lan are
  recognition candidates.
- **South/Andaman:** wider sea, limestone, piers and island movement. The large
  tourist island remembered above Phi Phi is almost certainly **Phuket**.
  Official Thai tourism material identifies Phuket as Thailand's largest island
  and pairs Phuket with Ko Phi Phi; because Phi Phi is also reached from Krabi,
  Phuket remains `PROBABLE` and the exact departure pier remains `CONFIRM`.

Across the longer journey, ordinary ride offers were usually explicit
transactions. On the way to the Phi Phi departure pier, the Founder was walking
through an unfamiliar area with many dogs when a local person judged that he
might need help and offered a ride to the pier without asking for payment. Do
not label the area objectively dangerous or use the dogs as a national
shorthand; the point is one person's situational care.

Inside this wider Thailand journey, the Founder made a highly compressed
Cambodia/Angkor excursion. He remembers arriving around midnight, obtaining a
visa at the border after a small payment request, reaching Siem Reap, hiring a
tuk-tuk with a driver for local movement and the Angkor visit, and being back at
the airport around 16:00–17:00. Official Cambodian tourism information confirms
that visa on arrival was an established border process and that Siem Reap's Pub
Street is a compact nightlife, restaurant and market district. It does not
verify the extra payment, exact border, transport mode or historical schedule;
retain those as testimony and confirm them before a labelled route.

The sequence is useful because almost every threshold depends on a different
stranger, but none of those handoffs persists. It should not imply that
MyScoutee replaces visas, transport planning, local advice or personal judgment.

### Russia — bureaucracy followed by one unplanned welcome

The Founder remembers needing a tourist invitation from Budapest. A travel
agency near Oktogon/Andrássy arranged it, one office redirected him farther
along Andrássy út and the process ultimately reached the Russian embassy. With
no usable advertised appointment in his account, the visa cost roughly three
times what he expected. These are personal process memories; do not identify a
specific agency or make a current consular claim without records.

After arrival he did not know how to buy a bus ticket. A local passenger used a
monthly pass to let him through into the passenger area. This is a strong
micro-story of unsolicited help after impersonal bureaucracy. The journey itself
lasted several days and connected **Moscow and Saint Petersburg**. The arrival
city, airport and historical ticketing system remain `CONFIRM`. Never present
the helper's act as official transit policy or certify that it was permitted.

Use the two cities as contrasting truthful atmospheres rather than one “Russian”
collage. Moscow candidates are the radial scale of Red Square, Kremlin walls and
Saint Basil's silhouette. Saint Petersburg candidates are the Neva, canals,
bridges, ordered embankments, Winter Palace/Hermitage and broad horizontal light.

### What these travel stories contribute to MyScoutee

The useful common pattern is not “travel was difficult.” It is that a life can
be full of people, brief cooperation, attraction, humor and generosity while
every connection is forced to restart from zero. MyScoutee's relevant promise is
modest: with consent it can retain who met where, surface shared context, expose
realistic capacity and make a later invitation possible. It does not promise
safety, solve distance, authenticate strangers, fix currency or visas, or turn
every beautiful encounter into a relationship.

### Istanbul–Dubai — hospitality, trade and two kinds of city scale

The Founder also made a five-to-six-day flight circuit linking Istanbul and
Dubai. Istanbul should feel layered and tactile: Bosphorus movement, domes,
ferries, dense covered-market passages and tea-sized conversations. Candidate
anchors include Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Grand Bazaar, Spice Bazaar,
Galata/Bosphorus and ferry silhouettes; retain only confirmed visits. Dubai is
the sharp counter-image: Creek and dhow/souk texture on one side, vertical glass,
Sheikh Zayed Road and Burj Khalifa scale on the other. Do not reduce either city
to “old East versus new money.”

In Istanbul the Founder was invited inside for tea and a carpet presentation.
The experience moved gradually from personal welcome and storytelling into a
sales conversation. Official Turkish tourism material describes carpet buying,
haggling and tea as intertwined bazaar rituals, so the scene can truthfully hold
two realities at once: the welcome may be warm, and the invitation has a clear
commercial purpose. Show the Founder listening, touching the weave and retaining
the freedom to decline. No scam accusation, hypnotic salesman, forced purchase,
fake price, exotic caricature or implication that Turkish hospitality is merely
a tactic.

This beat serves MyScoutee because context changes interpretation. A cup of tea
can be hospitality, negotiation or both; the relationship becomes humane when
intent, boundaries and choice remain legible.

### Return, private creative work and the unfinished product path

After returning to Budapest, the Founder describes roughly three years of
withdrawn creative work. The larger self-organized journeys continued during
this period; the love of travel had begun in London but did not end with London.
Do not mention health-insurance, tax or other administrative status in Film 1.
The relevant human image is a quiet flat, an open notebook, routes leaving and
returning, and sustained work that most people around him could not yet see.
The Budapest-cost financial independence was the time condition for MyScoutee:
ordinary full-time employment would not have left the sustained attention needed
to build it. The idea has a long calendar history, but the Founder does not claim
ten continuous years of development. Independence made concentrated weeks and
longer focused intervals possible; later AI assistance accelerated execution.

Returning with foreign experience did not automatically create a professional
or social place. Research on return migration is mixed: employers may value
language and foreign experience, while some returnees also face skill mismatch,
overqualification, weakened networks or stereotypes about unrealistic
expectations and leaving again. The film may show the Founder's feeling that
people formed theories about him, but it must not assign an unproved motive to a
specific employer or claim that all returnees are excluded.

The Founder also reports age becoming a stronger early filter in work and
partner-seeking, and personally felt this less sharply abroad. Age discrimination
nevertheless exists across European labour markets and in digital dating. Film 1
therefore treats it as a screen-level verdict made before shared context, not as
a uniquely Hungarian defect. MyScoutee retains truthful age/profile context and
does not claim to abolish prejudice; its bounded difference is that adults of
different ages remain agents who can express private intent, accept or decline a
group, meet and be invited again.

The product idea dates to 2016. Later AI assistance accelerated implementation;
it did not originate the insight, validate it, create users or prove a market.
The current honest ending is: a broad core has been built, while performance,
mobile and safety hardening, real-world testing, one dense pilot, measurement,
distribution and a repeatable launch playbook still need work. The invitation is
directed to technically capable local operators, pilot communities, product
collaborators and venue/activity/travel/experience partners. This is a request to
test and shape the core locally, not a claim of existing traction.

## 5. Dating-app complaints: supported, qualified, usable

### Unequal attention, not a gender war

Pew's U.S. survey found contrasting message-volume experiences: 57% of men who
had used online dating in the previous five years said they received too few
messages, while 30% of women said they received too many. These are survey
responses from the United States, not universal platform ratios.

A 2016 experimental Tinder study using curated profiles in London and New York
found that its male profiles matched with only 0.6% of the profiles they liked,
while female profiles accumulated matches faster. In the authors' accompanying
survey, 59% of male respondents estimated that no more than 10% of their likes
became matches; 49% of women estimated that fewer than 30% of their matches
became conversations. The method used only fourteen curated research profiles
and an older platform version, so it supports a mechanism, not the viral claim
that “90% of men get no matches.”

**Film image:** split-screen phone. One side remains still despite repeated
swipes; the other accumulates so many generic notifications that individual
names blur. Both hands eventually put the phone down. Neither person is mocked.

### Chats die when attention fragments

Common mechanisms:

- several matches compete at once;
- generic opening messages carry little information;
- notification timing interrupts conversational rhythm;
- browsing for another option is easier than repairing a weak exchange;
- work and travel make scheduling a one-to-one meeting feel expensive;
- safety verification and mismatched goals impose extra filtering work;
- a chat has no shared task, deadline or event around which to organize.

Longitudinal work on dating-app burnout observed increasing emotional exhaustion
and perceived inefficacy over twelve weeks among active single users. Do not
turn that result into “apps are harmful for everyone.”

**Film image:** two promising chat bubbles begin to meet; new profile cards slide
between them until the conversation becomes a thin, blinking cursor.

### Choice overload and rejection mindset

Experimental work found that people choosing from 24 profiles were later less
satisfied and more willing to reverse their choice than those choosing from six.
Other research found that a stream of profiles can foster a rejection mindset.
These effects depend on study design and person; online dating also produces
real relationships and broadly positive experiences for many users.

**Film image:** do not show a casino. Show a calm portrait multiplying into an
unreadable wall until no face can be held in focus.

### A picture is powerful but thin

Profile photos strongly drive immediate impressions. They can carry some valid
cues, but people also select and curate them to present a desired self; pictorial
information can dominate text and invite personality inferences that exceed the
available evidence.

**Film image:** a flat portrait rotates into a dimensional person in a group,
where gestures, listening, humor, practical cooperation and boundaries become
visible. The line is not “photos lie”; it is “a person is more than the frame.”

### Safety and unwanted contact

Pew's 2023 report found that 48% of U.S. online-dating users surveyed had
experienced at least one of four unwanted behaviors it measured, and 52%
believed they had encountered a scammer. Women under 50 and LGB users reported
particularly high exposure to several unwanted behaviors.

Safety is a reason for consent, moderation, public venues, clear exit paths and
group context. It is **not** evidence that MyScoutee has proven safer. The film
may show a public venue, visible host, check-in and report icon; it may not claim
prevention.

## 6. Why a group-first option can be credible

The research base is stronger for repeated shared activity and friendship
formation than for the exact proposition “a group first date is always better.”
Therefore the product and film wording stays modest.

Useful evidence and design logic:

- friendship development takes time; one longitudinal study estimated that the
  probability of moving from acquaintance to casual friend passed 50% at about
  43 hours together in its first-year-student sample;
- social infrastructure can nurture friendship by bringing people together over
  time with a shared focus and accessible space for informal interaction;
- small-group context may reduce some social-anxiety effects compared with large
  groups, but individual preference varies;
- a shared activity provides conversation material and reveals behavior beyond a
  profile;
- a group reduces the sense that one stranger must justify an entire evening;
- attendance can still be worthwhile even if no romantic pairing forms;
- a later one-to-one meeting remains voluntary and becomes a conscious next
  step, not a default obligation.

Counterpoints that must remain visible:

- some people communicate better one-to-one;
- groups can create cliques, competition or uneven speaking time;
- a group does not remove harassment or safety risk;
- accessibility, noise, alcohol, cost and travel still matter;
- participants need clear intent and an easy way to decline or leave.

Correct line:

> “Not a compulsory group date. A lower-pressure first context, when that is the
> better way to meet.”

Incorrect line:

> “Group dates solve online dating.”

The location is the antagonist; the date is not.

## 9. Story synthesis

The story needs only eight claims:

1. “I came from the land but found no door into shared life” — rural small-
   producer roots, completed studies combining economics, programming and
   mathematics, the cornfield-to-Trivandrum professional threshold, practical
   English and Budapest's beauty precede London.
2. “I hid the price of staying” — Soho software work, concealed hunger, milk and
   muesli, the thirteen-person HMO and shared bedroom kept London possible; some
   residents felt more belonging there than at home and no longer wanted to
   return. The Founder's room savings financed journeys and eventually supported
   independence in lower-cost Budapest, where his flat stood empty, but never in
   London. Normal London housing would erode that position, so housing became
   his stop-loss.
3. “Global routes become local encounters” — across almost forty countries,
   local people opened places and helped the Founder recognize different parts
   of himself; beauty alone still did not automatically create shared life.
4. “I came home to make the freedom livable and find someone to share it with”
   — returning is an active, hopeful decision, not retreat.
5. “I showed up, but the path was incomplete” — digital and physical beginnings
   repeatedly lacked reciprocal priority, repeat context and coordination.
6. “I built a different path” — follow strangers from editable priority through
   group, chat and first event, then preserve the relationship as it strengthens,
   becomes community or weakens through later priority changes.
7. “The idea became a testable core” — the 2016 insight preceded later AI-
   accelerated implementation; code is not validation, so real communities and
   partners must now shape and test it.
8. “One shared core, many local meanings” — own-brand independent servers can
   cooperate voluntarily around the same unsolved problem, while local people,
   cultures and organizers retain authorship and opt-out; no current deployment
   or proven-efficiency claim is made.

Everything else belongs in the research, pitch deck or product demonstration,
not in the emotional hero film.
