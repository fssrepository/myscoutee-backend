## 2. Logline

A man from a countryside small-producer family follows education and work toward
beautiful Budapest. He finishes university in 2004 after studies combining
economics, programming and mathematics. A delayed dissertation makes 2005 the
earliest possible diploma year, while one missing intermediate-level language
certificate delays actual receipt considerably longer. In October 2005, an
Indian company hires him while he is hoeing corn and sends him to Trivandrum,
where a mixed Irish–Hungarian team turns English from a formal obstacle into a
working language. Professional work still leads through a workers' hostel and
unstable rooms. His parents help him secure a flat, but material shelter does not create
a shared life. An anonymous employer's promised overtime payment does not
arrive after an exhausting United States assignment; he leaves, spends six and
a half months unemployed and reaches a fourth-round small-company interview
without an offer. He moves to London in 2011 while still experiencing a
frightening, non-graphically depicted health symptom, receives care there, and
finds software work within two weeks
despite weak English, and hides his hunger and isolation in Soho. For almost
three years he can barely converse with colleagues. Thirteen adults, one
bathroom, one separate WC and a shared bedroom keep London possible; the saved
rent pays for journeys and builds savings. In December 2015, those savings mean
financial independence at Budapest's much lower cost base, where the flat his
parents helped provide has stood empty throughout the London years. They never
mean financial independence in London. Normal private London housing would
erode that position, while permanent room-sharing is no longer a viable life;
housing becomes his stop-loss. Nearly forty countries still do not make solitude belonging.
Local people open each new environment and, through them, he discovers different
parts of himself. COVID closes the earlier carefree travel chapter. He comes
home to look for a partner, tries digital and real-world routes, and realizes
that the missing piece is not another doorway to strangers but a path that can
carry strangers through mutual priority, group context, a first managed event
and later meetings as known contacts. Relationships may strengthen, become
community or weaken as their editable priorities change. Budapest-cost financial
independence gives him sustained work periods that an ordinary job would not
have left. He gives that problem his full focus. A 2016 idea becomes a broad
built core with later AI assistance, then
opens to technically capable local operators and real communities to shape and
test under their own brands and cultural contexts within a voluntary server
network.

## 3. Theme and thesis

Theme:

> Proximity is not connection. A beginning needs somewhere to go.

Product thesis:

> Use a fast but humane filter: begin with editable private priorities for
> unknown people or pairs, use reciprocal/contextual evidence to form a group
> of strangers, carry the group through chat and a managed first event, then
> preserve the resulting contacts for later invitations while private
> experience and priorities continue to evolve.

Human thesis:

> Most people want to be seen as whole humans, not classified by their first
> visible problem. Acceptance can include moving through difficulty together,
> but never requires hidden context, surrendered consent, tolerated harm or lost
> boundaries.

Operator thesis:

> A common unsolved problem justifies one shared core. Technically capable local
> operators provide independent infrastructure, brands, communities and cultural
> testing while a voluntary server network/virtual enterprise provides shared
> leverage and a real opt-out.

The founder does not stand above the network. In the final shot he becomes one
node among many, smiling because the idea has outgrown a single desk.

Global/local thesis:

> **Global route, local encounter.** A shared route can cross borders, but a
> place — and a person — becomes knowable through local people and context. The
> product mirrors that truth: one shared core, many locally authored meanings.

## 4. Narrative architecture

### Act I — From the land to the city, then seeking a life (00:00–01:53)

The Founder comes from Hungarian countryside small-producer roots, follows work
and education toward Budapest, and finishes university in 2004 after studies
combining economics, programming and mathematics. A delayed dissertation means
he could first have collected the diploma in 2005, but one required intermediate-
level language certificate delays actual receipt considerably longer. His
first post-study Budapest attempt lasts about one month in relatives' large
house. Although there is physical space, he feels
that he is imposing; when the work does not match what the employer led him to
expect, he returns to his parents. Several later one-day Budapest interview
journeys have their travel cost reimbursed by the employment centre. He
is hoeing corn when, in October 2005, an Indian company finally hires him and
sends him almost immediately to Trivandrum for
about six weeks. Working there with an Irish and Hungarian team teaches him
practical English, the language also needed to clear the remaining diploma
condition. This full threshold belongs to the book and memory board; the locked
03:00 hero film compresses it rather than inventing another chronology. He
eventually enters settled professional work. Budapest is beautiful, culturally
alive and full of people, but settled work initially means a workers' hostel:
one late return ends at a locked room and a corridor. An undisclosed temporary
private-room arrangement also collapses, so the hostel's impersonal rules feel
more predictable than opaque personal acceptance. His parents eventually help
him secure a flat without taking the foreign-currency-mortgage route, but he
still feels outside shared social life. His final pre-London employer sends him
to work in the United States; in his account, exhausting promised overtime goes
unpaid, so he leaves. Six and a half months without work follow. A final small-
company process reaches a fourth round and psychological testing but no offer.
That accumulation, not one interview alone, makes England feel like an exit. He
moves in 2011 while still experiencing blood in his stool, a symptom he
associated with the American work pressure; his Hungarian public-health-
insurance coverage is no longer active, and he receives care in London. Keep
this non-graphic and non-diagnostic. He finds software work in London within two
weeks despite weak English. The Diamond Jubilee and Olympic summer enter the
already established London story in 2012, the following year. In Soho he works among colleagues
from across the world, but for almost three years can barely converse with them.
The cost of their shared lunches makes him pretend not to be hungry. After they
leave he eats muesli with milk, hiding how afraid he is that London will become
unaffordable before he has really begun. Museums,
exhibitions, nightlife, multicultural dates and friendships make the first
three years feel inexhaustible. The Victoria line is only a brief connective
beat to Tottenham, not a commute sequence. There he makes a hard, rational
bargain: the Founder and twelve other Hungarian adults share a converted
Victorian house, one full bathroom and one separate WC, while he also shares his
bedroom. The rent saved by sharing pays for low-cost travel and allows savings
to accumulate. In December 2015, his private diagram shows that those savings
mean financial independence in lower-cost Budapest, where the parent-assisted
flat has stood empty through the five London years. He is never financially
independent in London: a normal private home there would erode the Budapest
position, while continued room-sharing is no longer a viable life. Housing
becomes his stop-loss and a material reason to return. Do not imply that the
savings bought another home or created London financial independence.

The Hungarian household is not only hardship. In the Founder's personal memory,
many residents — perhaps roughly one in three of the people around him, never an
on-screen statistic — no longer wanted to return. Some felt their earlier roots
loosening and still felt happier or more alive within London's crowded human
world. Others kept a return plan. The film holds both without calling anyone
rootless or disloyal: material comfort and belonging do not move together in a
simple line.

Across the larger journey he reaches nearly forty countries. Six to eight
illustrated memories flash by in a surprising three-second animated chain; the
beauty is real, and each place becomes personal through a local human threshold.
Different environments also reveal different parts of the Founder. A restrained
COVID threshold ends the earlier carefree-travel feeling. Because larger travel
continued after the London return, the cognac notebook visibly folds chronology;
the next shot restates the purposeful but not yet permanent homecoming rather
than pretending COVID preceded it: **“I went out to make a living. I came home
to find a partner.”** London remains a possible destination after a break. He
uses apps, attends real events, dances and goes on dates. One recurring event
community—first introduced to him by a former date and attended only
occasionally after he returns alone from London, never regularly—eases that
loneliness and preserves shared interest and friendship without becoming
romance; that is a real human outcome, not failure. Keep the community unnamed
and unbranded, and never show a founding date. The Founder also attended hoping
to find a partner. Adults arrived
as singles, but in his experience the large recurring room did not provide a
legible private path from romantic intention to a supported next meeting.
It did give him somewhere to go, eased the immediate fact of being alone and
allowed him to get to know a few people; preserve this as a real positive
outcome.
Crowd size, social caution and fear of gossip remain possible interpretations,
not established causes. Other encounters lose momentum at different
boundaries. The problem is not effort, attractiveness or lack of people, and
friendship is never treated as a consolation prize.

The first 120 seconds should feel like one unbroken thought, with hidden edits
behind the Tottenham front door, the Soho office window, Victoria line glass,
the HMO bathroom and shared-room doors, travel images, the returning front door,
shoulders, dance turns, steam and the founder's dark coat.
This is the formal echo of TSB: The Story's long moving historical shot. Do not
copy its character design, rural sets, color system, story or compositions.

### Act II — Draw a path (02:00–02:40)

The moving world stops. The camera finally faces the founder. A blank notebook
page becomes the MyScoutee loop. Unknown individual and pair suggestions receive
editable 1–10 priorities. Reciprocal choices and context form a balanced group
of exactly six strangers, three women and three men, each with a distinct
expectation; accepted members enter group chat, introduce themselves and plan a
managed first event. Four blank planning cards represent the actual resource
buckets: members, transport, accommodation/venue and supplies. Tickets, check-in
and feedback remain separate. Event authorship is DIY-first, not DIY-only: a host
may build the plan, share bounded parts with participants/providers or choose a
more fully organizer-managed event. Show the collaborative middle mode and do
not claim a finished one-click travel-agency workflow. After meeting, those
people become known contacts who can be invited again. A later priority change
makes one future shared route fade.

The product is a rapid filter only in the bounded sense that finite attention
reaches a reciprocal candidate invitation sooner. The group lowers the
all-or-nothing pressure of a two-person date and can hold several conversations,
shared activity, friendship or later invitations; the film presents this as
design logic, not measured performance. If one invited adult declines, a place
may be offered again, but the next person still chooses and a full group is not
guaranteed.

The pace becomes legible and deliberate. The audience should feel “of course —
the missing step was the path,” not “an algorithm saved him.”

The product turn also recovers a human function weakened by photo-first screens:
people used to arrive with stories, places and people in common before making a
verdict. MyScoutee may surface that shared context and make a warm introduction
less cold, but it never publishes a human rating or asks an acquaintance to
guarantee character, compatibility or safety. The same six adults must visibly
progress from separate profiles to autonomous decisions, introductions, a real
event and later recognition; do not replace this lifecycle with static cards.

### Act III — Relationship evolution and voluntary local operation (02:40–03:00)

Former strangers can be invited again and repeated events can form community;
when one relationship changes, lowering its private priority makes another
shared path less likely. The Founder then makes an accountable technical handoff.
The Budapest-cost financial independence supplies the sustained attention that
ordinary full-time work would not have left for the 2016 idea. This is not a
claim of ten continuous development years: the long calendar span contains
concentrated creative and implementation periods. The notebook origin and later
AI-assisted implementation compress into a built-but-unvalidated core. One operator verifies the package and starts an
own-brand independent server;
distinct local community/cultural tests connect around the shared core as a
voluntary network/virtual enterprise, with one credible opt-out branch. Do not
imply current deployments or proven efficiency. Film 1 ends as a complete
product invitation. It contains no hint of the later legal/tax/court story; a
future Film 2 may inherit only its exact final frame and will be reconstructed
separately from documents.

The same ending carries a bounded localization idea: distance, place, time,
transport, resources, language, culture, venues and local responsibility shape
every real event. A person changing countries can reuse the method, but current
independent operators do not automatically exchange that person's profile,
chats, contacts or private rate history.

## 5. Visual world

### Style statement

> Premium 2.5D animated short film: expressive hand-drawn characters with
> restrained facial linework, dimensional miniature/paper city sets, realistic
> cinematic lighting, tactile surfaces, shallow atmospheric depth, elegant
> camera movement, and a coherent painterly finish. Human, slightly imperfect,
> never glossy corporate 3D and never photoreal uncanny AI footage.

Why this style:

- it honors the emotional, crafted scale of the reference film without copying
  its design;
- character identity and gestures are more stable across generation tools;
- city transitions can be theatrical and impossible while still feeling true;
- UI and graph metaphors can enter the world without becoming a screen demo;
- subtle face acting remains possible without lip sync; John carries the only
  spoken track.

### Texture

- paper tooth visible only in highlights;
- wool, painted wood, fogged glass, wet pavement and notebook graphite;
- London stone and tiled Underground surfaces simplified into original shapes;
- Budapest tram metal and Danube reflections simplified, no official transport
  logos;
- no plastic skin, hyper-sharp pores, game-engine sheen or generic Pixar look.

| Budapest origin | Danube blue, tram yellow, limestone cream, concert burgundy | public beauty and culture without a personal doorway |

recolored logo.

## 6. Character bible

### FOUNDER — protagonist and narrator

The final face must be derived from reference images supplied by the creator or
from an explicitly approved stylized design. Do not invent a “more handsome”
version. Preserve recognizable proportions and humane asymmetry.

Stable costume anchors:

- navy mid-length field coat;
- charcoal crew-neck knit;
- muted rust scarf in exterior winter shots;
- dark trousers and practical brown shoes;
- worn cognac notebook cover and mechanical pencil;
- compact dark backpack during travel;
- no visible luxury branding.

Performance rules:

- observant eyes; he notices the room before acting;
- shoulders begin open, tighten slightly through failed formats, then release at
  the insight;
- frustration is a quiet exhale, not anger;
- he never glares at women, couples, hosts or phones;
- humor is self-aware: a half-smile when the coffee machine defeats a sentence;
- the insight is not a eureka grin. It is a slow refocus, small inhale, pencil
  finally touching paper;
- final confidence is calm and direct, with relaxed brow and level eye line.

Expression sheet required before motion:

1. neutral attentive;
2. hopeful half-smile;
3. polite concentration in noise;
4. momentary social uncertainty;
5. tired but composed on the night train;
6. recognition/clarity;
7. listening inside a group;
8. quiet founder confidence to camera.

### MAYA — attention-overload counterpoint

Maya is not “the desirable woman” or a reward. She visualizes the other side of
attention scarcity.

- adult Londoner/newcomer; appearance derived from an approved reference or an
  original stylized design;
- moss-green coat, cream shirt, silver stud earrings;
- phone fills with generic notifications while her face moves from curiosity to
  cognitive overload and safety-checking caution;
- she later appears as one member of a MyScoutee group, listening and choosing
  for herself;
- she is never romantically paired with the founder in the film.

### DANCE STUDENT and PARTNER

- DANCE STUDENT: warm, focused, partnered participant; teal practice top;
- PARTNER: arrives naturally with an umbrella/coat and gives a friendly
  nonverbal greeting;
- neither is antagonistic and neither owes the founder romantic availability;
- the founder's micro-expression is simply “context understood.”

### THREE BAD-DATE PARTNERS

They may be different people or one composite. Prefer three diverse adults so
the montage reads as format friction over time, not judgment of a single date.

- all show effort and basic goodwill;
- the coffee date laughs with the founder when the steam wand interrupts;
- the bar date cups an ear and gestures gently for repetition;
- the formal-restaurant date releases a small nervous breath at the same time
  as the founder;
- no one checks a phone contemptuously, spills a drink, looks grotesque or is
  rejected for appearance.

### FIRST GROUP

Exactly six adults: three women and three men, including Maya and the Founder,
cast by plausible need rather than an age quota. The working picture may lean
toward the thirties, forties and fifties but can naturally include an adult in
their twenties or sixties. Vary ethnicity, body type, clothing and temperament.
Keep every face readable and give each person a distinct visual identity, life
stage and expectation. Their shared event is a low-pressure city walk
ending at a tabletop/community venue with a quiet conversation area. They arrive
as strangers and visibly introduce themselves before planning together.

Micro-actions reveal character:

- one checks the transport route;
- one confirms the venue key or booking;
- one brings the shared supplies;
- two discover a mutual joke;
- Maya listens before adding a line;
- the founder is part of the group, not its centre.

### LOCAL OPERATOR

An adult host who knows the venue staff and checks the room before participants
arrive. Do not dress them as a platform executive. Give them practical keys,
tablet/clipboard and a warm, competent presence. In the network montage, show
several operators of different cities and communities, each with distinct local
branding around the shared core.

## 7. Location bible

### Budapest

- dawn interior near a tram line, first as departure and later as return;
- stylized yellow tram reflection, no logo or route number;
- Danube/bridge geometry seen as layered paper planes, not a tourism postcard;
- dance studio with timber floor, tall windows and old radiator;
- familiar warm practical light, early winter atmosphere.
- for the broader illustrated-memory pool, Budapest can also become the departure
  point of a bounded one-day bus group toward Founder-confirmed High Tatras,
  Austrian, Croatian or Slovenian locations. The bus is temporary social
  infrastructure, not a tourism advertisement and not a historic MyScoutee event.

### Rural Hungary and Budapest

- rural ancestry appears through working hands, soil, ordinary tools, produce
  and a small station, never national costume, horse/puszta shorthand or poverty;
- the rural route becomes a wider stream toward Budapest for education and work;
  this is one personal route, not “everyone leaves the countryside”;
- Budapest is visibly beautiful: Danube/bridge and Buda–Pest layers, yellow tram,
  Andrássy/old-underground rhythm, museum and concert light, café/market life and
  thermal steam. Use two or three motifs, never a landmark checklist;
- completed university studies are shown through three interlocking visual
  languages — economics, code logic and mathematics — with no readable diploma;
- if the wider illustrated-memory pool shows the next threshold, match a corn
  row to the Arabian Sea horizon and then to an anonymous Trivandrum workplace.
  The exact company, campus and beach remain unidentified; the near-absence of
  tourists is the Founder's memory of one shoreline, not a regional claim;
- show the Indian-company job only through neutral office rhythm, never an
  identifiable employer or a claim that he was unemployed;
- the wider memory pool may show an anonymous United States work assignment as
  a clock, workload and promised-overtime ledger that fails to become payment.
  Do not identify the employer, dramatize wage theft as an adjudicated fact or
  merge this employer with the earlier Indian company;
- preserve the assignment's non-binary human truth in the wider memory pool:
  roughly four or five months based at an employer-arranged hotel outside
  Baltimore, a company-rented car, Ocean City, Washington/Adams Morgan,
  Baltimore–New York Greyhound travel, Niagara Falls, Amish country, an
  amusement park and Appalachian/park landscapes. The New York-to-Baltimore
  return becomes the moment the temporary base first feels like home. Exact
  Ocean City and amusement-park identification remains `CONFIRM`;
- Adams Morgan carries a first-person memory of women smiling and sometimes
  initiating contact. Show reciprocal social openness, never conquest or a
  claim about American women. Do not show visa/work-authorization status;
- six and a half months without work and a four-round small-company interview
  with psychological testing can compress into calendar pages and four neutral
  thresholds. The final non-selection is a personal memory, not a claim that
  psychological testing is inherently abusive;
- a workers'-hostel key, a late locked-room/corridor beat and an anonymous
  temporary-room suitcase compress the housing instability. Do not show a
  named street, identifiable landlord or alleged unlawful act;
- the parents' help becomes a final flat key beside an unsigned, unused
  foreign-currency-loan form. It signifies an avoided path, not a claim that a
  specific loan would certainly have been taken;
- warm groups remain visible through tram, museum and café frames while he
  returns alone; nobody visibly rejects or mocks him.

### London

- the Founder finds professional work within two weeks despite weak English;
  competence is visible in the software work while conversational isolation is
  visible in missed lunch rhythm and restrained silence, never comic accent or
  incompetence;
- for almost three years he can barely converse with colleagues. Trivandrum is
  the beginning of practical English, not instant fluency;
- no montage of Big Ben, red bus and phone box as shorthand;
- begin with a restrained 2011 arrival threshold; if health vulnerability enters
  an expanded board, use only a neutral clinic doorway or appointment envelope,
  never blood, diagnosis, NHS branding or medical spectacle;
- place the exceptional public energy of the 2012 Diamond Jubilee and Olympic
  summer in the following-year London sequence without official marks or royal
  likenesses; it is not the arrival date;
- use the specific lived world: a modest Soho software office, museums,
  exhibitions, nightlife, multicultural friendships and dates, one brief late
  Victoria line reflection, then Tottenham street and Victorian terrace;
- one distant skyline glimpse is enough;
- the surrounding city is visibly international, but the converted Victorian
  HMO is a Hungarian microcosm: the Founder plus twelve other Hungarian adults;
  make one full bathroom and one separate WC spatially legible, then show the
  Founder's shared bedroom with two personal zones;
- at lunch, colleagues leave into expensive Soho together while the Founder
  performs “not hungry.” Only after they leave does he use a two-litre milk
  bottle and muesli. The hidden constraint, not the diet, is the story;
- every flatmate has a trace of individual life — food, photograph, coat,
  greeting, routine and a different reason for leaving Hungary — rather than
  becoming anonymous crowd texture;
- preserve one dry, humane memory as a short sound bridge: after a flatmate has
  lost his money gambling and swaps places with an acquaintance, the Founder
  notices the unannounced change only because a different man is snoring in the
  shared room. The absurd housing turnover is the joke; debt and the person are
  not;
- South Tottenham sits beside the Stamford Hill/South Tottenham Charedi Jewish
  community. Ordinary street details may locate the house respectfully, but no
  religious resident becomes exotic background. The Founder may express that
  his immediate area felt safer while nearby directions felt more volatile;
  never claim the community caused safety or make a general crime assertion;
- the first three years retain wonder: a one-pound overnight coach to Edinburgh,
  Loch Ness, a Glasgow River Clyde powerboat, and an exact but over-compressed
  five-person London–Manchester–Liverpool–Barmouth–Cardiff–London rental-car
  circuit may flash as personal adventures. Two couples and the Founder travel;
  he plans but cannot drive, so a participating acquaintance drives, never a
  hired chauffeur. Unbranded budget hotels supply accommodation during a
  three-day itinerary that needs a fourth day to become humane rather than a
  rush. The point is his willingness to trade comfort for discovery, not a
  tourism catalogue;
- the rent saved by sharing the bedroom finances travel and lets savings
  accumulate. By December 2015, the savings support financial independence only
  at Budapest's lower cost base, reinforced by the parent-assisted flat that has
  stood empty during the London years. They never support financial independence
  in London. Years four and five reveal that a temporary bargain is becoming a
  permanent life; a normal London home would erode the Budapest position, so
  housing becomes the Founder's stop-loss.
  Brexit appears only as quiet public context around work and belonging, not
  proof that migrants displaced British workers or lowered wages;
- an imagined independent rental may show why the founder chose sharing, but no
  invented rent, wage or savings figure appears;
- international cultures live in the office, public city, friendships and
  dates; inside the house, individuality comes from personal history rather
  than national variety;
- travel is shown through repeated door frames and carriage windows;
- long work is shown by changing daylight, emptied desks and repeated meals,
  never by humiliating the worker or blaming one employer.

### Illustrated travel memories

- travel memories summarize the founder's wider life, not an invented sequence
  of factory assignments before London;
- no archive photos survive, so generate explicitly illustrated memories rather
  than fake home-video footage;
- the three-second burst can match-cut High Tatras → Schönbrunn arch →
  Neuschwanstein silhouette → Founder/tuk-tuk handoff at Angkor → reciprocal
  Taj Mahal bus conversation → Norwegian weather → one confirmed Egypt human-
  adventure frame → Chesapeake/Niagara water;
- the Egypt trip occurred after the London homecoming. Fold time through the
  notebook so the montage remains thematic rather than implying false
  chronology. Prefer cooperation around the Luxor balloon's vegetable-field
  landing over a landmark collage. Keep the Cairo taxi-roof luggage mishap as
  the first human alternate, not an additional ninth frame;
- hold each view for about 8–12 frames and use shape/motion continuity, not
  flags, souvenir costumes or landmark labels;
- the last view resolves into the founder's reflection and an empty adjacent
  seat, turning scale into intimacy.

### Bad-date locations

1. chain-like café, deliberately fictional and logo-free;
2. basement bar with too many reflective hard surfaces;
3. oversized formal restaurant where the table becomes a visual barrier.

### MyScoutee event

- independent multi-use community/culture venue;
- entrance visible from the table;
- quiet conversation zone plus shared activity;
- warm 3200 K practicals, soft daylight residue, acoustic panels/books/plants;
- clearly public and hosted, accessible route implied without turning it into an
  invented event-resource pictogram;
- six seats in a soft arc, not opposing interview pairs.
- later-event background participants widen the mix of adult ages and life
  stages organically; each remains visually distinct and able to accept or decline.

### Local-operator network workspace

Use one credible Linux/server workstation beside an own-brand organizer surface
and local community venue. A hardware key, compact install package and blank
verification checklist show a technically accountable handoff. Reveal several
independently hosted local servers and culturally distinct community tests around
one common coral core; one node can opt out while staying independently operable.
Reserve an exact central plate for the original unmodified repository
`frontend/src/assets/logo/heart.png`, composited in post. Its helmet-like shelter,
guiding star and central heart visually connect protection, direction and human
connection without turning the mark into a certification or safety guarantee.
This is the intended virtual-enterprise model, not a claim of current deployment,
proven efficiency or physical hardware shipment.

## 8. Recurring visual motifs

### The line

- Budapest tram wire on departure and return;
- London office timeline and rail line;
- phone swipe trail;
- dance-floor marking;
- coffee steam;
- Tube map line;
- notebook pencil line;
- direct invitation path and visibly open event chair;
- walking route;
- operator network connection.

At first the line repeatedly ends at the frame edge. After the insight it begins
to connect people and places without tying or trapping them.

Across travel, the line is global but its texture changes locally: rail, road,
river, footpath, tuk-tuk handoff or a host's gesture. A local person briefly
touches or redirects it, and the Founder's reflection changes with the
environment. In the product ending the same line becomes a shared technical core,
while the surrounding routes retain different colors, rhythms, brands and local
authors. Never render “global” as a homogenized world map or a ring of flags.

### Doors and thresholds

Every attempt uses a doorway. Early doors lead to another beginning. The event
door in shot S20 remains open and visible, symbolizing both invitation and easy
exit.

### The empty chair

An empty chair appears in the café, formal restaurant, London flat and founder's
workspace. In the event it is not “filled by the perfect partner”; it becomes
part of a six-seat arc. The problem transforms from missing one person to
creating a workable social context.

### The product mark

Use the unmodified repository `heart.png` briefly during the first 1.2 seconds,
at the shared MyScoutee Network core in S22 and fully on the end card. Composite
it in post at original proportions and colors; never ask a model to redraw it.

## 9. Cinematography bible

### Camera grammar by act

| Act | Lens language | Movement | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | mostly 28–40 mm equivalent, occasionally 50 mm for faces | continuous lateral track, hidden transitions, mild handheld only on bad dates | protagonist often occluded or divided by architecture |
| II | 50–65 mm for attention, top-down 35 mm for notebook | camera stops, then measured dolly/overhead moves | lines lead inward; faces gain breathing room |
| III | 35–50 mm | doorway track, gentle table arc, restrained workstation move | returning members stay balanced; Founder and operator meet as accountable peers |

Rules:

- no more than one conspicuous camera move in a shot;
- no constant push-in; reserve the strongest push for the insight at 01:53;
- keep faces at least 15% of frame height when expression matters;
- compose natively for 9:16: stagger multi-person depth vertically and reserve
  the top/bottom mobile UI zones;
- avoid extreme shallow depth on group shots; every participant needs agency;
- stabilize horizon through emotional clarity; mild Dutch framing appears only
  for the formal restaurant and resolves immediately;
- generate handles of at least 12 frames at both ends.

### Transition grammar

- foreground occlusion rather than dissolves in Act I;
- graphic match on line/shape in Act II;
- clean cuts on shared action in Act III;
- one short dissolve only: S08's last water memory into the solitary reflection
  around 01:03.

## 11. Acting and facial direction

AI video tends to overplay. Every prompt should use verbs and micro-actions, not
emotion labels alone.

Use:

- “his eyes search for the last word while the steam wand interrupts”;
- “she inhales to answer, then smiles because neither heard the question”;
- “his shoulders drop one centimeter when the pattern becomes clear”;
- “Maya reads three notifications, the fourth arrives before she finishes, and
  her thumb stops”;
- “the operator scans the entrance, checks the seat arc, then welcomes the first
  participant.”

Avoid:

- exaggerated sad eyes or tears;
- slumped defeated posture;
- finger-pointing, angry swiping or contemptuous eye rolls;
- generic continuous smiling;
- everyone laughing at exactly the same time;
- visible word articulation or lip sync; mouths remain naturally relaxed and
  minute jaw movement is allowed only as ordinary breathing.

## 12. Sound world

The sonic line mirrors the visual line:

- rural wind/wood/soil → train seam → Danube/tram bell → office ventilation →
  lunch chairs/quiet milk pour → room key → eight travel hits
  → tram bell → app tick → dance count → espresso steam → pencil stroke → chat
  send → ticket scan → room tone → final two-note motif.

Music begins with one felt-piano note and bowed texture. A restrained 7/8 pulse
under the attempt montage produces slight incompleteness. At the insight, it
resolves into 4/4 without becoming a corporate anthem. The final lift adds warm
strings, low woodwind and human hand percussion, not a giant trailer brass hit.

The voice is close, dry and human. Preserve breaths. Do not use a booming
motivational delivery.
