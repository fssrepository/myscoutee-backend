## The complete product reason in compressed form

Time is finite, so the product must filter. Its intended speed is the speed of
reaching a reciprocal invitation and real context, not the speed of judging a
person's worth. The six-person group reduces the all-or-nothing pressure of one
pair: even when one possible romantic path does not continue, the meeting can
still contain several conversations, shared activity, friendship and later
invitations. This is the design rationale, not a proven outcome claim.

After a real meeting, each participant's private experience may raise or lower
their own rates. Those changing inputs, reciprocity, distance and availability
reshape later candidates. If somebody declines for lack of time, Auto Inviter
may offer the place again, but the next adult chooses independently and the
system cannot guarantee a full group.

The emotional thesis is acceptance without erasure. People can continue through
problems together only when important context, consent, safety and boundaries
remain visible. MyScoutee is meant to give that decision a faster, more human
setting; it does not classify imperfect people away or promise a relationship.

Localization means the real place, distance, timetable, transport, resources,
language, culture, venue and accountable local operator. A country change can
restart the same method of building local context, but it does not currently
move profiles, chats, contacts or private rate history automatically between
independent operators. That migration remains later roadmap work.

## Belonging without a return statistic

The Founder remembers that many people around the Hungarian London household —
perhaps roughly one in three — no longer wanted to return home, and some felt
happier in London despite crowded material conditions. This stays personal
memory, never an on-screen `30%` or a general migration claim. The film contrasts
different relationships to home without calling anyone rootless, failed or
disloyal.

That ambiguity belongs to the product thesis: a global life does not erase local
roots, and a local home does not guarantee belonging. MyScoutee's shared core is
therefore not global sameness. Its operators and communities give it local
language, places, customs, boundaries and meaning.

## Age and return wording

Age discrimination exists in employment and digital dating across countries.
The film may show the Founder's experience that it felt less pronounced abroad,
but it must not claim that age discrimination is uniquely Hungarian or absent
in London.

Safe product line:

> Screens made us judge age, appearance and history before meeting; shared human
> context disappeared.

MyScoutee is not age-blind and does not promise to remove prejudice. Birthday
and derived age exist in the current profile. The honest visual claim is that
people across adult life stages remain full participants with private intent,
agency, group context, invitations and real events rather than being visually
erased. The first six-person group is cast by plausible need, not a quota. It
may lean toward the thirties, forties and fifties, the current working picture
of likely users, but an adult in their twenties or sixties can fit naturally.

Research boundary:

- European Commission analysis identifies age discrimination and stereotypes
  about adaptability, technical skill and trainability as barriers to hiring
  older workers across the EU;
- UOC researchers describe digital ageism in dating platforms and warn that
  age can shape filtering and visibility;
- these sources support the existence of the problem, not a claim that one
  country or one recruiter discriminated against the Founder.
