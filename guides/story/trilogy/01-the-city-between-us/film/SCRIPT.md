### English performance and legal notes

- Soho, Tottenham, the Victoria line, thirteen residents, one full bathroom and
  one separate WC, and the shared bedroom are first-person founder details.
  “One and a half bathrooms” is production shorthand for that exact arrangement.
- Countryside origin, small-producer agricultural ancestry, completed university
  studies combining economics, programming and mathematics in 2004, a delayed
  dissertation making 2005 the earliest possible diploma year, the diploma then
  remaining unavailable until one intermediate-level language certificate was
  obtained, and joining an Indian company in October 2005 are first-person
  Founder facts. Actual diploma receipt occurred considerably later; its exact
  date is not yet stated.
  The opening does not
  claim that every rural Hungarian moves to Budapest or that the labour market
  universally excludes hybrid graduates.
- The Budapest workers' hostel, late lockout/corridor sleep and undisclosed
  summer-substitute room are personal memories. Anonymize the private-rental
  location and people; the film makes no legal allegation. The hostel is shown
  as impersonal but comparatively predictable, not as a claim of homelessness.
- The Founder's parents helped him acquire a flat before London, and in his
  account he therefore avoided taking a foreign-currency mortgage. That avoided
  path is personal counterfactual context, not a guarantee of what would have
  happened without their help.
- Before London, the Founder reports working in the United States through an
  anonymous employer, leaving after promised overtime pay did not arrive, then
  spending six and a half months unemployed. His final Hungarian application
  reached a fourth round with psychological testing and still ended without an
  offer. These are first-person facts, not findings against identifiable firms.
- Blood in the Founder's stool first appeared during the American assignment's
  extreme work pressure. He associated the symptom with that pressure, but the
  film makes no medical diagnosis or independent causation claim. He moved to
  London in 2011 while still experiencing it, with inactive Hungarian public-
  health-insurance coverage, and received medical care in London. This remains
  non-graphic and omitted from the locked hero-film VO unless later approved.
- He found London work within two weeks despite weak English and reports that he
  could barely converse with colleagues for almost three years. Do not portray
  him as professionally incompetent or claim that Trivandrum had made him
  fluent; it was the beginning of practical English.
- Budapest is shown as a beautiful cultural capital: Danube, bridges, historic
  city layers, tram/underground, museums, music, cafés and baths. The Founder's
  exclusion and long-term solitude are personal experience, never a verdict on
  the city or Hungarian people.
- The Founder's parents helped provide his Budapest flat before London, and it
  stood empty through the five London years. Shared-bedroom savings financed
  travel and accumulated until his December 2015 diagram showed financial
  independence at Budapest's much lower cost base. He was never financially
  independent in London: normal private London housing would erode that position,
  while continued room-sharing was no longer a viable life. Housing therefore
  became his stop-loss and a material reason to return. Do not imply that the
  savings bought another home or made him financially independent in London.
- Soho lunch was socially important but too expensive for his budget. He
  presented a different self by pretending not to be hungry, then relied on a
  two-litre milk bottle and muesli. This is specific testimony, not a dietary
  recommendation, a claim of literal exclusive nutrition or proof that
  colleagues excluded him.
- The shared Victorian HMO is a deliberate financial strategy in an expensive
  city, not a poverty spectacle. Show individual flatmates and their small
  personal worlds. The Founder's agency appears in the journeys made possible by
  the saved shared-bedroom rent.
- The twelve flatmates were Hungarian. Their varied economic, professional and
  personal motives are founder memories rendered as fictional composites; the
  surrounding workplace, dating and cultural city supplies London's visible
  international mix.
- In the Founder's rough memory, many people around the household — perhaps
  around one in three — no longer wanted to return to Hungary and some felt
  happier in London despite material constraint. This is personal recollection,
  not a measured population statistic: never show `30%`, call anyone rootless or
  imply that every resident felt the same.
- The Founder arrived in London in 2011. The 2012 Jubilee/Olympic summer belongs
  to the following year of the London story. The Indian woman colleague,
  departing Polish man colleague and approximately remembered artificial penis,
  something like a vibrator, bought
  as a comic farewell gift in a Soho sex shop are personal memories. The gift
  affectionately calls him “a fucking dick”; the colleagues like him and he
  understands the joke. Keep the humor between competent friends; nationality
  is never the punchline.
- The shared-room resident swap, one-pound overnight coach to Edinburgh, Loch
  Ness, Glasgow/Clyde powerboat and compressed five-person London–Manchester–
  Liverpool–Barmouth–Cardiff–London rental-car circuit are personal memories.
  The party is two couples plus the Founder; he plans the route but cannot drive,
  so a travelling acquaintance drives as part of the group, not as a hired
  driver. Budget hotels supply the overnight accommodation. The trip has three
  days and needs a fourth to avoid the rush. Use these as quiet visual or sound
  beats under existing narration, not additional VO. The Founder confirms every
  specific landmark before it is presented as visited.
- The COVID line is the Founder's personal break between the earlier carefree
  travel feeling and later movement. It does not claim that nobody can travel as
  before or judge the earlier journeys as irresponsible.
- Most journeys were made alone and self-organized; a driver, host, hotel,
  agency or excursion may carry one handoff without owning the whole route. S20
  translates this into a DIY-first event model: fully self-organized, partially
  provider-assisted or more completely organizer-managed. The last mode is an
  intended operating direction, not a proven one-click delegation workflow.
- “Through local people” is the global/local spine: different environments and
  local encounters help the Founder recognize different parts of himself. It is
  not a tourist's claim to possess cultures. The product mirrors this with a
  globally reusable shared core whose meaning is locally authored and tested.
- Age can be shown as one premature filter in work and digital dating. The film
  does not claim that age discrimination is uniquely Hungarian, absent abroad
  or solved by MyScoutee. Older adults remain full participants in group, event
  and later-community frames; age remains profile context, not a public score.
- The product idea originated in 2016. Later AI assistance accelerated
  implementation; it did not invent the product, validate it or turn code volume
  into traction. The ending asks operators, pilot communities and product/venue/
  experience partners to test the built core.
- The adventure/crowding contrast carries the soul of the London chapter: a
  narrow private life existed beside a vast, beautiful public world. Do not turn
  either the hardship into misery spectacle or the travel into a tourism reel.
- The Brexit reference marks the founder's changing sense of work and belonging.
  It must not claim that migration generally displaced UK-born workers or caused
  low wages. Any argument about jobs remains background debate, not film fact.
- The spoken script does not list travel destinations. During its three-second
  burst, use only the confirmed illustrated memories in the creative bible.
- The travel burst is thematic, not strict chronology. The six-day Egypt trip
  happened after the Founder had returned from London; do not edit it as if it
  preceded the homecoming. A time-fold through the notebook can include it
  without changing the spoken return line.
- Lift warmly on “came home.” The physical return is purposeful and turns toward
  relationship and full focus, but it is not yet accepted as permanent: London
  remains a possible destination after a break. It is not a failed migration or
  a final rejection of London.
- Dance and date lines carry observation and dry warmth, never resentment.
- Pause before “the bridge.” The product follows a relationship lifecycle:
  editable priority → reciprocal/context group of strangers → chat → managed
  first event → known contacts → later invitations/repeated events → evolving
  priority. Community is a possible result of repetition, not the starting state.
- Lowering a priority after conflict is a private change to future weighting,
  not public punishment, reputation damage or a claim of proven matching quality.
- Direct invitations belong after people have met and become known contacts.
  Auto Inviter may fill available event places according to the priority plan.
- The film may say the workflow is designed to remove missing coordination
  steps. It must not claim measured speed or superior meeting rates.
- “Filter” means a rapid path from finite attention to a reciprocal candidate
  invitation, not a rapid judgment of human worth. The group format reduces the
  all-or-nothing pressure of one pair and may preserve value through several
  conversations, shared activity, friendship or a later invitation. These are
  design reasons, not measured outcomes.
- The planner and Auto Inviter target the intended accepted capacity after one
  person declines. Never claim that every group remains full; availability and
  consent can still leave an open place.
- Localization includes distance, place, timing, transport, resources, language,
  culture, venues and accountable local operation. A person changing countries
  may reuse the same method, but current independent deployments do not
  automatically carry profiles, chats, contacts or private rate history between
  operators.
- Operators are technically capable and run independently hosted, own-brand
  local deployments. The shared-core server network/virtual-enterprise and
  opt-out are an intended operating model, not current-deployment or proven-
  efficiency claims. Reconcile exact legal/network wording before release.
- Film 1 is a complete product film and contains no mention, hint, icon or sound
  concerning the Founder's later legal, tax or court matter. A future Film 2 may
  begin from its exact final frame but must be written separately from documents.

## 3. Subtitle rules

- Make SRT files after the final edit, not from planning timings.
- Maximum two lines, approximately 30–34 characters per line in 9:16.
- Keep most subtitles at least one second and roughly 12–17 characters/second.
- Break at natural phrases; identify meaningful nonverbal sounds when needed.
- There is no character dialogue or lip sync. John carries the complete spoken
  track; interaction is conveyed through listening, eye contact and gesture.
- Preserve a textless clean master for future languages.
