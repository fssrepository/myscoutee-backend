# MyScoutee — The Story book changelog

The trilogy part and the edition version are separate. This book is Part
`I`; its first public edition is `1.0.0`.

## Unreleased correction of 1.0.0 — 2026-08-09

- Corrected the Soho lunch memory across the editable book and production
  package. The Founder did not remain behind when colleagues ate: he joined
  group and farewell lunches, studied the prices, watched colleagues eat and
  performed “not hungry” as if it were a Hungarian habit. When he finally
  ordered food once or twice in year four or five, colleagues were surprised.
- Kept muesli and two-litre milk bottles away from the restaurant and connected
  every avoided meal to the private chart rising toward Budapest-cost financial
  independence—the reason for the Founder's extreme London focus. No colleague
  exclusion or diet claim is implied.

- Replaced book-series terminology from `Volume` with the more natural `Part`:
  this is Part I of one continuous trilogy.
- Added the post-return Budapest nightlife and re-entry-shock sequence: the

  shared interest and friendship. The book and film now treat that friendship
  as an equally valuable outcome rather than failed romance.

  date. After returning alone from London, the Founder began attending
  occasionally, never regularly, and it eased that loneliness. Its
  broader-than-dating philosophy and later geographic spread remain generalized;
  name, founding date, branding, event titles, locations and scale figures are
  intentionally excluded.
- Clarified the Founder's outcome there: adults attended as singles, but he
  found somewhere not to be alone, met a few people and gained programs,
  acquaintances and friendship rather than a partner. Crowd
  size, social caution and fear of gossip remain possible explanations, not
  asserted causes. The community supplied some inspiration, while its local
  organizer-led model remains distinct from MyScoutee's intended shared-core,
  local-operator scaling architecture.
- Added the 2017 post-return dating memory: almost two years without a match, an
  unusually flattering profile photograph, a subjectively promising date with
  an anonymous investment-bank financial adviser, the Founder's evasive answer
  about applying there because London remained emotionally unfinished, no
  second date or conversation, and the encounter's limited but real nudge toward
  re-entering ordinary Hungarian institutional and working life.
- Added the non-legal Part II bridge: after many interviews and a near return
  to London, the Founder entered an apparently stable Hungarian multinational
  role in August 2019; COVID ended it quickly, then enabled a Brussels-linked
  contract that looked less secure but proved more durable without requiring
  another move abroad. The memoir bounds 2021 as the Founder's one open remote-
  hiring year and the 2022 closure as his own applicant experience, not a
  universal labour-market claim.
- Added the London wedding invitation the Founder did not accept: a Black
  colleague's gesture felt like inclusion, while the Founder's fear of exposing
  his own culture shock kept him away. The memory now foreshadows the sharper
  reverse-culture shock he later experienced in Budapest without defining the
  colleague or ceremony through ethnicity.
- Restored the pre-London overtime-hearing corridor memory: an exchange about
  possible work for the Founder's lawyer's son damaged trust and helped make
  departure feel rational. It remains attributed memoir and makes no bribery,
  misconduct or case-outcome finding.
- Corrected that chronology across the book and memory-board prompts: the
  American assignment ended before the employment did. The Founder returned to
  the Hungarian office, continued working there for a time and experienced the
  missing promised overtime as a trust conflict before the later departure.

## Publication rule

- Clarified in the reader note that all character and scene illustrations are
  AI-generated visual interpretations and do not depict the actual participants.

- The Founder approved and published the first public edition as `1.0.0` on
  7 August 2026.
- Until the Founder explicitly locks the edition, the current versioned export
  may be rebuilt. After locking, use a patch increment for corrections that do
  not change meaning (`1.0.1`), a minor increment for added or materially
  revised content (`1.1.0`), and a major increment for a substantially
  restructured new edition (`2.0.0`).
- A later trilogy part receives its own Part number and its own independent
  edition-version history; Part `II` is not version `2.0.0` of Part `I`.
