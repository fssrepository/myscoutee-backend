## Stop-ship faults

Do not publish while any of these remain:

- an identifiable or unsupported employer allegation;
- altered or redrawn MyScoutee branding;
- generated nonsense text;
- identity drift or severe anatomy failure;
- a gender-, age- or nationality-generalizing narrative;
- a false product, traction, safety, revenue or outcome claim;
- an unvisited location presented as personal memory;
- unreadable mobile text;
- clipped, distorted or discontinuous audio;
- an export that does not play completely.
