The next work is less cinematic and more important: performance and mobile hardening, safety and privacy review, real-world testing, one dense pilot, measurement from proposal through acceptance, chat, event, attendance and feedback, and a launch process that a local operator can repeat.

That requires partners:

technically capable local operators;

pilot communities willing to test the complete path;

product collaborators;

venue, activity, travel and experience partners;

later strategic partners who understand the difference between built scope and proven outcomes.

I am not looking for somebody to admire the notebook.

I am looking for people willing to test the path.

Epilogue — The Star Above the Open Space.

The MyScoutee mark contains a helmet-like shelter, a star and an open human space at its centre.

I do not treat it as a guarantee. A logo cannot certify safety, direction or connection. It can only hold an intention.

When I close the cognac notebook, the routes do not disappear. The field row is still inside the rail line. Budapest is still beautiful. The hostel corridor, the London window, the shared bedroom, the one-pound coach, the fallen luggage, the balloon in the vegetable plot, the bus voice behind me in Agra, the Russian passenger's card, the local ride to the pier, the bridges inside the gorge and the six chairs around a real table remain connected by memory.

For years, I believed the central problem was finding the right person.

Now I believe the world already contains more possible connection than we can see. The missing element is often the path: context before verdict, intention without public humiliation, coordination without coercion, memory without gossip, and enough local humanity to turn a route into a meeting.

The path cannot manufacture acceptance. It can give acceptance a fairer chance to become mutual, informed and real.

After many interviews, I found a multinational position in Hungary in August 2019. I had come close to retreating to London again; by chance, I no longer had to. The position looked stable. COVID-19 quickly destroyed that expectation.

The same pandemic opened a different door: cross-border remote work. I secured a contract role connected to another multinational in Brussels. It had looked less stable than the Hungarian position, yet proved more durable. Without the new remote arrangement, I would have had to move abroad again. Professionally, I was already standing with one foot beyond the border while still living at home.

In my experience, 2021 was the one open year. Once the pandemic receded, a remote arrangement already secured could remain if the company did not require personal attendance. In 2022, as an unknown new applicant, I was no longer being accepted on the same terms.

What followed belongs to Part II.

Years ago, another Indian colleague in the London office told me that I should write a novel.

I did not write it then.

I am writing it now.

My story does not end with a perfect match.

It ends with an invitation.

MyScoutee. Test it with me in real communities.
