HUNGARIAN ORIGIN — RURAL ROOTS, BUDAPEST WORK AND UNSTABLE ROOMS

Create one portrait 9:16 planning board with exactly eight clearly separated
cinematic memory panels. This is a visual-development board for a humane
founder film, not a single continuous scene and not final archive evidence.

STYLE AND IDENTITY
Premium painterly 2.5D animation: expressive hand-drawn adult character
linework integrated into tactile paper and miniature sets, realistic motivated
light, quiet documentary observation, restrained micro-expression. Preserve the
same adult Founder in all relevant panels: intelligent, capable, ordinary,
slightly tired but never defeated; navy field coat, charcoal knit, muted rust
scarf, practical brown shoes, compact dark backpack and worn cognac notebook.
No text, captions, logos, readable documents, employer branding or identifiable
private person.

ARCHIVE MEMORY LOCK — SECONDARY-SCHOOL RUNNING
Before university, the Founder loves running and enters races. In one bounded
memory, anonymized classmates catch hold of his arms and try to pull him back;
he regains his stride and finishes first. Use breath, cadence, footfall and the
return of rhythm as the emotional language. Natural ability may help, but the
Founder's personal lesson is breathing and endurance, not a universal genetics
claim. No bullying spectacle, violence, revenge, humiliated classmates, podium,
medal or superhuman athlete. This remains archive material and does not become a
ninth panel or enter the locked hero-film opening unless the opening is recut.

PANEL 1 — AGRICULTURAL ROOTS
Earlier generations of small agricultural producers in rural Hungary: working
hands, soil, seasonal rows, weathered tools and a produce crate. Practical skill
and finite resources, not costume folklore, puszta cliché, aristocratic estate,
nostalgia or poverty spectacle.

PANEL 2 — TOWARD BEAUTIFUL BUDAPEST
An ordinary rural station and commuters become a yellow-tram line entering
Budapest. Use only two or three truthful recognition motifs: Danube and bridge
geometry, Buda–Pest historic layers, museum/concert light, old-underground
rhythm, café or thermal steam. The city is beautiful, culturally alive and full
of possibility; never make Hungary grey or backward.

PANEL 3 — COMPLETED STUDIES AND PROFESSIONAL WORK
The Founder studies three interlocking visual systems — economics, programming
logic and mathematics — and finishes university in 2004. A delayed dissertation
makes 2005 the earliest possible diploma year, but an abstract closed diploma
folder remains unissued much longer because one required intermediate-level
language certificate is still missing.
He later works competently in an anonymous professional office. Do not show an
issued diploma, permanent joblessness, a named company, factory or generic
worksite. The separate cornfield-to-Trivandrum board preserves the exact first
international assignment and practical-English threshold.

PANEL 4 — THE WORKERS' HOSTEL AT NIGHT
After returning late from work, the Founder finds the shared hostel room locked.
He remains composed and settles briefly in the corridor with his backpack and
notebook. The hostel is impersonal, rule-bound and modest but maintained; no
homelessness, degradation, guards, violence, filth or melodramatic collapse.

PANEL 5 — THE UNDISCLOSED TEMPORARY ROOM
An anonymous Budapest private room contains a half-unpacked suitcase and signs
that an absent social circle expects to return. The Founder realizes that what
he understood as a normal rental was only an undisclosed summer substitution
and that the arrangement has become untenable. Show ambiguity and pressure only
through space, packed objects and a closing door. Do not reproduce the real
street, identify owners or occupants, invent threats, show confrontation or
make a legal accusation.

PANEL 6 — PREDICTABLE IMPERSONALITY
The Founder returns to the workers' hostel, accepts a plain key and follows
clear shared rules. The place is emotionally impersonal but comparatively
legible: a posted grid without readable words, consistent bed zone and neutral
staff handoff. The emotional point is predictable access versus opaque personal
acceptance, not that hostels are desirable or private rentals are universally
hostile.

PANEL 7 — PARENTS HELP SECURE A FLAT
The Founder's parents place a modest apartment key in his hand. Beside it lies
an unsigned, unused foreign-currency-loan form rendered without readable text.
Their help gives material security and, in his personal interpretation, avoids
the foreign-currency-mortgage route. Do not depict loan repayment, foreclosure
or claim that a particular debt certainly would have happened.

PANEL 8 — HOME WITHOUT SHARED LIFE, THEN LONDON
From the modest flat, the Founder sees warm Budapest groups through tram,
museum and café reflections while his own window holds one quiet figure. The
flat is real security, but it does not automatically create belonging. His
cognac notebook, suitcase and a rain-softened route toward London form the next
threshold. No crowd rejects him, no verdict on Hungarian people, no claim that
London will solve him.

COMPOSITION
Every panel must remain independently crop-safe for 9:16 generation. Use
repeating lines — field row, rail, tram wire, corridor edge, door frame and key
— to create match-cut continuity. Leave faces large enough for identity review.
No tourist checklist, flags, maps, national costume, generated writing, fake
archive labels, police, courtroom imagery or villain framing.

OUTPUT
Generate exactly one eight-panel planning board. Do not animate it, add music,
voice-over or extend the film. The board is for confirming which memories enter
S01–S02 and which remain archive material.
