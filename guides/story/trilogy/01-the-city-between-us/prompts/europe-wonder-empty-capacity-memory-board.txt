PURPOSE
Create a planning storyboard board, not a final video, for the Founder's
European travel memories. The surface is beauty and highly coordinated movement;
the underlying MyScoutee image is unused human capacity — a ten-berth ferry
cabin or three-bed hostel room occupied by one person. Wonder and solitude must
coexist without implying that every solo journey was unhappy.

CONFIRMATION LOCK
Exact Italy changes/stops, Paris day trips beyond Versailles/Disneyland Paris,
the Norwegian route direction, Rotterdam ferry ports/operator/berth count and
Spanish city/property remain CONFIRM. Portugal is locked to Porto–Lisbon over
four days; individual monuments remain recognition candidates. Norway in a Nutshell is
PROBABLE based on the Founder's Bergen plus coordinated train/boat/bus memory.
Do not add a location label until confirmed.

STYLE AND IDENTITY
Use the approved Founder identity and premium painterly cinematic 2.5D style.
Architecture, terrain and transport should be recognizable without flags,
tourism-poster saturation, logos, readable tickets or generated text. 9:16,
mobile-safe. No fake archive footage.

PANEL 1 — ITALY, THE BEAUTIFUL RAIL SPINE
Begin in Bergamo and let one elegant rail line unfold south toward Naples.
Milan–Naples high-speed operation is factual current context, but the Founder's
historic changes and intermediate stops remain CONFIRM. Use separate truthful
city glimpses only after confirmation; never merge northern arcades, Rome and
the Bay of Naples into one invented panorama. Emotional note: this was the
Founder's most beautiful journey even without a personal anecdote.

PANEL 2 — PARIS AS A COMPASS
Place the Founder at a simple Paris rail-map center. On successive days one
route leaves in a different compass direction. Confirmed examples: Versailles
and Disneyland Paris/Marne-la-Vallée. Keep other spokes blank until destinations
are recalled. The idea is daily curiosity, not the false statement that Paris's
main sights sit outside the city.

PANEL 3 — PROBABLE NORWAY IN A NUTSHELL
Build a clean transport relay from Bergen: railway → Myrdal/Flåm Railway → Flåm
→ Nærøyfjord boat → Gudvangen → bus → Voss → railway back to Bergen. Direction
may reverse. Pass one small cognac notebook from conductor-like handoff to dock
to bus as a visual continuity device, but do not imply a human guide accompanied
the self-guided trip. This is a strong candidate until the Founder recognizes
the place sequence.

PANEL 4 — ROTTERDAM FERRY, TEN PLACES AND ONE PERSON
Inside a ferry cabin bound for Rotterdam, show the Founder with one compact bag
and approximately ten clearly unused berths. The room is quiet, clean and almost
absurdly over-capacity. Do not identify the operator or port, make the cabin
luxurious, or suggest loneliness as objective fact. This is a primary visual
candidate for the personal film and an optional replacement for one generic
landscape in the hero-film travel burst.

PANEL 5 — SPAIN, THREE BEDS AND ONE PERSON
In a youthful Spanish hostel or university-accommodation room, roughly three
beds are assigned but only the Founder arrives. Warm exterior life spills under
the door while the spare beds remain still. City and property are CONFIRM. Do
not imply that students rejected him or that the accommodation was empty.

PANEL 6 — PORTO TO LISBON IN FOUR DAYS
Four notebook tabs move from Porto to Lisbon. Porto begins close, granite and
steep beside the Douro: Ribeira, Dom Luís I Bridge, Clérigos and São Bento tile
rhythm are candidates only. The same river line becomes Lisbon's brighter,
wider Tagus light: Baixa and Praça do Comércio, Alfama/tram, São Jorge viewpoints
and Belém are candidates only. Delete any landmark the Founder does not
recognize. Preserve city atmosphere without azulejo, tram or port-wine cliché
overload. No invented personal encounter.

PANEL 7 — NEUSCHWANSTEIN, ONE SOUL-SIZED FLASH
Show Neuschwanstein from a truthful Bavarian exterior viewpoint for one clean
silhouette, then let its tower shape become an empty berth reading light. It is
not labelled “Disney castle,” has no Disney mark and is not turned into fantasy
architecture.

EMOTIONAL PURPOSE
Europe's trains, boats and day routes can connect distant places with exquisite
precision. They do not automatically place a mutually available person in the
next berth, bed or seat. MyScoutee's role is not to fill capacity mechanically;
it is to make consented social context and realistic availability visible so an
invitation can exist.

OUTPUT
One clean seven-panel planning board with no generated text. Editor adds only
confirmed place and route labels in post. Generate no motion.
