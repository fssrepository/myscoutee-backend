THE LAST STRAW BEFORE LONDON — WORK, REJECTION AND A NEW EXIT

TIMELINE LOCK
The Founder left his final Hungarian employer around September 2010. Roughly
six and a half months without work preceded the 2011 London move. This became
the beginning of a wider, roughly eight-year feeling of having disappeared from
ordinary Hungarian institutional visibility, ending on 10 November 2018. He was
not physically abroad throughout those years. Do not turn the period into
continuous exile or a claim that authorities could not locate him.

PANEL 1 — WORK IN THE UNITED STATES

PANEL 3 — PAYMENT DOES NOT ARRIVE; HE LEAVES

A calendar folds through six full months and one half-month while the Founder

PANEL 5 — FOUR INTERVIEW GATES

PANEL 6 — STILL NO OFFER

PANEL 7 — LONDON OPENS QUICKLY, LANGUAGE DOES NOT
