BUDAPEST 2017 — PHOTOGRAPH, DATE AND UNFINISHED RETURN

Create one portrait 9:16 visual-development board with exactly four clearly
separated cinematic memory panels. This is a privacy-safe book and character
reference, not an extra scene for the locked 03:00 hero film.

STYLE, IDENTITY AND PRIVACY
Premium painterly cinematic 2.5D animation with expressive hand-drawn adult
faces, tactile paper and miniature environments, realistic Budapest light and
quiet documentary observation. Preserve the approved adult Founder identity.
Create one distinct adult woman with ordinary professional clothes, agency and
boundaries. No real names, employer branding, bank logo, readable profile, chat
message, application form or workplace. Do not glamourize either person, infer
her motive, make her a prize or depict the Founder as entitled to continuation.

PANEL 1 — ALMOST TWO YEARS OF SILENCE
In 2017, show the Founder after almost two years without a dating-app match. A
single unusually flattering but still recognizably truthful profile photograph
of him becomes visible on a quiet phone-like frame with no legible interface.
The image opens one possibility; it is not a claim that appearance alone defines
him and not a deceptive false identity.

PANEL 2 — A SUBJECTIVE FIRST IMPRESSION
The woman's separate profile photograph catches the Founder's attention
immediately, before he knows her voice, work, temperament or circumstances.
Make the attraction explicitly subjective through his restrained reaction, not
through universal beauty coding, spotlight worship or sexualized framing. Keep
both people physically separate because they have not met.

PANEL 3 — A GOOD DATE WITH AN UNFINISHED ANSWER
They meet in an ordinary calm Budapest public setting. From the Founder's side,
the conversation feels easy. She works in financial advisory for an anonymous
multinational investment bank and asks why he does not try looking for work
there. Represent the question only through an abstract office doorway with no
logo or readable text. He answers indirectly. Behind his posture, a faint London
route remains open: money is not the real obstacle; he has not emotionally
closed London and is not yet fully present in Hungary.

PANEL 4 — NO SECOND DATE, ONE SMALL NUDGE HOME
Afterwards, show two separate paths. She does not want another date. A quiet
private-chat possibility remains visually open for a moment, but no second
conversation occurs. Her reason is unknown. Do not depict disgust, accusation,
ghosting, punishment or a hidden diagnosis of either person. The Founder turns
slightly from the fading London route toward an understated Hungarian civic and
work doorway. This encounter is one modest nudge toward registering himself
back into ordinary Hungarian institutional life and beginning to look for work,
not the sole cause of that later decision.

COMPOSITION AND OUTPUT
Use the profile-frame edge, café doorway, London route and Hungarian doorway as
match-cut geometry. Keep faces large enough for identity review and every panel
independently crop-safe. Generate exactly one four-panel planning board. No
animation, narration, music, captions, app UI, flag, logo, product interface or
claim about successful matching.
