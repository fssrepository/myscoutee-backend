PURPOSE
Create a planning storyboard board, not a final video, for two autobiographical
UK journeys made while the Founder was living very frugally in a crowded London
shared house. The emotional contrast is essential: a constricted private life
beside a vast public world. He accepts discomfort for discovery, but companions
have different limits around time, money and comfort. Nobody is judged for those
limits. Premium painterly cinematic 2.5D style, 9:16 portrait compositions.

IDENTITY AND TONE
Use the approved Founder identity and stable clothes. Curious, self-aware and
resourceful, never a heroic explorer or luxury tourist. Ordinary companions are
autonomous adults, not interchangeable travel props. Humor is dry and humane.
No tourism-ad gloss, national stereotypes, flags, fake archive footage, readable
tickets, brand marks or invented claims.

BOARD A — THE EXACT BUT OVER-COMPRESSED CIRCUIT
Show five vertically connected panels for a journey planned as:
London → Manchester → Liverpool → Barmouth → Cardiff → London.
The Founder plans the complete route for five people: two couples and himself.
He cannot drive, so they rent a car and one acquaintance travelling as a member
of the group drives it; never show a hired or uniformed chauffeur. Unbranded
budget hotels provide the overnight accommodation. Everything works on time,
yet the three-day pace is exhausting because nobody can make a fourth day
available. Show a
precise folded road route, five people and luggage entering the rental car, the
companion at the wheel, a restrained budget-hotel check-in beat, a very short shared laugh,
then tired faces arriving exactly on schedule. The problem is compatible
availability, not bad planning and not bad friends. Show no readable hotel brand.

Possible city anchors — all require Founder confirmation before final use:
- MANCHESTER CONFIRM: John Rylands Library Gothic reading room or exterior;
  Manchester Cathedral; Manchester Art Gallery; National Football Museum.
- LIVERPOOL CONFIRM: Pier Head and the Three Graces; Royal Albert Dock; the
  Mersey waterfront or ferry; Cavern/Beatles context only if actually visited.
- NORTH WALES LOCKED — BARMOUTH: very wide low-tide sand at the mouth of the
  Mawddach Estuary, with sea ahead and the town held between the surrounding
  mountains and coast. From far out on wet sand, look back toward Barmouth on
  the slope. Do not substitute another North Wales village.
- CARDIFF CONFIRM: Cardiff Castle; Victorian arcades; Cardiff Bay, Wales
  Millennium Centre or waterfront only if actually visited.
- LONDON is the departure and return frame, not another landmark checklist.

BOARD B — THE ONE-POUND SCOTTISH ADVENTURE
Show five vertically connected panels:
1. A one-pound overnight coach leaves London for Edinburgh. Inside, another
   passenger removes shoes; the Founder quietly turns toward the cold window.
   The joke is his willingness to tolerate the shared-coach smell for adventure,
   never the passenger's body or poverty.
2. Edinburgh dawn. Candidate anchors requiring confirmation: Old Town stone,
   Royal Mile, Edinburgh Castle silhouette or Arthur's Seat.
3. The Highlands and Loch Ness, treated as a real immense landscape, not a
   monster gag. Add Urquhart Castle only if confirmed.
4. Glasgow, western Scotland: a small powerboat on the River Clyde passes bridges
   and industrial waterfront. The Clyde is strongly supported by the memory but
   still requires visual recognition before final lock.
5. At an ordinary ticket counter, a German tourist hesitates at the price of a
   child's ticket. The Founder almost offers to pay so the child can join. The
   moment is generosity meeting different budgets; nationality is context, not
   the punchline. No readable price and no claim that payment occurred.

CONNECTION TO THE PRODUCT STORY
End the board with two unresolved motifs, not product UI: one empty day in the
compressed circuit and one empty seat beside the Founder. Later MyScoutee scenes
may answer this with private priorities, an independently declined invitation,
an open event place and another adult choosing to accept. Never imply forced or
automatic substitution, guaranteed companionship or that people are equivalent.

OUTPUT
One clean reference board with clearly separated panels and no generated text.
The editor adds city names, CONFIRM status and route arrows in post. Generate no
motion and no additional people beyond what each panel needs.
