PURPOSE
Create a planning storyboard board, not a final video, for the Founder's
five-to-six-day flight circuit between Istanbul and Dubai. Contrast two city
scales without reducing them to “old East” and “new wealth.” The central human
scene is an Istanbul carpet-and-tea invitation that is simultaneously warm
hospitality, craft storytelling and a commercial conversation.

CONFIRMATION LOCK
The year, flight order, nights per city and individually visited landmarks
remain CONFIRM. Every landmark below is a recognition candidate. The carpet
invitation and tea are confirmed personal memory; exact bazaar/shop, carpet
type, price and purchase outcome are not invented.

STYLE AND IDENTITY
Use the approved Founder identity in premium painterly cinematic 2.5D. Turkish
and Emirati residents, migrant workers, merchants and visitors are distinct
adults with agency, never exotic scenery. 9:16, mobile-safe. Preserve material
contrast — Istanbul stone, timber, textile and water; Dubai creek wood, sand-
filtered light, glass and steel — inside one film grade. No flags, airline logos,
generated text, fake Arabic/Turkish writing or luxury advertisement.

PANEL 1 — ONE SHORT FLIGHT CIRCUIT, TWO CITY RHYTHMS
A five-to-six-day notebook line links Istanbul and Dubai by air. Istanbul begins
with ferry arcs and dense pedestrian passages; Dubai answers with a creek curve
and precise vertical lines. Keep order and dates blank until confirmed.

PANEL 2 — ISTANBUL RECOGNITION STRIP
Atmosphere: layered, tactile, crowded but intimate; Europe and Asia joined by
water and daily movement. Candidate anchors, each separate: Hagia Sophia, Blue
Mosque, Bosphorus ferry, Galata silhouette, Grand Bazaar and Spice Bazaar. Keep
only recognized places. Do not merge every dome and bridge into a fantasy view.

PANEL 3 — INVITED IN FOR TEA
A carpet merchant or host opens a doorway and offers tea in a small tulip-shaped
glass. The Founder chooses to enter and sits at an ordinary conversational
distance. Begin with listening, hospitality and curiosity: hands pour tea,
unroll one textile and point to weave/detail. No predatory blocking, hypnosis,
sexualized exoticism or instant suspicion.

PANEL 4 — WHEN WELCOME BECOMES A SALES CONVERSATION
As successive carpets unfold, the commercial intention becomes clear. Preserve
both truths: the welcome can be genuine, and the host wants to sell. The Founder
asks questions, listens, examines the craft and retains an unobstructed exit and
the ability to decline. No fake bargain, scam accusation, forced purchase,
villainous seller or claim that tea creates an obligation.

PANEL 5 — DUBAI, OLD CREEK AND NEW VERTICALITY
Atmosphere: a rapid shift between older trade water and engineered skyline.
Candidate anchors: Dubai Creek with dhow/souk texture, then Sheikh Zayed Road,
Burj Khalifa and Downtown scale. Keep each layer distinct; do not imply Dubai
has no history or portray glass towers as moral emptiness. Include ordinary
people and daily work, not only luxury consumers.

PANEL 6 — CONTEXT CHANGES THE SAME GESTURE
Match the Istanbul tea glass to a Dubai tower/creek reflection. A simple offer
can mean welcome, craft education, negotiation or several things at once. The
Founder is not naïve for accepting and the merchant is not dishonest for
selling. What matters is whether intent, boundaries and freedom of choice remain
legible.

EMOTIONAL PURPOSE
MyScoutee should help people carry context into a meeting instead of forcing an
instant verdict from one image. It does not certify merchants, prices or
motives. The product connection is the ability to know how people arrived, what
they are doing together and whether each person can freely continue or leave.

OUTPUT
One clean six-panel planning board with no generated text. Editor adds only
confirmed city order, dates and visited-place labels in post. Generate no
motion. Keep this board for the personal film; the hero film may borrow only the
tea-to-carpet human gesture as a sub-second illustrated memory if it replaces,
rather than adds to, an existing plate.
