BUDAPEST CONNECTIONS BEFORE AND DURING LONDON — CONTEXT AND ACCEPTANCE

Create one portrait 9:16 visual-development board with exactly eight clearly
separated cinematic memory panels. This board preserves a private chapter of
the Founder's pre-London Budapest life and later visits home from London for the
public book and character development. It is not eight extra scenes for the
03:12 hero film.

STYLE, IDENTITY AND PRIVACY
Premium painterly cinematic 2.5D animation with expressive hand-drawn adult
faces, tactile paper and miniature environments, realistic motivated light and
quiet documentary observation. Preserve the same adult Founder in every panel.
Create five distinct adult women with stable identities, ordinary individual
clothes, agency, boundaries and different temperaments. They are not romantic
prizes, villains, diagnoses or a catalogue. No real names, addresses, medical
symbols, violence, readable messages, glamour casting, sexualized framing or
claim about another person's unspoken intention.

PANEL 1 — DEBRECEN AND A NARROW WINDOW
A woman arrives from Debrecen with another Budapest commitment that remains
deliberately unlabelled; it may have been a lecture or presentation, but the
exact event is unconfirmed. They spend about three hours on a date before the
programme. Afterwards they walk together to its venue. Along the way she links
arms with the Founder. At their quick goodbye outside the venue she gives him a
slightly shy cheek kiss, then leaves for the programme. Mutual possibility is
visible. The Founder cannot host her, but he has not told her that he lives in a
workers' hostel. Keep that fact as private Founder context — for example a faint
internal corridor memory or a concealed key in his pocket — never as information
she can see or understand. She forms her own incomplete picture from what he
does not explain. Show warmth constrained by distance, timing, withheld context
and space, not rejection. No readable event name or invented institution.

PANEL 2 — THE MESSAGE FROM DEBRECEN
After she has returned home to Debrecen, the same woman writes to the Founder in
a private chat that she should have kissed him instead of giving him only the
shy kiss on the cheek. Do not render the message or any chat UI as readable
text. Use a restrained visual echo of the unfinished goodbye outside the
programme venue. Do not reverse the subject: her message concerned what she
felt she should have done. Do not depict the later partner or invent broader
regret, anger or blame. The point is that attraction does not make geography
and timing disappear.

PANEL 3 — A SPONTANEOUS OPENING
A second woman approaches the Founder in a Budapest shopping centre, sits beside
him on a public bench and opens a lively conversation through abstract Chinese
and Western astrology motifs without readable charts. She creates the doorway
herself. Preserve surprise, humour and reciprocal attention; no pickup cliché.

PANEL 4 — MARGARET ISLAND IN PLACE OF A ROOM
The same pair walks and pauses on Margaret Island because neither circumstance
provides reliable private space. Trees, path, Danube light and public distance
create tenderness without voyeurism. A later difficult family/health chapter is
represented only by the Founder accompanying her toward a neutral care doorway
on a workday. No diagnosis, treatment detail, family confrontation, slap,
specific clinic or city.

PANEL 5 — SPACE EXISTS, JUDGMENT ARRIVES FIRST
Later, in an ordinary Budapest shared flat with three genuinely separate,
non-passage bedrooms, the Founder meets a third woman through online dating.
The physical boundary is finally normal, but an abstract ready-made label
briefly sits between them before softening into one private adult night. Keep it
tasteful and non-explicit. Do not imply deceit, conquest, permanence or that a
single night is failure.

PANEL 6 — THE FOUNDER CLOSES A POSSIBLE PATH
Before London, a fourth woman met through online dating makes a calm, unforced
gesture that she would like to continue getting to know the Founder. He becomes
visibly frightened by commitment and steps back. Do not make her demanding or
him a victim. The point is responsibility: rooms and systems did not break every
path; sometimes he withdrew from a real possibility.

PANEL 7 — HOME VISITS WITH INCOMPLETE CONTEXT
During a visit from London, the Founder enters a Budapest online-dating meeting
while keeping his London suitcase and route outside the visible frame. A former
woman colleague, remembered as distant during his earlier Budapest years, now
speaks with sudden intensity about love and wanting a child. Show her as a full
adult with her own unknowable reasons. Show his alarm and the missing London
context without depicting manipulation. His interpretation of her speed is not
her proven motive; his concealment of where he lived is his responsibility.

PANEL 8 — THE WHOLE PERSON AND THE FASTER HUMAN FILTER
The memories return as separate cognac-notebook fragments around one calm
shared table. Quick labels fall away while intact adult silhouettes remain.
Distance, housing, health, age, money, timing and past difficulty are context,
not complete human verdicts. End on the insight: time is finite, so the useful
filter moves reciprocal intention toward a real, humane context; it does not
filter imperfect people out of view. Acceptance means seeing a whole person and
deciding freely whether to continue through problems together, while preserving
truth, consent, safety and boundaries.

COMPOSITION AND OUTPUT
Keep every panel independently crop-safe and faces large enough for identity
review. Repeat rail line, hostel key, bench edge, island path, bedroom doorway
and notebook rule as match-cut geometry. Generate exactly one eight-panel planning
board. No animation, narration, music, captions, app UI, logo or product-outcome
claim. Use it to lock humane characters and book illustrations; do not expand
the hero-film runtime from this board.
