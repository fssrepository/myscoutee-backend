# Tone and irony ledger

## Governing editorial contract

The external AI critiques and the Founder's later corrections are direction,
not prose to quote or conclusions to insert. Apply them through these rules:

1. Put the reader in the room before naming the pattern. Show the masked eyes,
   silent desk, unanswered card, elevated leg, one-word answer, smile or empty
   chair. Do not replace the scene with a thesis.
2. Preserve the knowledge horizon. At each moment, the reader knows only what
   the Founder knew then; later documents may reframe the room but may not make
   the earlier self clairvoyant.
3. Do not prescribe emotion or judgment. The work may produce anger, doubt,
   recognition, discomfort or another response. Its task is to make the lived
   sequence available, not to tell the reader what it proves.
4. Let ordinary choices accumulate into the system. A person may remain human
   inside one role while the joined process behaves inhumanly. Avoid a single
   invented villain and avoid using that restraint to erase responsibility.
5. Distinguish event, memory, document and interpretation without stopping the
   story to explain each boundary. Put the slower legal and provenance limits
   in the notes.
6. Preserve contradiction. Beauty and exclusion, courtesy and hierarchy,
   competence and panic, humour and fear, home and exile may occupy the same
   image. Do not resolve one by deleting the other.
7. Translate Hungarian force through rhythm, image and black humour. A portable
   metaphor should let a non-Hungarian reader feel the direction of the line
   without literal profanity, local jargon or a lecture on national character.
8. Compress the film more aggressively than the book, but never change causal
   order, merge distinct people or turn an attributed perception into fact.
9. The Founder supplies lived reality: memory, consequence, purpose and moral
   responsibility. AI supplies learned reality: patterns, structures,
   alternatives, continuity and speed. The final meaning emerges through their
   correction of one another, not through AI replacing the witness.

The practical test for every passage is simple: can a reader imagine being in
that room without being instructed what to think about it?

## Translation rule

Hungarian anger is not flattened into a repeated English obscenity. Preserve
who is speaking, whom the line passes, its rhythm and the concrete image behind
it. Prefer a portable image an ordinary reader can see at once: it should work
without native slang, legal vocabulary or prior knowledge of Hungarian speech.
When a literal translation would sound merely crude or nonsensical, signal that
the Hungarian was rougher and let the surrounding black humour carry its force.
Never change the direction of an action or invent a target in order to improve
the line.

This ledger separates recurring authored language from remembered reactions and
later literary compression. It exists so that black humour can remain part of
the memoir without being mistaken for an official finding, a literal act or a
claim that another person agreed with the Founder.

## Editorial rule

The recurring tone can be summarized as follows:

> The frightening part was the absurdity; the humour was how the person stayed
> visible inside it.

The narrative turn underneath that tone is equally important: the accusation
is frightening first, but the deeper fear arrives when institutional language
keeps coercive force after losing credibility. This is the low-trust thesis of
the memoir, not a claim that official acts have no legal effect.

This is an editorial synthesis, not a quotation. The public book may use the
two short contemporaneous formulations above with attribution to the Founder's
own submissions. The film should communicate the same pattern through timing,
expressions and impossible administrative geometry rather than readable insults.

## What the humour must not imply

- A smile does not prove that an officer or judge agreed with the Founder.
- The difference between the contemporary word *smile* and the later memory of
  a laugh must remain visible in the evidence note.
- The impossible-position email is self-directed parody, not a literal job
  application, confession or threat.
- Contacting German police does not show that a foreign authority accepted the
  case, had jurisdiction or endorsed the Founder's account.
- The dental complaint and next-day search form a documented chronology, not
  proof that one caused the other.
- The remembered courtroom phrases do not establish the judge's private intent.
- An expression read by the Founder does not reveal a judge's inner conclusion.
- “Conscientiously defended the system failure” is the Founder's later ironic
  answer, not judicial language.
- Sarcastically firing an institution does not transfer authority.
- The leg image does not describe an attempted or desired physical assault.
- The profane “Go on, then. Fine me” rendering remains memoir-layer until the
  exact scanned filing is isolated. It records collapsed trust, not a request
  to punish a real relative or an excuse to diagnose the speaker.
- The KATA/Hulk/reaper material is self-directed political black comedy, not an
  instruction, plan or depiction of violence against a real person.
- Later political change does not convert the Founder's earlier interpretation
  into a legal or clinical finding.
- The two forensic experts must not be merged into one voice, and an attributed
  life-imprisonment remark must not be presented as a sentence or formal advice.
- The social inquiry records circumstances; an unheated, untidy flat and no car
  do not prove incapacity, neglect or misery.
- Repetition across the Founder's own submissions is evidence of recurring
  self-description, not independent corroboration.
