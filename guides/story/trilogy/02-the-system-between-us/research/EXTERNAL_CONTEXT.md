Fidesz-led government:

- https://apnews.com/article/6207293383e991b96c64e94ef425f10c

programme presented by the new majority as restoring rule-of-law democracy:

## The Venice Commission crossing

Sulyok asked the Venice Commission to assess the constitutional conflict over
his proposed removal. The Sándor Palace later published questions dated 29 May
and supplemented on 4 June 2026:

- https://www.sandorpalota.hu/sites/default/files/2026-06/Questions%20adressed%20to%20the%20Venice%20Commission_2026_07_06.pdf

On 5 June, the Founder separately emailed the Venice Commission. The local
source copy argues that his own KATA/NAV/criminal-procedure history illustrated
why Sulyok's institutional record should be examined. The message is evidence
that it was sent; it is not evidence that the Commission accepted its factual or
legal conclusions.

The Commission's official account says its President and delegation visited
Budapest on 2 July and met Sulyok, the Prime Minister, several ministers and the
Speaker of Parliament:

- https://www.coe.int/en/web/venice-commission/-/venice-commission-president-concludes-visit-to-hungary

Telex reported the Prime Minister's statement that the delegation had voiced no
criticism of the proposed amendment, while later press reporting said the
Commission declined to comment publicly as Parliament acted:

- https://telex.hu/english/2026/07/02/venice-commission-delegation-met-with-both-magyar-and-sulyok-pm-says-no-criticism-of-constitutional-amendment-to-remove-sulyok
- https://elpais.com/internacional/2026-07-13/el-parlamento-de-hungria-aprueba-la-reforma-constitucional-que-destituye-al-presidente-orbanista.html

Safe narrative: the Commission encountered an evident constitutional conflict
and remained publicly cautious while events moved faster than a final opinion.
Unsafe narrative without new evidence: the Founder's letter caused the
Commission to retreat or determined the President's removal.

## The Constitutional Court split and later personnel change

Sulyok also asked the Constitutional Court for constitutional interpretation.
On 19 June, seven of its fifteen members declared personal/direct involvement
and recused themselves. The full sitting required at least ten members, so the
matter was removed from the agenda for lack of quorum:

- https://telex.hu/belfold/2026/06/19/alkotmanybirosag-sulyok-tamas-kizaras-eltavolitas
- https://hvg.hu/itthon/20260619_sulyok-tamas-beadvany-alkotmanybirosag-ules-alkotmanybiro-kizaras

This is the precise meaning behind the visual phrase “the Court almost divided
in half.” The judges did not leave the Court at that point; they withdrew from
that case.

A separate part of the seventeenth amendment restored a mandatory retirement
age of seventy. Four of the fifteen sitting judges, including the Court's
President, were due to leave on 1 September 2026. The government's account also
reduced future terms and changed leadership-selection rules:

- https://cms.law/en/hun/legal-updates/hungary-s-parliament-passes-amendment-of-the-constitution
- https://kormany.hu/hirek/megszavazta-az-alaptorveny-17-modositasat-az-orszaggyules

## Institutions named during the transition

Public reporting records that the Prime Minister called for the resignation or
replacement of multiple long-mandate office-holders, including the President,
Prosecutor General, heads of the Constitutional Court, Curia and National Office
for the Judiciary, State Audit Office, Competition Authority and Media
Authority:

- https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/06/01/hungarian-government-to-amend-constitution-to-allow-removal-of-president
- https://telex.hu/english/2026/06/02/a-two-thirds-majority-can-do-anything-they-can-remove-the-president-through-creative-legal-maneuvering
- https://apnews.com/article/6207293383e991b96c64e94ef425f10c

By this research snapshot, the President's mandate had ended, public-media
structures and leaders had been changed, and constitutional/judicial personnel
rules were in motion. Other offices followed separate legal and political
routes. The book should not flatten them into “everyone was removed” or imply
that a public attack itself proves wrongdoing.

## Foreign-currency loans

The Court of Justice of the European Union's 2025 judgment in Case C-630/23
`AxFina` and the Hungarian Curia's Uniformity Decision 10/2025 JPE show that the
legal consequences of invalid foreign-currency-denominated contracts continued
to require high-level judicial interpretation:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A62023CJ0630
- https://kuria-birosag.hu/en/uniformity-decisions/operative-part-uniformity-decision-no-102025-jpe

This supports the bounded statement that the social and legal aftermath was not
simply over. It does not support a claim that every contract was invalid or
every case remained open.

## Migration and the beauty/exit contrast

Hungarian Central Statistical Office material acknowledges measurement limits
in emigration data and records years in which outward movement exceeded return:

- https://www.ksh.hu/s/publications/vital-events-2021/index.html
- https://www.ksh.hu/docs/eng/seemig/outputs/SEEMIGHistoricalAnalysisHungary.pdf

A 2015 report described contemporary KSH departures and contemporary estimates
of Hungarians working abroad:

- https://time.com/3945480/hungary-immigration-come-home-young-person-allowance/

The book should say that large numbers left and that many personal reasons
overlapped. It must not claim that everyone fled, that all emigrants were
foreign-currency borrowers, or that Budapest's beauty made departure irrational.
