*Public-Interest Action* are related, but they are not the same document.

The later document is five pages long and organizes the Founder's argument into

## Charge and expert material

The reviewed package includes:

- a scanned indictment/charge document;
- a summons/defence-related document;
- a scanned forensic psychiatric opinion signed by two experts.

The expert report records an examination on 30 November 2023 and a signed
opinion in January 2024. It describes the Founder as calm, cooperative,
oriented and cognitively intact, then gives a contested personality-disorder
opinion and a conclusion about moderately limited responsibility. The book must
state both parts and must not convert a forensic opinion into the narrator's
identity or claim that later political events clinically disprove it.

The report itself recounts substantial parts of the Founder's tax/accounting,
surgery and restricted-mobility explanation. The publication may therefore say
that he contests the weight or interpretation given to those circumstances; it
must not say the experts were wholly unaware of them. A later defence speech
attributes several oral remarks to “forensic experts,” including “You will
certainly receive life imprisonment for this.” That speech documents the
Founder's later written account of the remark, not a court sentence or an
independently verified transcript. The same source does not allocate every
remark reliably between the two experts.

The clemency request also records that its social inquiry was made without prior
notice. Current Hungarian procedure identifies the person preparing such an
inquiry in an ordinary pre-judgment clemency matter as a probation officer. The
private visit details remain memoir-layer until the inquiry itself is isolated.

## Audio

The bounded root review set contains thirteen WAV files totalling approximately
94.5 minutes. Most appear to be tax-administration calls from July 2022 to May
2023; two later recordings are dated November 2024 and April 2025. The expanded
export contains 32 WAV paths, including repeated/exported copies; that path count
must not be mistaken for 32 distinct conversations.

The defence speech refers to the audio material and records the Founder's later
account of several important exchanges. The most consequential calls or remarks
in that account are not present as surviving recordings. The available sound may
establish that surrounding calls occurred and may preserve tone or fragments of
the sequence, but it is only indirect support for what happened in the absent
key exchanges.

Current rule:

- do not upload these recordings to a cloud transcription service without
  explicit authorization;
- transcribe locally where possible;
- even after transcription, do not treat an absent key call as directly proved
  by a neighbouring recording;
- any remembered official remark from an absent call is labelled **attributed
  account supported indirectly by later authored material**, not a verified
  quotation;
- a call absent from the surviving recordings cannot be reconstructed as
  verbatim dialogue.
