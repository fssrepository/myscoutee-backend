# Long-term work exclusion — comparison boundary

This note gives Part II a public comparison point for the Founder's account of
spending roughly nine of eleven years outside conventional employment. It does
not convert that memoir statement into an official labour-market statistic or
a diagnosis.

## Statistical boundary

Eurostat defines long-term unemployment as unemployment lasting at least
twelve months and very long-term unemployment as unemployment lasting at least
twenty-four months. Its unemployment measure also requires active job seeking
and availability for work. A person outside the labour force is a different
statistical category.

The Founder's eleven-year sequence mixes materially different states:

- periods of unemployment and active job search;
- deliberate withdrawal supported by foreign-earned savings;
- independent product and research work without ordinary employment;
- a Hungarian multinational role;
- cross-border contract work;
- the later proceeding and renewed exclusion from ordinary work.

The book may therefore say **roughly nine of eleven years outside conventional
employment**. It must not label all nine years continuous unemployment in the
Eurostat sense unless the underlying job-search record establishes that.

## Human comparison

Research does not support a simple multiplier such as “nine years is nine times
more damaging than one year.” It does support the more restrained context that
unemployment and re-employment can affect mental health, that duration matters,
and that the relationship runs in both directions. A recent longitudinal
systematic review found higher symptom levels among unemployed adults and lower
symptom levels after re-employment, while rating the certainty of the evidence
as low. Earlier longitudinal studies likewise associate longer unemployment
histories with later depressive symptoms, but do not justify diagnosing an
individual memoir subject.

The relevant Part II meaning is therefore resilience under prolonged
uncertainty, not immunity from it. Planning, savings and continued creative work
made survival possible. Productivity does not prove health, innocence or the
correctness of every project; it shows what the Founder continued to do while
ordinary institutional belonging repeatedly failed.

## Approved story formulation

> Across the eleven years after I left London, I spent roughly nine outside
> conventional employment. That is not nine continuous years of official
> unemployment. Some were spent searching, some surviving on reserves, some
> travelling, and some building work no employer had commissioned. The usual
> statistical language calls twelve months long-term and twenty-four months
> very long-term. It has no equally simple name for a life that keeps working
> without being allowed to feel securely inside work.
