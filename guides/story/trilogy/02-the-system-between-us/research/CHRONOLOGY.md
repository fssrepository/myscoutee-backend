## Universal-pattern thesis

The evidence supports a narrative question larger than one biography:

```text
distorted work incentives
        ↓
legal route chosen under constraint
        ↓
sudden rule change and short adaptation window
        ↓
accountant, taxpayer and administrator each hold a partial view
        ↓
delegated action without direct legibility
        ↓
errors, corrections and delayed notice
        ↓
distress becomes the most visible fact
        ↓
the person is interpreted before the system is reconstructed
```

The book argues that any citizen could face a comparable pattern, not that
every citizen would write the same words or receive the same legal response.
The Founder's choices remain part of the story; the system context remains part
of it too.

The temporal proximity of the two Venice Commission submissions is narratively
powerful but does not prove influence. The public version must not say the
Commission changed position because of the Founder's email unless the Commission
or another reliable record establishes that link.

## Trilogy boundary

Part II ends with the institutional and personal outcome still open. Part III
owns the final hearing result, any later remedy or consequence, the settled
meaning of the new institutional order, and the final relationship between the
Founder, his country and MyScoutee.
