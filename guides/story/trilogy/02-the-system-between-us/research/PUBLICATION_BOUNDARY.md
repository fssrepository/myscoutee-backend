# Publication and privacy boundary

Part II concerns an active or recently active legal matter, identifiable public
institutions and private third parties. It therefore requires a stricter public
boundary than Part I.

## Safe public layers

The public memoir may use:

- the Founder's own work, tax and procedural chronology;
- public laws, public demonstrations, published decisions and official public
  statements;
- the existence and high-level outcome of filings addressed to the Founder;
- the Founder's own emotions, humour, mistakes, interpretations and policy
  proposals;
- anonymized memories that explain the social pattern without identifying a
  private person.

## Attribute, do not declare

Use formulations such as:

- “The filing records my recollection that…”
- “I understood the call as…”
- “The prosecution characterizes the messages as…”
- “The forensic report concluded…, a conclusion I contest.”
- “The authority's letter states…”
- “The new government described its programme as…”

Do not silently turn allegations into narrator-independent fact.

## Withhold or generalize

Do not publish without a separate necessity review:

- names of accountants, lawyers, police officers, judges, clerks, experts,
  health workers, former colleagues or family members;
- private addresses, identifiers, signatures, phone numbers, account numbers,
  portal IDs or case-document images containing them;
- raw medical details beyond a mobility-limiting operation and rehabilitation;
- sexual, family or clinical details about third parties;
- raw threatening language where paraphrase is sufficient to explain the case;
- security-sensitive speculation about credentials or state systems;
- private audio or a speaker's voice.

## The humour rule

The Founder often used humour under pressure. A police officer, clerk or judge
smiling may be shown only as a human reaction remembered by the Founder. It may
not be described as ridicule, bias, agreement or proof of innocence unless the
record actually establishes that meaning.

Humour should reveal the absurd situation:

> A room can briefly recognize the joke even when the procedure cannot
> recognize the context.

## Legal posture

The book is not a defence submission, judgment, diagnosis or legal opinion.
It must not:

- predict the result of the pending proceeding;
- present a later election or reform as proof that an earlier charge was
  invalid;
- equate a disputed forensic diagnosis with malicious fabrication;
- accuse a private person of crime;
- state that one institutional failure caused every later event without showing
  the intervening choices and uncertainties.

## Foreign-currency-loan memory

The London-household story is anonymized. It may say that one Hungarian
housemate remained abroad while trying to service a foreign-currency mortgage
and other debts, and that prolonged separation damaged his family. Do not name
his employer, relatives, bank, property, town or precise finances. His story is
one remembered life, not a statistic or a diagnosis of all Hungarian migration.

## Release gate

Before a public PDF or film is produced, obtain a focused review of:

- criminal-procedure wording;
- defamation/privacy exposure;
- clinical wording;
- quotations against local audio and original documents;
- current political and judicial facts as of the release date.
