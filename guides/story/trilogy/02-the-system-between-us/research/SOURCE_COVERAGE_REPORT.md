# Part II source-coverage report

Audit date: **9 August 2026**
Private source root: `/home/raxim/Downloads/targyalas/`  
Expanded backup: `/home/raxim/Downloads/targyalas/backup/`

This report defines what “reviewed” means for Part II. It prevents access to a
large archive from being mistaken for a page-by-page merits review of every
file.

## Executive result

The bounded root package was reviewed as the primary narrative set. The
expanded backup was structurally inventoried, content-hash deduplicated and
searched for named unresolved questions. High-value primary records were then
isolated and checked. The entire 4.35 GB archive was **not** read linearly and
the 4,573 PDF paths were **not** all reviewed page by page.

The resulting book is therefore based on a controlled source hierarchy:

1. issued or filed primary records for exact procedural facts;
2. the Founder's dated submissions for his contemporaneous account;
3. later memoir for lived experience and remembered reactions;
4. independent public sources for law and political context;
5. explicit uncertainty where those layers do not reconcile.

## Control rule for future revisions

New factual detail enters the book only through a named question and a recorded
source promotion in [REVIEW_LEDGER.md](REVIEW_LEDGER.md). Large-volume searching
may discover a candidate; it does not promote that candidate to documentary
fact. The editable draft can evolve, while every published edition keeps its
own checksum, source boundary and date-specific uncertainty record.
