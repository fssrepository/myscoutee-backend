## Tier 3 — corroboration and scene texture

- call lists and locally transcribed audio;
- portal screenshots and status histories;
- email chains with the accountant;
- authored jokes, sarcasm and remarks later quoted in submissions;
- financial-position questions and government-bond references;
- the London foreign-currency-loan echo from Part I.

Tier 3 can make prose vivid. It cannot silently override Tier 0 or Tier 1.

## Tier 4 — private or bulk material

- mobile and laptop backups;
- raw medical images;
- full account records;
- third-party contact data;
- repeated police/prosecution export bundles;
- unrelated dental and private disputes;
- generated images already present inside the source archive.

Tier 4 is not a default research pool. It is opened only for a named unresolved
question, locally and minimally.
