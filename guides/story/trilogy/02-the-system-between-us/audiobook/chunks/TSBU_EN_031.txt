Private recruitment correspondence records an intended January 2021 start and an exit email dated 28 January. The book does not determine the purpose or independence of the internal review, make a legal finding about the manager or bank, or claim that a later unresolved proceeding has already produced a criminal-record outcome. His indifference to the bank dismissal describes his burned-out internal state, not the objective seriousness of the employment decision.

The government account and the later two-factor change.

The “government account” in the narrative was Hungary's Citizen Portal identification service. In 2022, the relevant login was based on a username and password. From 16 January 2025, access to the same public-service surfaces required either the two-factor Citizen Portal Plus method or the Digital Citizenship application. This later security change does not rewrite the 2022 scene.
