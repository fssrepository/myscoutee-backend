Official guidance for accountants describes using another person's Citizen Portal identity instead of acting as an authorized representative as a bad, legally problematic practice. It points to registered authorization and the Hungarian tax administration's (NAV's) representation form as the lawful alternative. That is an identity boundary; it does not, by itself, resolve the separate visibility problem in the Founder's record: who received each submission result and notice, and whether both sides could see the same chain.

Citizen Portal Plus installs a secret into an authenticator through a setup quick-response (QR) code and then generates time-limited login codes from that secret. It follows technically that copying the setup QR to another device can duplicate the second factor. That inference explains why photographing a setup code may be used as a workaround, but it is neither secure delegation nor an officially endorsed accounting practice. Two-factor authentication protects a login; it does not turn shared identity into granular authorization.

Clemency social inquiry.

The Founder's application reached a clemency institution already transformed by the 2024 presidential-pardon scandal. The President resigned after the pardon became public. The former justice minister whose countersignature had made the decision effective withdrew from public life. The next President subsequently described clemency as an exceptional power and promised greater transparency.

Official presidential statistics record four grants among 689 decided cases in 2025 and no grants among 314 decided cases in the first half of 2026. The Founder's private record dates his refusal to 30 April 2026. The figures establish the rarity of clemency in this period; they do not establish the unrecorded reasoning behind an individual decision.

A signed private filing dated 19 May 2026 records that the Founder had nearly withdrawn the application in November 2025 because he did not consider the incumbent President suitable to decide it. The same filing asks that revised papers be held by the incoming Justice Minister until the presidency changed, after which the Founder intended to submit the request again. The public narrative leaves the minister unnamed; the filing supplies the role and date.

Hungarian procedural rules identify the person preparing a social inquiry for an ordinary procedural-clemency request as a probation officer. The inquiry may describe personal, family, health, housing, employment, income and property circumstances. It is evidence assembled for the clemency file; the probation officer does not decide the request or sentence.
