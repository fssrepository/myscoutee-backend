Strasbourg means the European Court of Human Rights, an institution of the Council of Europe rather than the European Union. Its official guidance treats the Court as a safety net after available domestic remedies have been exhausted; the current general time limit is four months from the final domestic decision. The memoir's minefield image is an account of how that principle felt inside this proceeding, not legal advice that the domestic route is already exhausted or that an application would be admissible.

The primary record.

This edition was reconstructed from a private source inventory containing tax and portal evidence, authored submissions, the charge, a forensic report, correspondence, product screenshots and a bounded set of private recordings.

The surviving recordings contain little of the decisive sequence. Their value is atmospheric: the calls they preserve are ordinary and non-threatening, not the menacing scene that later institutional language might suggest. The key exchanges survive through the defence speech, later submissions and the events that followed.

The four direct remarks in Chapter 6 are close English translations of the Hungarian wording preserved in the signed defence speech. The decisive calls do not survive in the reviewed recording set, so the book attributes the words to that later written record rather than presenting them as an independently verified transcript.

The Founder also records that he made an undisclosed audio recording when he collected the investigation material alone around 12 November 2024. A private recording dated 12 November remains in the reviewed source set. This edition does not quote or publish it and does not decide its admissibility or lawfulness. Its narrative use is narrower: it shows how far trust in a shared institutional account had already collapsed.

The public-interest material contains two related but distinct source lines. The first is the constructive system proposal of 1 December 2022 described in Chapter 7. It begins the design line: direct notice, visible delegated action, granular authority, an auditable correction chain, health-crisis handling and safer tax transitions.

The second is a five-page authored document dated 30 October 2025 and titled Public-Interest Action. Its cover deliberately reverses the ordinary positions: the Hungarian State is placed in the position of the accused and the Founder in the position representing the accusation. It organizes the argument under five headings: constitutional injury, data-protection injury, public-portal failures, criminal procedure and moral redress. Its final proposal imagines a KATA film fund whose cultural proceeds would support ecological restoration.

The reviewed root PDF is marked version 8; a separately signed copy is marked version 5. Their wording differs in places. Both are authored advocacy. The title alone does not establish that a court accepted the document under that procedural classification or decided its merits. The later European petition and the wider GDPR/taxation petition are separate source families again.

The raw package is not published with the book because it contains identifiers, private third-party data, medical-adjacent material and unresolved allegations.

Several black-comedy details have a firmer documentary layer than their tone might suggest. A 4 January 2024 email to the police career office attaches an English CV and describes the investigation as an impossible position whose third round has produced no feedback. An authored English submission dated 17 April records outreach to German police and German/Belgian press. A clemency submission records a further police report about the interrupted orthodontic treatment on the day before the home search. These records support the dates and the Founder's own language. They do not prove that the dental report caused the search, that a foreign authority assumed jurisdiction or that any officer accepted the satire's interpretation.

A 7 April 2024 submission records the reaction when the Founder raised the password-sharing problem and preserves the judgment that he had done something stupid. The scene in Chapter 11 gives the lived exchange; the filing supplies its documentary trace.
