Section 44 of the Criminal Procedure Act makes participation by defence counsel mandatory where the offence carries imprisonment of five years or more. A lawyer, not an AI system, must fill that legal role. The memoir's Trojan-horse image records the Founder's experience of a compulsory but discontinuous relationship; it is not a finding that appointed counsel colluded with the judge, breached a professional duty or represented the institution instead of the accused.

The discontinuity is not based only on a later impression.
