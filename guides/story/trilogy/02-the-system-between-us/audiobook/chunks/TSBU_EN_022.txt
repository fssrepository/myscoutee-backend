Sometimes the border is only the visible part of an escape. We also run from the person we became while adapting to a place: the hunger we hid, the fear we normalized, the silence that kept a room workable. A new country can interrupt that version of us, but it cannot automatically remove it. In another world we may recognize ourselves more clearly. Looking back, we can ask whether home—and we ourselves—can do better.

From the Danube embankment, none of this looked like the edge of Europe. Trams crossed the river; Parliament held its familiar place in the light; elections, courts and offices carried the vocabulary of democracy. The acts inside my story remained ordinary enough for each participant to continue. The question was left between the beauty and the open route out: how long can a beloved home ask a person to trade dignity for staying?

18. The Breaking Point Is Not the Beginning.

The file can be made to begin with one charged line because that line is short. It is also the version that makes everything before it disappear.

The public meaning begins earlier.

Anyone can delegate a task and remain responsible for it. Anyone can miss a notice routed to a representative. Anyone can encounter a law that changes faster than their professional support can adapt. Anyone can be physically vulnerable when an institution expects ordinary performance. Anyone can have their worst response become more legible than the path that produced it.

One line is easier to print than years of exclusion, lawful effort, repair and endurance. Once that happens, cause and effect can change places. Pressure becomes personality. The attempt to recover missing context becomes evidence of obsession. Survival outside ordinary work becomes another condition to explain.

That reversal is what makes the case public in meaning even while it remains mine in fact. The danger is not that everyone would write my sentence. The danger is that a low-trust system can first exclude a capable person, then use his breaking point to explain away the system that excluded him.

The system was not an independent creature hovering above the country. People made it each morning. A legislator accepted speed. A professional protected a professional habit. An office defended the edge of its competence. A lawyer ran out of time. An expert preferred a total explanation. A judge narrowed the next question. Each choice could look ordinary from inside one role. Placed in sequence, the ordinary choices became the machine.

That does not make every participant cruel, or every Hungarian participant the same. It removes the comforting fiction that a broken system exists without human maintenance. No single person designed the entire corridor. Responsibility moved away from every desk; accountability moved toward the person with nowhere further to send it.

Part Six — A Private Case Enters Public Time.

19. The Streets Begin to Speak.

I did not join the KATA protest in 2022. Other citizens were already teaching the city a different language: teachers, students and parents formed chains; the presidential-pardon scandal filled public spaces; judges and court employees later rallied for judicial independence and the freedom to speak.

By then emergency had become an ordinary political grammar. During COVID-19, a curfew decided when a person could cross his own street. As the pandemic order receded, the war next door supplied another state of danger. In 2025, the capital's mayor announced Pride as a city event after the police had prohibited the march. He was questioned, then charged. After the election and a European court judgment, the charge was dropped. The paving stones had not moved. The law around the people on them had.

These movements did not share one programme or know my case. Their cumulative effect was to make institutional operation a public experience.

My own path was quieter and stranger. I moved through electronic forms.

I asked the Hungarian office charged with protecting fundamental rights to look at the police process, the failures of representation and the password-sharing trap. Its answer mapped the limits of its own jurisdiction: it could not review how police and prosecutors weighed the evidence, could not intervene in a pending court process, and directed parts of the complaint back toward the same legal routes already surrounding me. A later answer said that further submissions repeating the issue could be placed in the archive without another reply. An office named for fundamental rights had given me a careful map of the doors it could not open.

A petition to the European Parliament was declared admissible in January 2025. The European Commission response described the data-protection framework and pointed enforcement back toward national authorities and courts. The private file had entered a legitimate public channel, only to be routed back toward the same national architecture.
