A Note to the Reader.

This is a memoir of an unresolved period.

Part I ended with an invitation. I had returned from London, travelled, looked for a partner, and spent the independence I had built on a product that might help people reach one another more humanely. The last pages moved through a Hungarian multinational role, COVID-19, and a cross-border remote opportunity. The sentence at the threshold was simple: what followed belongs to Part II.

What followed survives as contracts, portal records, calls, police papers, petitions and public events. It also survives as rooms: the bedroom where my leg was raised above me, the ordinary police office, the smaller room where counsel asked for time, the C-shaped court table, and the desk where a cursor in an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant kept blinking beside the possibility of prison.

Public history usually arrives as laws, elections, resignations and dates. A person receives it differently. A law becomes a cancelled contract. A digital design becomes a password handed across a desk. An institutional failure becomes a night without sleep, then a sentence, then a knock at the door. This book follows that smaller route through the larger period. The personal story does not replace the history. It is where the history becomes measurable in a human life.

The central absurdity can be stated without legal vocabulary. A sudden law put livelihoods under threat. The accountant, the tax administration and I each passed responsibility toward the next point in a chain whose legal burden never left me. When I finally saw the whole failure, I understood in about a week what the professional process had not resolved over several months. I corrected the return, paid the tax and ended the failed representation. The return had been corrected and the tax liability resolved. Roughly a year later, eight days after the police search, the forensic process examined the highly educated professional who had repaired the failure and turned him into the subject of a label of pathological mental illness.

After two decades of exclusion, adaptation and repeated attempts to build a lawful life in my own country, one sentence broke loose under accumulated pressure. I corrected it immediately and kept writing. Its wording remains part of the record. So does the repaired return, the paid tax, the ended representation, the written administrative history and the request for peace around surgery. The state later allowed the sentence and later messages to travel farther than those repairs. A system cannot preserve the breaking point, discard the path around it and then use the surviving fragment to prove that the person had always been the problem.

Nearly four years after the work stopped, and nearly three years into the criminal proceeding, the case had not produced a first-instance judgment, let alone reached an appeal. Minutes of language had expanded across years of life. A sentence born inside one badly assembled sequence was already consuming years; if every remaining stage moved at the same pace, the process itself could take a decade before anyone reached its end.

The proceeding has not reached the ending reserved for Part III. The factual record, attribution limits and source references appear at the back, where they can protect accuracy without interrupting every scene.

All character and scene illustrations in this book are AI-generated visual interpretations. They do not depict the actual participants.

That matters because the pattern is not mine alone. Another person might never write my sentences and could still enter the same corridor.

The corridor was not evidence of a defective nation, and not every Hungarian office or official behaved alike. It belonged to a low-trust culture many of us learn to navigate before anybody names it. The written rule points one way. The workable custom points another. Authority travels upward, responsibility travels downward, and the citizen is expected to know both routes without ever confusing them in public.

You smell that culture before you can define it. Everybody in the room knows a process is broken, yet each person protects the narrow role that lets the failure become somebody else's problem. A smile acknowledges the absurdity and the procedure continues. A password is personal when responsibility is being assigned and shareable when work needs to be done. The correction disappears. The outburst remains.
