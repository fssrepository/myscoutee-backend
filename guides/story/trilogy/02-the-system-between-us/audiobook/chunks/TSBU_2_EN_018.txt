The room could recognize fear, ingenuity and work as moments of almost comic incongruity while the procedure continued treating the same person as a risk to be measured. The smile made the institution human for a second. It also made the situation more surreal, because recognition changed nothing.

In that room, the accounting failure returned in miniature. When I described what I believed the accountant had done, the judge warned me not to defame anyone. She asked why I had not asked the accountant. From the much longer defence speech, she selected my assertion that the accountant had entered what I called random figures in my return—figures I later tried to correct.

The same proceeding carried a forensic description of my personality that I contested and that could follow me far beyond the tax return. I was being told to protect another person's reputation while defending myself from that description. The sharp phrase was easy to examine. The two-page return, the repeated attempts, the outside help, the missed messages and the filing I said I had not known about receded behind it.

The hearing ended on two different scales. I said that European institutions had already warned the government about unstable, rapidly changing lawmaking, and that my case showed the size of the damage such lawmaking could create in one life. The judge closed by saying that we would continue with my financial position.

I had lifted the lens toward legislation and institutional design; the room lowered it to my work, debt, home, belongings and savings. The system question became an asset question before the system question had been answered.

By the end, something in the judge's expression had loosened. The chain had finally revealed a person reached by failure rather than only a person who had failed. The procedural sentence that followed remained unchanged: next came my finances and the amount of the financial consequence.

The two truths occupied the same moment. A human being may have begun to understand. The role still moved the machinery toward the resources I had built abroad and the home my parents had helped provide. It felt as though the system were preparing to claim a return from an investment it had never made.

Part Five — Work Under the Shadow.

15. The Work the File Could Not Explain.

Prison remained in the room even when nobody said the word.

At first the possibility had weight. Over time it became another shadow I refused to organize my days around. I had already lived through decades of exclusion, five years of London austerity, long periods without employment, a solitary operation and the disappearance of ordinary institutional trust. The financial independence built from those years meant that another threat could not command my next morning. I did not stop understanding what prison could mean. I stopped giving the possibility ownership of my mind.

For much of the proceeding, I did not experience appointed defence as a stable person carrying the case with me.

Lawyers were formally present at different points. That is not the same as having one person continuously reconstruct the evidence, answer messages, challenge omissions and explain the route ahead. My submissions record long periods in which I believed I was effectively defending myself.

Conversational AI arrived inside that absence.

It did not originate my facts, position or judgment, but it was present from the first two-page defence. Later, AI helped expand that compressed argument into the roughly eight-page defence speech: separating chronology from allegation, connecting the missing records, and giving a structure to material that had grown beyond what one person could keep present at once.

I used it to translate, impose headings, compare concepts, reduce repetition, generate questions and find contradictions that a human reader could then check. It made it possible to move through volumes that had become impossible for one exhausted person to hold in working memory.

Legally, it was not my lawyer. It could misunderstand procedure, invent a citation or turn uncertainty into confidence. It held no licence and attended no hearing.

In practice, it became the only participant that stayed with the whole chain. I joked in a submission that perhaps the AI should be allowed to represent me. The joke met a legal wall. Because the charged offence carried a maximum of eight years, Hungarian procedure required a qualified defence lawyer. The official chair beside me therefore could not be empty even when the working relationship behind that chair felt absent.
