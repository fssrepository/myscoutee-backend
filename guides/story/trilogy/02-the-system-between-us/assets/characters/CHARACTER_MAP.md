## Identity rules

- Never swap a board identity for a more dramatic generated face.
- Never reintroduce an old rough-checkpoint Founder placeholder; use the locked Part I
  `[FOUNDER_REF]` now stored in this directory.
- Age the Founder only through subtle fatigue from 2021 to 2026; do not change
  facial proportions, hairline, coat, scarf or notebook.
- A smile or softened expression is a human reaction, never proof of agreement,
  guilt, innocence, bias or institutional position.
- The accountant, officer, expert and judge remain ordinary adults inside
  partial systems. Clothing, gender, age or office clutter never codes guilt or
  incompetence.
- Public figures are not characters. Use hands, silhouettes, institutional
  geometry and post-produced dates instead of generated likenesses.
- Any crowd must contain varied adult identities and must not reuse or clone the
  locked role characters.
- Hearing-room geography is fixed from the Founder's viewpoint: prosecutor on
  the left, clerk in the far-left corner, judge in the centre, court-appointed
  defence counsel on the right; the Founder sits before the C-shaped desk with
  a table microphone directly in front of him.
- The forensic examination contains two experts. Their identities, positions
  and remembered reactions remain distinct even though they sign one opinion.

## Platform mapping

In Flow, save the recurring crops as named Ingredients or Characters using the token
names without brackets. In OpenArt, save each crop as a Character reference and
keep the same token name. Attach only the characters used in one interval; extra
references can weaken identity control.

The complete board is useful for review and book layout. Video generation uses
the individual crops in this directory, not the whole board.
