## Truth layers

Every scene must identify which layer it belongs to:

- **D — documentary record:** date, filing, official decision, public law or
  verifiable public event;
- **M — memoir:** what the Founder remembers feeling, seeing or saying;
- **A — attributed account:** a remark or reaction recorded in one of the
  Founder's submissions but not yet independently verified;
- **C — context:** later public developments placed beside the personal story
  without claiming they prove the Founder's earlier legal interpretation.

The film may compress time but may not merge those layers into one claim.

## Production boundary

- build a slideshow or animatic;

After approval, the separate benchmark phase may generate only the selected
interval's exact first and last frames from the locked role and location
descriptions. Do not create disposable midpoints or book-only substitutes.
