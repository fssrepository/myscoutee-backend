# MyScoutee — The Story · Part II

## The System Between Us

Status: **English public edition v1.0.0 published 8 August 2026; editable
manuscript reopened for an unreleased source-led revision on 9 August. The
05:20 script, 38-interval storyboard and prompts are synchronized to the current
editable manuscript but remain unapproved until the reading pass is complete.**

Part II continues from the exact final threshold of Part I. The same founder who
crossed countries to find work and human connection now crosses a landscape of
tax rules, delegated access, notification gaps, official calls, medical
vulnerability, police procedure, expert interpretation, courtrooms and public
petitions.

The central conflict is not one bad actor. A sudden tax-law transition placed
three already tense participants inside a low-trust triangle:

1. the Founder, legally responsible but unable to see every delegated action;
2. an accountant working through fragmented procedures and uncertain rules;
3. tax-administration staff trying to explain a changing system after the fact.

The resulting error chain is the inciting incident. The deeper story is what
happens when every participant has a partial view, nobody trusts the next
participant, and the person asking for context becomes the object being
interpreted.

Humour remains essential. The Founder sometimes makes police officers, clerks
or a judge smile while the institutional roles around them remain adversarial.
Those moments do not trivialize the proceeding. They expose its central irony:
the people in the room can still recognize one another even while the system
between them cannot reconstruct the context that brought them there.

Part II is designed as both:

- an independent memoir/documentary story about 2021–2026; and
- a continuation of the product-origin trilogy, ending with a more demanding
  MyScoutee design question: how should a system preserve consent, context,
  direct notice, reciprocal agency and a humane route to correction?

Part II ends inside an institutional transition, not with a verdict. **Part III
owns the outcome**: the final procedural result, the settled consequences of the
new legal order, and what ultimately becomes of the Founder and MyScoutee.
