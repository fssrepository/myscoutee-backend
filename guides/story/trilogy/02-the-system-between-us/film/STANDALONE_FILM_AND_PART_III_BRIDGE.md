# Standalone Part II film and Part III bridge

## Part II completes one claim

The film has a complete internal transformation:

1. the Founder enters a legal route to keep working;
2. sudden change and low trust assemble an avoidable failure;
3. his worst response becomes more visible than its correction and context;
4. procedure turns the person into a file;
5. AI-assisted self-defence turns the file into public design questions;
6. national change shows that those questions were not isolated;
7. MyScoutee returns as a system that must itself be tested for context,
   consent, notification and correction.

The ending is therefore meaningful without a verdict:

> Test the systems between us before pressure tests them for us.

## Part III owns the outcome

Part II must not state or imply the final result of:

- the criminal proceeding and the hearing currently scheduled for 16 September
  2026;
- any new clemency, constitutional or international route;
- the recent new-justice-administration reply until its source is reviewed;
- the election of Hungary's next President;
- the final composition and behaviour of reformed institutions;
- later work, property, citizenship or health consequences;
- the product's real-community validation.

Part III may begin with the exact last four seconds of Part II held longer. The
open MyScoutee line becomes a court timeline, then either closes, branches or
breaks according to the verified outcome.

## No false cliffhanger

Do not use a prison door, gavel strike, guilty/not-guilty card or sensational
“to be continued.” The final condition is uncertainty, not manufactured danger.
The interim presidential chair and the Founder's scheduled hearing are enough.
