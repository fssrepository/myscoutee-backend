## 2. Logline

The Founder leaves Part I as a builder connecting strangers and enters a rare
COVID-era remote role serving a Brussels telecom programme from Budapest. A
sudden 2022 tax-law change places him, his accountant and the tax administration
inside a low-trust triangle in which each sees a different screen. Repeated
filings, missing context, physical vulnerability and a written outburst become
a criminal case. Without stable effective legal help, he spends three years
using AI to organize his own defence and transform complaint into institutional
design. As Hungarian streets, judges and finally an electorate force a wider
political transition, his file crosses paths with the President, the
Constitutional Court and the Venice Commission. The country changes systems
before his case reaches an ending.

## 3. The contrast that earns three minutes

The film alternates two scales:

| Personal scale | Public scale |
| --- | --- |
| one laptop | a law passed in a day |
| one delegated tax return | hundreds of thousands adapting |
| one post-operative body made visible in a portal | an administration maintaining ordinary deadlines |
| one disputed sentence | years of procedure |
| one seized phone and computer | a national digital state |
| one AI-assisted self-defence | courts, petitions and constitutional offices |
| one open hearing date | a country with an interim President |

The public scale never proves the private case. The private case makes the
public design problem emotionally legible.

## 4. Visual grammar

Carry forward Part I's painterly cinematic 2.5D world and exact Founder identity.
The recurring line changes function:

```text
network edge

→ invoice row
→ portal progress line
→ hospital monitor trace
→ police evidence tape
→ fingerprint ridge
→ Danube light and brown stone
→ C-shaped court line
→ forensic report curve

```

Palette arc:

- `00:00–00:24`: Part I navy, cognac amber and calm screen light;
- `00:24–01:20`: compressed graphite, tax-form ivory, warning red and sodium
  street amber;
- `01:20–02:08`: clinical cyan, police blue, Danube silver, brown institutional
  stone and forensic paper white;
- `02:08–02:40`: archive grey, cautious violet AI light, desaturated home warmth
  and widening civic gold;
- `02:40–03:00`: civic gold, constitutional red, interim empty-chair navy and

Do not use horror, riot spectacle, prison clichés, axes, flames, terrorism
iconography, diagnostic labels floating over a face or a comic-book villain.
Do not turn the film into a legal explainer. Statutes, citations and the
reference apparatus belong to the book's end matter. The picture carries rooms,
bodies, objects, lines, thresholds, expressions and consequences.

## 5. Character rules

### Founder

Use the exact approved Part I Founder identity, aged only by the passage from
2021 to 2026. Preserve the cognac notebook, modest Budapest flat, workstation
and restrained clothing. His humour appears through micro-expression and the
other person's brief smile, never exaggerated clowning.

### Accountant

One competent-looking, overworked adult, visually distinct from the Founder and
tax officer. Show competing messages and client load. Do not code gender, age,
clothing or office clutter as incompetence. No readable business name.

### Tax officer

One ordinary professional inside a call-centre/administrative environment. Do
not show corruption, conspiracy or malicious delight. The screen is partial;
the person is not omniscient.

### Police, experts, probation officer and judge

Show role, attention and occasional human reaction. A smile means only that a
moment of humour landed. Never animate sneering, mockery or predetermination.

The police sequence includes an ordinary interview room, a separate counsel
room and restrained photographic/fingerprint processing. The female officer who
guides the Founder's hand remains professional; his awareness of attraction is
one human glance, not flirtation or erotic framing.

The forensic examination contains two visibly distinct experts and one final
opinion. Their remembered oral reactions must not be collapsed into one person
or promoted into a judicial sentence. The clemency home visitor is a probation
officer preparing a social inquiry, not a judge or clemency commissioner.

### Public figures

Use documentary-shaped silhouettes, podium geometry, hands, institutional
rooms, exact post-added date cards and licensed/editorial source material where
available. Do not rely on a video model to invent accurate likenesses or quotes.

### Crowds

Hungarian public scenes must include varied adults across age, gender,
occupation, mobility and style. Do not render protesters as a homogeneous angry
mass or imply every protest shared the Founder's cause.

## 6. Irony and humour

The governing tonal rule:

> The situation is absurd; the people remain human.

Acceptable:

- a police officer trying not to smile at the Founder's dry answer;
- a judge's expression briefly softening;
- solitary bed rest and meticulous preparation exposing the curse of solitude;
- a clinical close photograph of the actual closed incision carrying an obscene
  verbal joke that remains unmistakably metaphorical;
- an ordinary moment of attraction during fingerprinting, without romance;
- a powerless citizen sarcastically firing an institution in prose;
- seven chairs sliding back from a fifteen-seat constitutional table followed
  by an eighth, with
  mathematical precision;
- an enormous archive compressing into one tiny input field;
- an AI cursor proposing a heading while a hearing notice waits beside it.

Not acceptable:

- slapstick arrest or surgery;
- a bedside carer or visitor during the solitary rehabilitation;
- a literal kick, dismissal, pasture or act of violence created from the
  Founder's written sarcasm;
- eroticized fingerprinting or an official exploiting attraction;
- merging two experts into one ominous authority figure;
- presenting the probation officer's remembered reaction as legal advice;
- a laughing court audience;
- caricatured accountant or official;
- presenting humour as proof that the charge is frivolous;
- triumph when an office-holder loses a position.

## 7. Evidence layers on screen

Use a small editable corner marker during internal frame review only:

- `D` document;
- `M` memoir;
- `A` attributed account;
- `C` external context.

Remove the marker from the public film, but preserve the mapping in the shot
manifest. Every displayed document must be recreated as an anonymized editable
plate; never feed raw case pages to a generative video model.

## 8. Audio architecture

Audio is assembled after picture timing is locked.

- one continuous English narrator, no lip sync;
- one continuous instrumental composition from `00:00.000` to `03:00.000`;
- no Flow/OpenArt clip-level music or generated dialogue;
- exact foley synchronized in post: keyboard, notification, paper, phone,
  crutch/step, door, evidence bag, crowd, chair movement and pen;
- carry a low 60–80 Hz urban/system pulse across every cut;
- reuse Part I's final harmonic note, turn it into an unresolved pulse, and
  return to the MyScoutee interval only in the last eight seconds;
- no silence at clip boundaries and no music restart per eight-second scene.

Score arc:

| Time | Musical direction |

## 9. Truth boundaries

- The Founder did not attend the KATA protest.
- The accountant, tax officer, police, two experts, probation officer and judge are not declared
  malicious or criminal.
- The most charged messages are paraphrased, not reproduced or visualized.
- The solitary rehabilitation, actual incision photograph and vulgar joke
  establish body, absence, protest and sarcasm; they do not depict physical
  action or intent.
- The report records surgery and accounting context; the Founder contests the
  interpretation and weight given to it, not the fact that it was mentioned.
- Courtroom remarks concerning defamation, the accountant and the defence
  speech remain the Founder's attributed account, not a finding of bias.
- The forensic opinion is shown as contested document content, not objective
  character identity.
- AI assisted self-defence; it did not provide licensed legal representation.
- Seven of fifteen constitutional judges initially recused themselves from
  Sulyok's matter and an eighth followed; they did not leave the Court in June.
- A separate rule scheduled four over-seventy judges to leave on 1 September.
- The Venice Commission's caution is not attributed to the Founder's email.
- The Constitutional Court letter records a premature route before a
  first-instance decision and does not decide the factual merits.
- The first departed judge, second challenged judge and separate deciding judge
  remain three distinct roles; the short film need not visualize all three.
- Questions about work, debt, home, belongings and savings do not establish that
  confiscation or a final financial penalty has been ordered.
- The 2026 election and institutional changes do not decide the criminal case.
- No new President is invented.
- Part III owns the final legal and personal result.
