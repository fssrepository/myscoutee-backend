# MyScoutee — something to do, people to share it with

**MiniMax H3 · two 15-second sources · final 30 seconds, 9:16.**

## Story and feeling

Six people have interests, energy and a free moment. Each has prepared for
something they would enjoy, with a brief hesitation because company is missing.
The opening carries longing and possibility. A backpack, bicycle, ball, guitar,
game box and camera make those wishes visible without separate dream sequences.

Mara chooses her priorities in MyScoutee. Mutual interest leads to a group and
a plan. Later, the same six share a simple outdoor game at a park table.
This is one example of an event; MyScoutee is not presented as a café-only or
board-game-only service. The opening shows the broader variety of activities.
It does not promise that all six different wishes happen in this one event.
