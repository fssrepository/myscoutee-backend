# English-first translation lock

The English manuscript is canonical. The Hungarian manuscript and Hungarian
PDF are translations; they do not introduce product claims, examples or source
interpretations that are absent from English.

When the English source changes:

1. update the English manuscript and claim review first;
2. translate the changed meaning into Hungarian without expanding it;
3. keep the same nine numbered chapters and final evidence boundary;
4. rebuild both editions and compare their page counts and extracted text;
5. update both caption languages from the locked English narration, not from
   an independently rewritten Hungarian script.
