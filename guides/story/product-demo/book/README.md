# A Match Is Not a Meeting

This is a deliberately short English-first product companion, not a general
dating manual. The canonical editable source is
[A_MATCH_IS_NOT_A_MEETING_EN.md](A_MATCH_IS_NOT_A_MEETING_EN.md). The Hungarian
[A_MATCH_NEM_TALALKOZAS_HU.md](A_MATCH_NEM_TALALKOZAS_HU.md) is its controlled
translation. Read [TRANSLATION_LOCK.md](TRANSLATION_LOCK.md) before changing the
Hungarian text.

The book continues the story publications as a marketing artifact: its cover,
type system, page colour, contents hierarchy, initials and ornament rhythm stay
in the same publication family, while the subject pivots to a standalone product
experience rather than a new biographical chapter.

Run `../scripts/build_mini_book.py` to rebuild both HTML editions, both draft and
published PDFs, checksums and publication records.

Two registered illustrations are mapped by `illustrations.json` and
`illustrations.hu.json`. Their stable files live under `assets/illustrations/`;
the second also supplies the cover's human product image.

The book flows continuously after its contents page: chapters are not forced
onto separate sheets. Small Hungarian-inspired, symmetric folk ornaments mark
selected chapter initials, the end of the contents page and the end of the
book; the cover is the only place where the MyScoutee logo is used.

The English source lets familiar friction emerge through one user's journey. It
does not read like a complaint ledger, insult users, attack named competitors or
interrupt the experience with internal product-evidence commentary.
