# Product-demo checkpoint generation briefs

These seven frames are original assets owned by this package. They were created
with the built-in image-generation workflow, then normalized to `720×1280` by
`scripts/build_brand_frames.py`. The resulting production package does not
reference a trilogy asset.

## Shared visual lock

Premium cinematic painterly 2.5D storybook illustration; expressive hand-drawn
mature-adult linework integrated into dimensional paper, painted wood, wool,
brass and graphite textures. Portrait `9:16`, central mobile-safe composition,
warm autumn lamplight, deep navy shadows. Exactly three women and three men in
the CP03–CP07 user group, all approximately 30–55. Founder John appears only in
CP01–CP02 and is not a group participant. The five CP02 demonstration portraits
are deliberately different from the later six-user group. No text, logo, app
UI, public rating, forced pair, child, duplicate identity, server,
administrator, operator or watermark.

recognizable problem without naming or mocking another service.
