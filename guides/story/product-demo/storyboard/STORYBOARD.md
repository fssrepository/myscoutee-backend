# 48-second product-reel storyboard

Every shot is one exact eight-second Google Flow / Veo 3.1 Lite first-and-last-
frame generation. `P00` and `F48` are deterministic post composites, not Flow
inputs.

| Shot | Time | Exact endpoints | Retention job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S01 | 00:00–00:08 | `PR_CP00 → PR_CP08` | frame-one brand; a match lacks time/place/plan; private intent supplies the first missing step |
| S02 | 00:08–00:16 | `PR_CP08 → PR_CP16` | private `1–10`, reciprocal/context/distance, six distinct candidates |
| S03 | 00:16–00:24 | `PR_CP16 → PR_CP24` | six separate acceptances converge on one shared activity proposal |
| S04 | 00:24–00:32 | `PR_CP24 → PR_CP32` | the accepted proposal becomes people, transport, place and supplies |
| S05 | 00:32–00:40 | `PR_CP32 → PR_CP40` | the plan becomes a real meeting before 00:37 |
| S06 | 00:40–00:48 | `PR_CP40 → PR_CP48` | shared context becomes a warmer next invitation; closing brand |

## P00 — first visible frame

Use the approved binary-choice image as the moving plate. Composite the original
MyScoutee mark plus editable `yScoutee` at the top and `MATCH ≠ MEETING` at the
bottom. Hold only long enough to read—approximately 1.6 seconds—then fade the
overlay while the Founder moves from phone choice to the desk model.

## S01 — the match still needs a path

The Founder receives a familiar online signal without sadness or blame, then
faces two sepia portrait cards with simple reject/accept symbols and a phone.
There is still no shared activity, time or place. The cards settle onto the desk
and the simple symbols disappear. Ten
small amber tokens, one portrait card, an aqua reciprocal token and a blank
activity card become visible. No named competitor,
generated interface text, mockery or public human rating.

## S02 — graded private intent to candidate group

One independent aqua return signal and restrained context cues join the
Founder's private priority. Four more portrait cards arrive. End on the exact
six separate adult windows. The group is only a candidate, not a promised
result.

## S03 — each person decides

The same six adults independently consider the same activity proposal, with
accept and decline equally available. Only after the last acceptance do all six
paths reach the central activity model. They remain physically separate here;
no pair dominates.

## S04 — the match becomes a plan

One contextual group conversation produces a chosen activity and exactly four
event-resource families: people, transport, place/accommodation and supplies.
The same six people remain visible around one shared practical plan.

## S05 — people actually meet

The symbolic plan becomes a real public room. The same six adults sit around one
shared creative/tabletop activity with equal space. Reach the meeting image by
00:33–00:34 and let it breathe through the observed 00:37 retention boundary.

## S06 — the next invitation is no longer cold

The same adults finish the shared activity and leave the same venue together.
Several conversations continue without forcing romantic pairs. The Founder
offers one blank tactile card for a future shared activity while the used table
remains visible inside. Fade in the exact mark and
`DESIGNED FOR THE MEETING.` during the final 2.5 seconds; end exactly on the
deterministic `PR_F48` composite over `PR_CP48`. No operator, administrator, server, deployment
or business-model explanation appears.
