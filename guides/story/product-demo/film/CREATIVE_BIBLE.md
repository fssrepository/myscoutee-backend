# Creative bible — Designed for the meeting

## One sentence

A familiar online signal still needs a path to a real room. MyScoutee makes that
path visible: private intent, a voluntarily accepted group, one practical plan,
the meeting and a warmer next invitation.

## Audience and retention rule

The primary viewers are tired but not cynical people struggling with online
dating. The user's observed Facebook Reel retention falls
away at approximately 00:37, so the film follows these locks:

- brand and problem are visible in frame one;
- the first product difference starts at 00:08;
- the group and separate accept/decline are clear by 00:24;
- planning is clear by 00:32;
- people physically meet from 00:32, before the observed drop;
- the final 8 seconds remain entirely with the people after the meeting; no
  operator, administrator, server or deployment explanation enters this reel.

## User-experience frame

Research about swipe apps, long-profile sites, matchmakers and singles events is
background only. The public film never lists, names, imitates or judges them. It
stays with one experience the viewer can recognize:

| Moment | Felt experience | MyScoutee step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a mutual signal appears | hopeful, but nothing is scheduled | private editable intention |
| several possibilities exist | no single opening must carry everything | balanced activity group |
| a group is proposed | personal agency remains essential | each person accepts or declines |
| people want to go | “sometime” needs owners and resources | one contextual chat and plan |
| the door opens | profiles become people with voices | real shared activity |
| the evening ends | a next message no longer starts cold | later invitation with common context |

## Tone

Direct, observant and generous. Never call users foolish. The mini-book may call
familiar internet attempts absurd, but the target is the product logic, not the
lonely person using it. No gender war, humiliation, dating-market statistics,
fake testimonials, lonely loser imagery or romantic guarantee.

## Visual language

Inherit the approved Part I painterly 2.5D Founder and six-person group. Start on
an all-or-nothing binary phone choice, turn it into private graded intent, then
let separate people become a voluntarily accepted group, a shared activity, a
real table and a warmer next invitation. Amber is private intent, aqua is
reciprocal/contextual evidence, coral is the candidate group.

Exact branding is a deterministic post layer. The logo is never generated by
Veo, recolored, redrawn, cropped, glowing or used as certification.
