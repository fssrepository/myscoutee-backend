A match is only a yes. Reaching a real meeting still needs people, timing and a place. And it still needs a shared plan.

MyScoutee starts differently: with a private, editable priority list. Then everyone arranges their own.

Across those private choices, mutual priorities can reveal a balanced group around one shared activity.

Once the group is formed, everyone can organize the practical parts: people, transport, place and supplies.

Then the plan becomes a meeting. Profiles gain voices. Strangers gain real context. Private priorities stay private.

The next invitation begins with shared experience, not another cold opening line. MyScoutee is designed for the meeting.
