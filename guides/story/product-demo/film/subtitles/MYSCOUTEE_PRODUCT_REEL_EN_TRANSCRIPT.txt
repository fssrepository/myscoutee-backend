You get a match. But it has no time, place or shared plan. MyScoutee builds that missing path.

Your interest becomes a private, editable one-to-ten priority, connected with mutual intent, distance and context.

A balanced group can form around one activity. Each person sees the proposal and accepts or declines for themselves.

Those who accept share one contextual chat, then coordinate people, transport, place and supplies.

Then you meet. Profiles gain voices, and strangers gain real context, while every priority stays private.

The next invitation begins with shared experience, not another cold opening line. MyScoutee is designed for the meeting.
