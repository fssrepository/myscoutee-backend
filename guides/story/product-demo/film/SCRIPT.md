## Voice direction

Warm, grounded and brisk without sounding like an advertisement read. Pause
after “Then the plan becomes a meeting.” Stress **private, editable priority
list**, **mutual priorities**, **meeting**, and **private**. Do not stretch the
last sentence into a triumph; it is a product design claim and invitation to
test.
