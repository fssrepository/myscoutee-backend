## Voice direction

Warm, grounded and brisk without sounding like an advertisement read. Pause
after “Then you meet.” Stress **meeting**, **accepts or declines**, and
**private**. Do not stretch the last sentence into a triumph; it is a product
design claim and invitation to test.
