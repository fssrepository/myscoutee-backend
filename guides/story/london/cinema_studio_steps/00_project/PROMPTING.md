# Prompting evidence and working method

Reviewed 2026-09-09. These are source-backed writing practices and our untested application, not a guarantee of generation quality. Keep the creator's continuous, gaze-led transformation and existing audio/identity requirements.

## Sources and limits

- [Higgsfield's published prompt library](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/seedance-prompting-guide) supplies exact tutorial prompts. Its transformation examples use multiple cuts; its POV and train-fight examples explicitly request one uninterrupted shot with linked physical actions. It is labeled Seedance 2.0: use its writing patterns as examples, not proof of our 2.5 result. Do not copy its montage, aggressive camera shake, particles or hidden transitions into this film.
- [Dreamina's Seedance 2.5 guide](https://dreamina.capcut.com/seedance/seedance-2-5-prompt) emphasizes observable action, explicit reference roles, consistent camera direction, compact constraints and action-linked sound. It identifies competing camera instructions and excessive action density as failure risks. Its interface/finishing claims do not establish equivalent Higgsfield controls or pricing.
- [A creator's Higgsfield/2.5 prompt reuse](https://www.reddit.com/r/HiggsfieldAI/comments/1vi2zce/i_generated_this_video_using_seedance_25_and/) publishes the gladiator prompt from the Higgsfield library. This is a creator report, not an independently reproduced success rate.
- [A creator's 30-second continuous sequence](https://www.reddit.com/r/Seedance_AI/comments/1vg13x1/seedance_25_handled_this_30s_singletake/) includes the complete timed prompt and follows one person through connected spaces. It was reported on Atlas Cloud, not Higgsfield. The author acknowledges detail drift. We reviewed the written prompt, not the original downloaded video.
- [ONEIRIC's public project](https://higgsfield.ai/@higgsfield.studio/projects/oneiric) exposed only a page shell through this research tool; its individual production prompts were not inspected. Do not attribute invented instructions to that film. The linked ByteDance Lark manual was also inaccessible.

## Application to this pilot

The rejected output and current prompt do not prove that the word `reconfigure` caused the failure. The grid appearing to guide successive views is an inference from our sampled frames.

The revised pilot separates four reference roles, selects the top-left world cell for the opening street axis, and treats remaining cells only as material information. Text-only cell selection remains unproven; it is not equivalent to supplying a separate full-frame image. No new image, Element, duration or paid job is introduced.

The revised pilot begins with one forward tracking path at the café frontage, keeping the man small enough for the street to remain visible. Architecture changes at 1–3s; the complete life-size restaurant emerges directly at 3–5.5s and withdraws at 5.5–6.5s. His gaze then leads one smooth turn across the road, landing on a broad taxi/tram/architecture view through 8s. The wide ending reveals connected near, middle and distant city volumes changing together, with substantial transport and a recognisable landmark axis. The restaurant still supplies reconstruction information rather than an image to paste on glass. The same four image references remain; the revised camera path and ending have not been generated.

Precision means stating the subject, starting shape, observable movement, final shape, spatial anchor, human gesture and corresponding sound. Keep those details even when they make the prompt longer. Avoid contradictory blanket rules: photographic material does not require ordinary city behaviour, and a memory need not remain confined to a reflective surface. Use PROJECTION, SPATIAL EMERGENCE and MATERIAL TRANSFORMATION as distinct beat-specific mechanisms. Source sheets remain direct, reusable views; film prompts determine how their content enters the impossible world.

Keep current pedestrians' heads and full cards unobstructed, with no open umbrellas. A card has a persistent frosted body and visible fill bar; it is not a faint lens artefact. Its visibility must survive the actual composition, not only a sentence requesting it. Test the named scene and gesture, rather than accepting generic camera travel as an equivalent.

This is a revised production candidate, not a controlled experiment isolating one word. The eight-second action density and four-reference competition remain risks. If another output skips a central mechanism, review it before spending again; do not automatically extend it or increase resolution as a supposed motion fix.
