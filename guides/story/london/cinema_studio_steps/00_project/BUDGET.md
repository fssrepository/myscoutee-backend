## Keep or stop

Keep the first usable image or clip. Strange but readable architecture, small background flaws and unusual coherent reflections fit this film. Stronger effects are not a reason to generate multiple candidates.

For missing story content, materially wrong protagonist identity, unintelligible narration, an early clinical reveal or unusable output, identify the exact failure and smallest repair before spending again. Repairs count against the same ceiling. Another creator's reported 100,000-credit film is an anecdote, not this project's cost basis.
