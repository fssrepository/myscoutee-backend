# Visual direction — an entrancing, overwhelming, transforming city

The September 9 direction is **fast camera movement, a living city, stronger effects and physically transforming buildings; the walker is almost lost inside the experience**. This direction is already included in the world, storyboard and video prompts. Do not paste this guide alongside them.

The man walks at human speed. The camera overtakes him, swings ahead, accelerates sideways, catches his gaze on glass and travels around him in short purposeful arcs. Umbrellas, buses and close columns provide foreground wipes. Strong parallax gives depth; a face or gesture briefly becomes stable at the end of each surge, giving the overload recognizable human content.

Architecture actually changes: window bays slide, facades lengthen, arcades repeat and roof structures articulate into new forms. Stone, metal, glass, shadows and wet reflections follow the transformation. Bodies and faces retain their shapes. The pedestrian route stays connected; bystanders do not experience the change as a disaster.

| Block | Camera and architectural transformation | Human anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Low forward rush and lateral move; Piccadilly brick becomes Pest stone and balconies | The Soho smile; the same protagonist |
| 02 | Transit wipes and lateral acceleration; Charing Cross ribs become Nyugati | Helping hands and the shared taxi |
| 03 | Fast approach to glass; multiplying windows and Lechner roof detail | Three people with three separate records |
| 04 | One purposeful half-orbit; Temple arcades seem endless | Ordinary people inside procedure |
| 05 | Riverfront acceleration; Gresham ornament and Chain Bridge detail | The person behind the file; black humour |
| 06 | Maximum density; Great Market Hall ribs unfold into Bálna glass | A settled glimpse of six people around a table |
| 07 | Movement and architectural change drain away | Hard cut to an ordinary nurse and still reality |

One dominant transformation reads at a time. Continuous spinning, deformation on every plane and uninterrupted flashing would obscure the story. Memory participants still act; the film must not become camera movement over static reference images.

Cards remain normally visible above heads and gain only a little internal fill during direct gaze. The protagonist's own card becomes readable in Block 06. No cards, architectural deformation or spectacular camera movement survive the clinical cut: stillness is the final contrast.

## Personal memory and sound

The existing rural memory fragments belong to childhood near Orosháza in the Viharsarok: paprika rows, corn rows, a flat Great Plain horizon and wind through the leaves. Keep these inside the remembered world. The original Dél-Alföld-inspired tekerő and clarinet motif carries this sense of home through the fused city, from intimate longing to controlled overload; the facade starts and settles on the motif's main accents, with synchronized stone and metal/glass foley giving the change material weight.
