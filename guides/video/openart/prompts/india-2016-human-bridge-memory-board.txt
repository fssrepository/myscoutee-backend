PURPOSE
Create a planning storyboard board, not a final video, for the Founder's
five-day India journey in November 2016: about three days on the ground and two
travel days via Dubai. The visible route is the Golden Triangle — Delhi, Agra
and Jaipur — with every city kept distinct. The story is about beauty, cash
friction, dependence on brief human help and one respectful missed connection.

PERIOD FACT LOCK
On 8 November 2016 the Indian government announced that existing ₹500 and ₹1000
banknotes would cease to be legal tender from 9 November. Banks and ATMs were
temporarily closed or reconfigured. Show one dated contextual discontinuity,
not timeless Indian dysfunction. No readable banknote reproduction, invented
exchange rate, riot, starving crowd or claim that all cash disappeared.

STYLE AND IDENTITY
Use the approved Founder identity in the premium painterly cinematic 2.5D
style. Indian residents, the driver, travelers, the young woman and her
grandfather are distinct autonomous adults, not cultural shorthand. 9:16,
mobile-safe, no flags, national-costume montage, logos or generated text.

PANEL 1 — FIVE DAYS COMPRESSED, CITIES KEPT TRUE
Use a clean notebook route: Budapest/flight via Dubai → Delhi → Agra → Jaipur →
return. The route is a planning memory, not a claim about exact road order until
the itinerary is confirmed from records. Separate Delhi urban geometry, Agra's
Taj Mahal setting and Jaipur's architecture; never merge them into one fantasy
city.

PANEL 2 — CASH WITHOUT SPECTACLE
One closed or reconfiguring ATM, a patient queue and the Founder holding an
unreadable budget envelope. His memory includes people hired to hold places in
ATM queues; present this only as a small observed detail, not a universal claim.
No panic, mob, humiliation or comic poverty.

PANEL 3 — WHATSAPP DRIVER AS A HUMAN HANDOFF
Through an abstract unreadable chat, the Founder coordinates a local driver.
Because his exchange access is constrained, he asks the driver to cover some
entrance tickets and tracks the debt carefully in his cognac notebook. Show
trust under a finite practical constraint, not begging, servitude or a driver
used as a wallet. Do not display the WhatsApp brand.

PANEL 4 — TAJ MAHAL BUS, THE CONNECTION
On a bus near the Taj Mahal visit, the Founder hears a pleasant voice behind
him, turns and briefly loses his words. A young woman visiting with her
grandfather meets his gaze; they talk naturally and reciprocally while the
grandfather remains a real companion, not a chaperone caricature. The Taj Mahal
may sit softly through a window or reflection, but faces and listening carry
the frame. No fantasy romance, touching, wedding symbolism or claim of love.

PANEL 5 — REAL ENCOUNTER, NO REALISTIC NEXT STEP
The bus aisle becomes a long, quiet route line. The Founder recognizes that the
distance makes continuation unrealistic and leaves the encounter respectfully
where it is. The point is not regret over “winning” a woman. It is that chemistry
can exist while shared context, consented continuation and capacity do not.
MyScoutee must not be shown as erasing geography or guaranteeing romance.

OUTPUT
One clean five-panel planning board with no generated text. The editor adds the
November 2016 date, city labels and any confirmed itinerary in post. Generate no
motion and invent no additional event.
