GLOBAL EMOTIONAL PURPOSE:
This is a film between two worlds: a nationally homogeneous house inside a
heterogeneous global city; private reasons hidden behind crowded doors; magical
possibility beside stalled personal time. Do not flatten either world into good
or bad. The later MyScoutee product turn grows from the need to let distinct
inner lives become visible, meet with consent in real context and create a
shared path across cultures.
