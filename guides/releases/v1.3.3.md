# MyScoutee 1.3.3 — granular ratings and fractional affinity

Release date: 2026-10-01. Task: [MSC-113](https://github.com/fssrepository/myscoutee-roadmap/issues/151).
Git tag: `v1.3.3` in the frontend and backend repositories.

Home and Activities share a detailed-rating popup above the existing bar. Its
number/chevron button precedes Go/Save at the same height. The popup follows the
base width, retains the underlying controls without a backdrop, and uses the
existing mobile menu rule for floating launchers. EN/HU labels and guide steps
are included.

Criteria are integers; the main slider step derives from their count. Their
fractional mean passes unchanged through outbox sync, Mongo, read models and
scalar affinity on the original 1–10 scale. Actor-owned criterion snapshots
are supplementary audit data. Dating is configured; residential profiles are
not implemented. Fractional badges display at most two decimals.
