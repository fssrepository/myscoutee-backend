## Current copy

- `6 people · 6 priority lists → 1 team`
- `Standard · Tournament · Speed meeting`
- `Interest 1–10`, with the selected value `8`
- `Mutual interest. Group chat. Real plans.`
- `Meet through events.`
- `FREE TO JOIN`

The feed and square images also carry:

> OPEN IN EXTERNAL BROWSER
> Facebook / Instagram may block location access.

Their lower-right diagonal ribbon reads `myscoutee.com` in lowercase white text.
The upper brand is MyScoutee, without `.com`; its official colourful M emblem
replaces the initial letter and is followed by `yScoutee`.

## Design brief

Keep the established illustrated style: six distinct adults, six priority cards,
an interest scale and one group-chat motif leading into the same six people at
an outdoor café table. Use the existing navy type, pale lavender background,
blue/purple/pink accents and original heart-and-star emblem. Preserve the
recognisable people and all current copy when adapting a format.

Keep the complete wordmark, captions and badge visible. In the feed and square
variants, the compact navy/violet corner ribbon must leave faces and the full
`Group chat. Real plans.` caption unobstructed.
