## Design brief

Retain the panoramic illustrated landscape: blue-violet sky, peach/lavender
clouds, mountains, lake, café, flowers and groups of adult friends. Keep the
branding compact and centred within the existing crop-safe composition.

The accepted layout has one closely fitted, rounded, translucent dark-indigo
panel behind the central branding block. Preserve its position, the complete
heart-and-star emblem, white lettering and purple free-to-join badge. The
source artwork and upload export belong to the same banner package.
