# Books and audiobooks

- [The City Between Us — Part I](trilogy/01-the-city-between-us/README.md)
- [The System Between Us — Part II](trilogy/02-the-system-between-us/README.md)
- [A Match Is Not a Meeting — product companion](a-match-is-not-a-meeting/README.md)

Each book keeps its manuscript, source illustrations and PDF/EPUB editions in
one directory; its audiobook files stay in `audiobook/`. [Audiobook production](audiobook-production.md)
and `build-audiobooks.py` are the common narration workflow. [Product research](product-and-sources.md)
owns the underlying product claims and sources.

The [Google Flow films](../films/google-flow/README.md) adapt the books. The
[Higgsfield film](../films/higgsfield/the-path-between-people/README.md) owns its
separate production and accepted narration.
