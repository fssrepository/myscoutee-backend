# Mini-book illustration assets

Two registered, print-oriented `3:2` illustrations support both language
editions:

- `missing-middle.jpg`: the familiar match-to-meeting gap becomes a constructive
  path, used after chapter 4;
- `after-door-opens.jpg`: the same adults leave a real shared activity with a
  warmer next invitation, used after chapter 8 and as the cover image.

`../../illustrations.json` and `../../illustrations.hu.json` are the filename,
placement, alt-text and caption contract. The English registry is canonical;
the Hungarian registry translates its meaning without adding claims.

Both images were generated from approved product-reel checkpoints. They contain
no generated MyScoutee mark or readable UI. The cover composites the untouched
repository `heart.png` and editable `yScoutee` separately in HTML/CSS.
