# Part I book illustrations

This directory contains the approved book derivatives listed in
[`../../illustrations.json`](../../illustrations.json). The editable illustrated
draft now embeds these plates together with approved locked character crops;
checkpoint generation remains a separate later gate.

Canonical identity rules live in
[`../../../assets/characters/CHARACTER_MAP.md`](../../../../../films/google-flow/the-city-between-us/assets/characters/CHARACTER_MAP.md).
Copy-paste generation baselines live in
[`../../../prompts/book-character-illustrations.txt`](../../../../../films/google-flow/the-city-between-us/prompts/book-character-illustrations.txt).

- Keep original approved character references in `assets/characters/`.
- Put print-ready crops and montage derivatives here.
- A book registry may embed an approved locked crop directly from
  `../assets/characters/` when no additional crop or colour treatment is
  required; this avoids stale duplicate portraits drifting from the identity
  contract.
- Portraits are tight 4:5 crops; do not add brown or other coloured side bars to
  make a narrow subject appear wider.
- Montages are landscape plates with all required faces readable at A4 size.
- The portrait montage and its individual crops must contain the same identity,
  not regenerated substitutes.
- Missing files are intentionally omitted by the book builder; do not substitute
  the rough storyboard placeholders.
