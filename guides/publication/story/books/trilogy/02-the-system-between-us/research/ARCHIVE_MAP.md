## Duplicate rule

One PDF appearing in five export paths remains one source. A signed PDF, an ODT
draft, a rendered copy, a KRX attachment and an email export can be related but
are not automatically five independent confirmations.

Before a statement is promoted from memoir to documentary layer:

1. identify the earliest available source;
2. determine whether later copies are byte-identical, rendered variants or
   materially revised versions;
3. prefer the issued or filed copy for procedural facts;
4. compare authored recollection with the independent response;
5. record conflicts instead of silently choosing the cleaner narrative.

third-party correspondence and unresolved allegations. It must not be committed,

reference. Public outputs use anonymized roles, abstract documents and bounded
claims only.
