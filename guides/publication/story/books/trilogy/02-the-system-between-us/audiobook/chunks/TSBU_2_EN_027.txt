How to use these notes.

The memoir has already established its point of view. These notes keep the legal and documentary background available without interrupting the story.

Private people remain anonymous. The two forensic experts are separate people. No final confiscation, financial penalty or criminal judgment is asserted. Political change supplies public context; it does not decide the pending case or clinically resolve a contested expert opinion.

The legal links below are orientation points, not a substitute for advice. A lawyer should match every proposed argument to the law in force on the relevant date, the exact charge, the filed submissions and the complete procedural record.

The charging document is a prosecution position, not a neutral biography or a judgment. In the Founder's reading, it organizes the sequence so that the institutional failures become background while legal causation and responsibility narrow around the individual. That is a defence interpretation of the document's architecture, not a finding that the prosecution concealed a proved institutional cause or that the court accepted the interpretation.

The evidence-reconstruction passage in Chapter 14 is grounded in the defence speech and the private investigation evidence bundle listed in the internal source inventory. The defence speech lists missing electronic submissions, tax-authority call recordings and the call history recovered from the returned phone as material the Founder gathered after finding the investigative selection distorted. Letters reproduced in the bundle record requests made through the tax authority's Central Directorate, the staged release of recordings, delayed delivery and the later supply of electronic submissions after an acknowledged administrative error. The defence speech records that the Founder forwarded the recovered material and that police later requested material again. The narrative therefore describes the Founder performing the first reconstruction before the authority repeated the request through its own channel. This supports the sequence of the search; it does not establish that an official deliberately deleted evidence, deliberately waited for the Founder or that every missing call had been recorded.

The same private source inventory contains the tax authority's official complaint-handling notice dated 18 December, which records that the Founder had settled the matter, and the official forensic opinion, which places the relevant pathological period from 3 December onward. The narrative treats those dates and the positions recorded in the two official documents as documentary facts.

The fundamental-rights passage in Chapter 19 refers to Hungary's Commissioner for Fundamental Rights, not the European Ombudsman. Letters in the same private bundle describe jurisdictional limits over police evidence assessment, prosecutorial activity and pending court proceedings, and state that further repetitive submissions could be archived without another reply. The later European Parliament petition and Commission response form a separate route. The memoir may let their proximity carry institutional irony, but it does not merge the offices or claim that either decided the criminal case.

The preparatory-hearing notice dated 6 May 2025 explains that the accused may admit guilt and waive trial in the admitted part, or present defence facts, evidence and exclusion requests; it also allows agreed facts to be accepted without further proof. Section 316 of the Criminal Code sets a range of two to eight years for the charged offence. The Founder records that the prosecution proposed the statutory minimum of two years, suspended, in exchange for admission and waiver. The offer and the Founder's understanding of the room are memoir layers until the complete preparatory-hearing record is isolated.
