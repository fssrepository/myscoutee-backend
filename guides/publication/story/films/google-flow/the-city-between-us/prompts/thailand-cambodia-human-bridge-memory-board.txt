PURPOSE
Create a planning storyboard board, not a final video, for the Founder's roughly
two-and-a-half-to-three-week Thailand journey across northern, western,
eastern/Gulf and southern regions, followed within that wider trip by an
extremely compressed Cambodia/Angkor excursion. Preserve regional contrasts
without claiming that he literally saw the whole country. The human theme is
the difference between commercial transport, an ongoing driver relationship
and a stranger who helps because they notice a need.

CONFIRMATION LOCK
The full Thai itinerary, exact northern/western stops, Phi Phi pier, named
Cambodian entry point, arrival transport and calendar date remain CONFIRM. The
Cambodian sequence itself is Founder-confirmed: arrival around midnight, visa at
the entry point after a small payment request, continuation into Siem Reap,
tuk-tuk negotiation, Angkor visit and return to the airport around 16:00–17:00.
Phuket is PROBABLE:
it is the large southern tourist island the Founder remembers above Phi Phi,
and official Thai tourism material joins Phuket and Ko Phi Phi in one route,
but Krabi is also an access side. Every attraction below is a recognition
candidate, not a claim of a visit, until the Founder confirms it.

STYLE AND IDENTITY
Use the approved Founder identity in premium painterly cinematic 2.5D. Thai and
Cambodian residents, drivers and travelers are distinct people with agency.
9:16, mobile-safe. Give each region its own air, density, terrain, transport and
sound-implied rhythm while preserving one grade. No flags, costume shorthand,
poverty spectacle, readable money, logos or generated text.

PANEL 1 — TWO AND A HALF TO THREE WEEKS, SEVERAL ATMOSPHERES
Open the cognac notebook into a route with separate central, northern, western,
eastern/Gulf and southern clusters. Use blank route nodes until exact places are
confirmed. Bangkok is one hub, not the whole country. The page should feel
spacious enough for weeks rather than an overnight stop.

PANEL 2 — BANGKOK/CENTRAL RECOGNITION STRIP
Atmosphere: dense, hot, layered, river-and-rail movement; ceremonial compounds,
street commerce and modern vertical city coexist. Candidate anchors to show as
separate thumbnails only: Grand Palace/Wat Phra Kaew, Wat Pho, Wat Arun,
Chatuchak and the Chao Phraya. Delete every unrecognized candidate before final
generation. No impossible all-monuments skyline.

PANEL 3 — NORTH RECOGNITION STRIP
Atmosphere: cooler mountain edges, Lanna urban texture, slower courtyards and
contemporary creative streets. Chiang Mai candidates: Doi Suthep, Doi Inthanon,
Tha Phae Gate and walking-street rhythm. Separate Chiang Rai candidates: Wat
Rong Khun, misty mountains and the Mekong-side northern Golden Triangle. Never
confuse it with the Delhi–Agra–Jaipur Golden Triangle.

PANEL 4 — WEST RECOGNITION STRIP
Atmosphere: river, forest, waterfalls, reservoirs and the weight of historical
infrastructure. Candidate anchors: Kanchanaburi, Bridge over the River Khwae,
Erawan Falls, Sai Yok and Three Pagodas Pass. Keep historical memory restrained;
no war spectacle or scenic-adventure trivialization.

PANEL 5 — PATTAYA, A NEUTRAL PERSONAL IMPRESSION
Atmosphere: concentrated Gulf beach resort, entertainment traffic, signs,
tourism services and nearby quieter water. The Founder remembers Pattaya as
emotionally neutral and less enjoyable than the other regions. Express that
through his observant but unengaged body language, not a grey or hostile city.
Candidate anchors: Pattaya Beach, Jomtien, Ko Lan and Sanctuary of Truth. Do not
judge residents, nightlife workers or visitors.

PANEL 6 — PROBABLE PHUKET AND PHI PHI
Atmosphere: open Andaman light, deep blue-green water, limestone, piers and
constant boat transition. Show Phuket as a PROBABLE large southern island base
and Ko Phi Phi as a separate destination. Keep the departure pier blank because
Phuket and Krabi access are both plausible. Candidate anchors: Phuket Old Town,
Patong/Kata/Karon only if recognized, Phi Phi Don, Phi Phi Le and Maya Bay only
if personally visited. No empty-paradise fantasy or tourism-poster saturation.

PANEL 7 — TRANSACTIONAL RIDE LANGUAGE
Across Thailand, ordinary drivers ask whether the Founder wants a ride and make
the price explicit. Keep it neutral: transport is work and payment is not
unfriendliness. Repeated handlebar, tuk-tuk and door gestures become a practical
rhythm across regions.

PANEL 8 — THE UNASKED-FOR RIDE
On the way to the CONFIRM Phi Phi departure pier, the Founder walks in an
unfamiliar area where many dogs are present. One local person reads the
situation, offers a ride to the pier and asks for nothing. Show a cautious
question, the Founder's consent and simple arrival. Do not stage an attack,
label the district objectively dangerous or turn the helper into a savior.

PANEL 9 — CAMBODIAN ENTRY AND SIEM REAP NIGHT
Inside the wider Thailand trip, the Founder reaches an unnamed Cambodian entry
point, completes a visa-on-arrival process and remembers a small extra payment request.
Around midnight he reaches Siem Reap and its compact restaurant, market and Pub
Street atmosphere. Keep forms and currency unreadable. Do not call the request
a bribe without evidence or portray the whole city as nightlife.

PANEL 10 — TUK-TUK DRIVER, ANGKOR, AIRPORT CLOCK
The Founder agrees a route and price with one tuk-tuk driver who provides the
local movement for the visit. Driver and traveler coordinate stops and time
rather than resetting their relationship in every frame. They reach Angkor Wat
in humid morning light; the same tuk-tuk wheel becomes an airport clock near
16:00–17:00. This is an exhausting personal chain, not a recommended one-day
itinerary. Keep Angkor architecture truthful and separate from Siem Reap night.

EMOTIONAL PURPOSE
The trip proves that one country can contain radically different atmospheres,
and that moving through them depends on a series of people who quote, guide,
notice, negotiate or help. Even a recurring driver relationship usually
disappears after the handoff. MyScoutee may preserve consented context and make a
later invitation possible; it does not replace transport, visas, local advice,
travel insurance or judgment.

OUTPUT
One clean ten-panel planning board with no generated text. The editor adds only
confirmed places and route labels in post. Generate no motion. Preserve the full
board for the personal film; the MyScoutee hero film may take only one restrained
Angkor driver handoff or one Thai kindness image, never the whole itinerary.
