checkpoint endpoints and later Veo 3.1 Lite motion in **The City Between Us**.
The Founder resumed generation on 8 August 2026. The identities listed here are
now the locked visual baseline; the old CP images remain layout studies until
they are regenerated from these assets. Part II inherits the exact
`[FOUNDER_REF]`; it does not create a replacement Founder.

## Journey encounters

The global/local montage uses scene-specific local drivers, passengers, hosts,
vendors, residents and organizers from the approved travel-memory boards. They
are not one recurring "helpful stranger" identity and must not be cloned across
countries. The Founder is the only identity deliberately repeated between those
panels. Cultural place is carried through truthful environment and ordinary
human action, never flags or costume shorthand.
