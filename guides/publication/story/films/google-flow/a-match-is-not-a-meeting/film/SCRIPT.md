## Voice direction

Warm, grounded and brisk without sounding like an advertisement read. Pause
after “Then the plan becomes a real meeting.” Stress **private, editable priority
list**, **a signal**, **mutual interest**, **meeting**, and **private**. Do not
stretch the last sentence into a triumph; it is a product design claim and
invitation to test.
