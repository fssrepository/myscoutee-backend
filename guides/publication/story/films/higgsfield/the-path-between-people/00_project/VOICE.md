# Accepted narration

The final film uses the complete **Neutral v4** recording, `narration_v4.mp3`,
with the existing Grady voice. [Spoken text](../03_film/narration_directors_cut_v4.txt)
and [paragraph placements](../04_tracking/DIRECTORS_CUT_NARRATION_PLACEMENT_V4.json)
own the wording and timing. Complete paragraphs remain in order; only their
pauses were extended. No internal speech cuts, reordering, speed or pitch edits.

The clean aligned MP3 and listening movie are recorded in the
[final production delivery](../03_film/production/README.md). Recording and editing
are closed; earlier model auditions and rejected narration versions stay in Git.
