MyScoutee: More Ways to Connect

A great connection can become a first date, a friendship, a working partnership, or a group of neighbors making something happen.

MyScoutee brings those possibilities together through three connected spaces: Dating, Work, and Community.

❤️ DATING — MAKE ROOM FOR A REAL CONNECTION

Meet people, discover mutual interest, and get to know each other through conversations and shared activities. Group experiences create opportunities to connect in a more relaxed setting, with a common plan to bring people together.

💼 WORK — TURN AN INTRODUCTION INTO COLLABORATION

Bring people together around work and business opportunities. Create campaigns, organize groups, and coordinate related events. Give a promising introduction somewhere to go: a conversation, a meeting, or a project people can work on together.

🌍 COMMUNITY — MAKE EVERYDAY COOPERATION EASIER

Organize a local activity, raise a shared issue, find a service provider, or coordinate the people helping resolve it. Community spaces bring cases, service offers, quotations, appointments, and tasks into a shared workflow. Announcements and voting help groups stay informed and take part in decisions.

WHAT CONNECTS ALL THREE?

People need a way to move from interest to action.

MyScoutee connects discovery with groups, chat, events, and practical coordination. In its social matching flow, people can express their priorities on a 1–10 scale, giving more room to show the strength of their interest. Those choices support group planning, while people remain the ones deciding whom they want to meet.

The goal is a real-world meeting that everyone involved wants to be part of.

That might mean coffee with someone new, a shared activity that becomes a friendship, a meeting with a future collaborator, or neighbors working together on a local problem.

Dating is one part of MyScoutee. Work, friendship, and community belong in the picture too.
