# MyScoutee

**Designed for the meeting, not the swipe.**

MyScoutee is a group- and event-centred social platform for lower-pressure,
activity-led social and dating experiences. Its purpose is to turn mutual
interest and shared priorities into real-world connections instead of treating
a match or a message as the final outcome.

## From priorities to shared experiences

MyScoutee replaces binary swipes with editable 1–10 priorities. Reciprocal
preferences, profiles, distance, and context contribute to a weighted affinity
graph that can bring compatible people together in groups.

The experience follows the connection all the way into the real world:

1. People express who and what matters to them.
2. Mutual affinity helps identify compatible group candidates.
3. Groups connect through chat and plan an activity or event.
4. Event tools support invitations, resources, attendance, and coordination.
5. Structured feedback captures what happened after people met.

The product's north star is a **completed, mutually welcomed real-world
meeting**. Matching, group planning, chat, event hosting, resources, feedback,
moderation, and administration work together in support of that goal.

## Built for local communities

MyScoutee can support independently operated communities and locally branded
deployments. Each operator can build its own community, organize experiences,
work with local partners, and retain control of its customer relationships and
commercial decisions while using the shared MyScoutee product foundation.
